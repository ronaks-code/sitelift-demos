Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need to be home during the detail?

Not necessarily. As long as they can access the vehicle and confirm power and water are available, they can detail while you are at work or running errands.

How long does a detail take?

Most packages run two to five hours. Showroom Restoration jobs typically take a full day.

Are the products safe for coated and wrapped cars?

Yes. Every soap, polish and sealant is pH-neutral and coating-safe, and the process is adjusted for wrapped, PPF-protected and matte finishes.

Do you service fleets?

Yes. Ask about volume pricing and recurring service for company vehicles, dealerships and rideshare fleets.
