About

Aden and the mobile rig

Mobile detailing and ceramic coating in Bloomington, Indiana. Since 2023, professional techniques and premium products, brought to a location that works for you.

Aden is personable, professional and proud of his work. Cars leave looking new and smelling clean, not like chemicals.

Every job follows the same process: deep vacuuming, wiped and sanitized surfaces, a pre-wash soak, foam cannon and microfiber mitts, and wheels cleaned with a brake dust compound.

  • Premium productspH-balanced soaps, microfiber mitts, no harsh degreasers.
