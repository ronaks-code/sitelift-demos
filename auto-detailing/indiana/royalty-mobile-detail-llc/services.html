Good to know

Questions people ask

Can you remove road paint?

Yes. Yellow and white road paint that other detailers called impossible has come off. Call and tell us what you have.

What else do you detail?

Cars and trucks inside and out, plus motorcycles, boats and lawn mowers.

Do you give care tips?

Yes. Sean and Cat walk you through the vehicle, give instructions for keeping it clean, and Cat follows up with a call.

Can you handle a really dirty car?

Yes. Cat hair, mulch, dead leaves, old paint splatters and spilled coffee all come out.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 11 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

How do I schedule?

Call Sean or send a request from the contact page with your vehicle and what it needs.
