Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you come to me?

Yes. Services are available in the shop or through the mobile team, so ask when you call.

Where do you work?

The shop is in Round Rock and the team serves North Austin, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Cedar Park and Leander.

What is the difference between paint protection film and ceramic coating?

Film is a clear layer that shields paint from chips and scratches. Ceramic coating adds gloss, UV resistance and durability. Ask which suits your car.

How do I get a quote?

Send a request on the contact page with your vehicle and the job, or call the shop.

Are you insured?

Yes, Miko's says it is fully insured.
