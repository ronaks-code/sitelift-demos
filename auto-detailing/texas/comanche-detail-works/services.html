Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer mobile detailing?

Yes. Interior, Exterior and Complete Details are mobile across our service area. Drop-off is better for large jobs and heavy stains. We need power and water.

Can I drop my vehicle off?

Yes. Drop off in Pflugerville so the work is never rushed.

How long does a detail take?

Most appointments take between 2 and 6 hours, depending on the package and the condition of the vehicle.

What about stains, pet hair and odors?

Quoted separately by severity. Add-ons start at $50 for pet hair or engine bay, $100 for odors, $120 for headlights.

Who can get a Maintenance Detail?

Returning customers after an initial Complete Detail. It excludes stains, heavy pet hair, odors and restoration work.

Do you detail trucks and SUVs?

Yes. We service cars, trucks, SUVs and commercial fleet vehicles.

Ready for a reset?
