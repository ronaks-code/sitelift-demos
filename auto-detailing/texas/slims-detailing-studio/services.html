Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 9am to 6pm, Friday 9am to 5pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm. Closed Sunday.

What do you do?

Paint protection film, ceramic coatings, window tint, vinyl wraps, paint correction, full detailing and chrome deletes.

Where do you work?

The studio is in Round Rock and serves Austin, Leander, Georgetown and Central Texas.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the vehicle and the work, so we do not post prices. Send the year, make and model for a quote.

How long does a job take?

Often a couple of days, depending on the work. Ask about your vehicle.
