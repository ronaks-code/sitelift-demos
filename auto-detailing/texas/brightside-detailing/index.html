Everything we install

How a ceramic coating goes

  1. 01

    Look it over

    A paint inspection and a talk about how you drive and park, then the 5, 7 or 25-year package that fits.

  2. 02

    Prep and coat

    Hand wash, clay bar and light paint correction first. The coating goes on last, panel by panel.

  3. 03

    Cure and go

    An aftercare walk-through: keep it dry the first day and skip soap washes for about a week.
