How a ceramic coating goes

  1. 01

    Look it over

    A paint inspection and a talk about how you drive and park, then the 5, 7 or 25-year package that fits.

  2. 02

    Prep and coat

    Hand wash, clay bar and light paint correction first. The coating goes on last, panel by panel.

  3. 03

    Cure and go

    An aftercare walk-through: keep it dry the first day and skip soap washes for about a week.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need to bring the car in?

No. Brightside detailers come to your home, office or any parking lot in the Austin area.

What does a ceramic coating visit involve?

Consultation and paint check, hand wash, clay bar, light paint correction, panel wipe, then the coating and aftercare walk-through. Plan on 1–2 days.

How long does an interior detail take?

About 2.5 hours for a car or small SUV and about 3 hours for an XL truck or 3-row SUV, per the shop.

Can you do same-day?

Same-day appointments are often available. Call or text to check.

What days are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 7pm. Closed Sunday.
