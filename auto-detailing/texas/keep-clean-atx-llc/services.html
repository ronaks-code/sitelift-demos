Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you come to me?

Yes. Keep Clean ATX is mobile and brings its own water and electricity, so no hookups are needed at your home.

What hours are you open?

Every day, 7 AM to 7 PM. Hours can change on holidays, so call before you book.

Where do you work?

Across the Austin metro, including Pflugerville, Round Rock, Manor, Hutto and Georgetown. Call to check your address.

Do you detail every kind of car?

Yes. All makes and models, from Hondas and Toyotas to Teslas and Porsches.

What if I am not happy with the result?

Love your detail or we come back and make it right.

What does it cost?

Exterior from $70, refresh $120, interior $180, odor removal $280, full detailing $330, paint correction $400, ceramic coating $600.

Do you work on fleets?

Yes. Fleet detailing is quoted, so send a message with how many vehicles.
