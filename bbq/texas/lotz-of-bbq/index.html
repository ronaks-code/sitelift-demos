Behind the table

Roy Lotz and his backyard

Roy Lotz grew up in Austin grilling steaks over a charcoal Weber in his father's backyard. After Gallaudet University, he came home and started cooking barbecue.

Brisket, smash burgers, tallow wings and breakfast tacos, plus catering for backyard parties and corporate gatherings. Roy and his staff are Deaf.

Before you book

Good to know

Are you open on weekends?

Google lists us open Monday to Friday, 5 to 9 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Call to confirm before you drive over.

Do you cater?

Yes. Backyard parties to corporate gatherings. Send the event request or call and tell us the date and guest count.

Can I book a holiday party?

Yes. Holiday parties are open for booking. Send a request or call.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Order online and skip the wait.

How do I order in person?

Our owner and staff are Deaf and serve in American Sign Language. Ordering ahead online is the easiest way.

What if the menu says something is out?

Ask at the counter to double check. It may still be available.
