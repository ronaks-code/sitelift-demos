Our story

Roy Lotz and his backyard

Roy Lotz grew up in Austin grilling steaks over a charcoal Weber in his father's backyard. After Gallaudet University, he came home and started cooking barbecue.

Brisket, smash burgers, tallow wings and breakfast tacos, plus catering for backyard parties and corporate gatherings. Roy and his staff are Deaf.
