Our story

Ali Clem and the pit

Chef and owner Ali Clem grew up in Fort Worth. A food trailer on South First Street became this East Cesar Chavez spot. Female-owned.

Brisket cooks low and slow over Texas post oak, with beef from a local ranch. Shade umbrellas out front, murals all over.
