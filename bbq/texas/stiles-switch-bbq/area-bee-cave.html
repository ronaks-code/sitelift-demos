Five stars across the board for Stiles Switch. I got there about a half hour after they opened and walked right up to order. By the time I had my tray and started eating, the line was already building, so the timing was perfect. The St. Louis ribs were the star of the show for me. Really excellent and easily better than the brisket I had. I ended up with 3 slices from the flat, so it was a little dry, but next time I’d ask for the fatty/moist brisket. The barbecue sauce was incredible.
