Said at the counter

The food was delicious! We got there at about 5p on a Wednesday and there was no line but several tables of diners. Shortly after we got there it got a little busier. Staff at the counter were so nice and helped us make some recommendations. Awesome to see everything cut and plated for us fresh. The woman at the register was a hoot! When she offers you the homemade ranch, take it!

Crystal C. ★★★★★

Five stars across the board for Stiles Switch. I got there about a half hour after they opened and walked right up to order. By the time I had my tray and started eating, the line was already building, so the timing was perfect. The St. Louis ribs were the star of the show for me. Really excellent and easily better than the brisket I had. I ended up with 3 slices from the flat, so it was a little dry, but next time I’d ask for the fatty/moist brisket. The barbecue sauce was incredible.

Kevin W. ★★★★★

Loved loved loved this place! We'd traveled from Houston to San Antonio to Austin but weren't inspired to try TX BBQ until someone suggested Stiles Switch, great recommendation! The 2 of us shared a 3-meat of Brisket, Ribs & Pulled Pork, plus 2-veggie combo of Corn Casserole, Cole Slaw & we added a side of Brussels Sprouts. All 3 meats were perfect, tender & juicy. The Cole slaw had pineapple, which added just a touch of sweetness. The Brussels were cooked tender, a sweet glaze with a nice punch of heat.
