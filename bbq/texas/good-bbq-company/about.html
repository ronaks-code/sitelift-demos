About us

Texas and Alabama barbecue, veteran owned.

Kitchen notes

Smoke, family and service

Founded by Army veteran Daniel Monplaisir, a backyard enthusiast turned pitmaster. Texas and Alabama barbecue, slow-smoked, with house-made Aunt Clyde's BBQ Sauce.

A stone and wood front with a red door, a big dining room and plenty of outdoor space. Clean, with character and charm.

  • Tender meatBrisket and ribs, smoked until tender.
  • Two saucesSpicy and sweet, to try with your meal.
  • CateringBirthdays, corporate events, weddings and more.
