Meet the crew

From trailer to Chalmer's

Rollin Smoke started as a food truck. The graffiti trailer with the pink pig is still part of the story. The pit now lives inside Chalmer's Bar.

Pitmaster and owner Kyle Stallings cooks with local post oak and an in-house rub. Sausage is made in house, buns come from New World Bakery.

Outdoor seating, music, big screens and a cowboy mural.

  • TexasSmoked on sitePost oak and a Moberg smoker, all on the premises.
  • TexasSides that hold upMac and cheese, hash brown casserole, spicy slaw.
  • TexasRoom to hangA patio, big screens and a covered area for parties.
