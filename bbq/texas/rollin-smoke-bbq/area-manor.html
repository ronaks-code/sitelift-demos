Rollin Smoke for Manor

Coming from Manor? Rollin Smoke BBQ is on East Cesar Chavez in Austin, inside Chalmer's Bar. Brisket, burnt ends, ribs and house sausage off a post oak pit, open for dinner.

Meats by the pound

Prime brisket, spare ribs with a pepper jelly glaze, house sausage, pork belly burnt ends and brined chicken. Sliced to order.

Sandwiches and tacos

The Playboy, the Silky, brisket and pulled pork tacos, and the G-Thang Burrito crisped in beef tallow.

Party platters

Parties and catered buffets for family nights, company get-togethers and game days.
