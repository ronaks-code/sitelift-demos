Our story

A cathedral of smoke

Louie Mueller opened in 1949. His son Bobby took over the smoker in 1974. Since 2007 it has been Wayne Mueller's, the third generation.

A cathedral of smoke, honored by the James Beard Foundation with an American Classics award. Old-time atmosphere, and still a Mueller behind the counter.

  • Three generationsLouie, then Bobby, now Wayne at the pit.
  • Plain and old-schoolClassic Texas barbecue. Nothing fancy or trendy.
  • Ships to your doorCan't make the drive? We ship nationwide through Goldbelly.
