Our story

Fire, smoke and a little sci-fi

Look for the orange script sign over a black storefront. Polish sausage, pork belly and pulled lamb share the pit with the brisket.

Chef and lead fire tender John Bates, a Corpus Christi native, calls it Modern Texas barbecue. Michelin star in 2024 and 2025.

  • Meats you don't see elsewhereLamb, duck, pork belly and sausage. Creative, never careless.
  • Sides with backboneBrown butter mac, smoked scalloped potatoes, charro beans.
  • Worth the waitGood barbecue takes time. Come early.
