Kitchen notes

From brewery idea to BBQ trailer

A born-and-raised South Austinite, in restaurants since 2003, had an idea over good beer: put great BBQ and craft beer in one business.

Fresh ingredients, local beer, and a slow and low approach to smoking meats. Now burgers too.
