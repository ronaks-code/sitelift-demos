Meet the crew

Jonathan, the fire and the story

Sí Baby-Q began as a shared vision: a barbecue concept that honored Texas smoke while embracing the bold flavors and cultural influences each founder grew up with.

Pitmaster Jonathan Lagos, a Mexican-American cook trained in Austin barbecue programs, is now sole owner. The menu still blends Central Texas technique with Southeast Asian and Mexican-inspired flavors.
