What neighbors say about Sí Baby-Q.

Said at the picnic table

What an amazing BBQ experience! The sausage was very tender, moist, and flavorful. The brisket was just as good. The location has been there for less than a year and in a nice little trailer park area with other food trucks. Seating was good as we were able to get some shade while we were eating. The Mac and cheese was solid but the street corn was what really stole the show for me.

Isac K. ★★★★★

Best bbq I had during my stay in Austin. Alex was awesome and helpful. Let me know their story and the “secret” labor of love. The quality is insane! Melt in your mouth brisket. Ribs leave tooth mark, but can slide off the bone. Mac and cheese to die for. Dessert absolutely great! Will be back

Seth G. ★★★★★
