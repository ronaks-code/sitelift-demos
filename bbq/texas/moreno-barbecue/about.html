Our story

Meat first, off I-35

It started as a truck. Now it's a full restaurant with a bar, wood tables and the game on the TVs.

All natural Hartley Ranch Angus beef, house-made sausage, and pork belly with a house rub and post oak smoke. Friendly crew, short lines late in the day.

  • Every meat its ownSmoky pulled pork, spicy pork belly. No two meats taste alike.
