Kitchen notes

Papaw's name, Brad's smoker

Brad's grandfather Jim Yerkes ran Jim's Smokehouse in Rockport from 1970 to 1985. Brad worked there as a kid and watched how Papaw smoked.

Since 2019 Brad has brought that smokehouse to Austin. Family owned, prepared simply, with fresh ingredients.

  • Prepared simplyFresh ingredients, good meats.
