Coming from Bee Cave? Jims Smokehouse Four Points is in Austin, at The Rock House on RM 620. Brisket, ribs, sausage and tacos, open seven days a week.

What to order

From the smoker

Brisket, ribs and sausage, by the pound or on a bun.

ITEM 1

Meats by the pound

Black Angus brisket, baby back ribs, sausage and turkey breast, sold by the pound. Tender, smoky, juicy.

ITEM 2

Tacos

Brisket tacos with sweet pineapple pico. The menu also lists the De Leon, Maui Waui and Jim's Habanero.

ITEM 3

Sandwiches & plates

Sliced brisket and house sandwiches, or a 2 meats 2 sides plate with mac and cheese, jicama slaw and bacon jam.

Stopped by Jim’s Smokehouse and was really impressed. The brisket was tender, smoky, and full of flavor without being overly salty. The cheddar jalapeño sausage was absolutely delicious with just the right amount of spice and smoke. The mac and cheese was creamy and rich, and adding the bacon jam was chef’s kiss 🤌🏼. The BBQ sauce was so good I ended up putting it on everything. Service was absolutely PHENOMENAL!

This really is the way bbq was meant to be enjoyed.
