Our story

Cookery, culture, community

We cook from the flavors and heritage of the African American diaspora: pecan and mixed hardwood smoke, bigger spice, nose to tail, root to stem.

We want a welcoming, food-driven place that grows creativity, equity and education in the community. We work with local farms, artisans and historical preservation groups.

The trailer opened on August 28, 2020. Juicy smoked chicken, tender ribs that hold to the bone, a mustard-based sauce.

  • SmokePecan and mixed hardwood, cooked here.
  • SpiceBigger flavor. The sides bring some heat.
  • CommunityLocal farms, artisans and neighbors.
