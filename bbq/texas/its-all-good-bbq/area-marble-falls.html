Brisket · Ribs · Chicken

From the pit

What we smoke and what goes with it.

1

Brisket

Smoked brisket by the pound, half pound, plate or sandwich. Tender, juicy, with a dark bark.

2

Ribs, chicken and sausage

Pit-smoked pork ribs, baby backs, smoked half chicken and Hill Country sausage. Plates come with pickles, onions, jalapeño and bread.

3

Drive-thru and holiday orders

Order and pick up right off Highway 71. For the holidays we smoke whole turkeys and hams on site.
