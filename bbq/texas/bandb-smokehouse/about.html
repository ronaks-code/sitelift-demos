Kitchen notes

The red building on Pleasanton Road

A red block building with a longhorn painted on the wall, Bar-B-Q in white letters over the door and a catering number.

Inside: corrugated tin walls, an old wooden bench and wood tables under big windows. Order at the counter and find a seat.

  • Three brisket cutsFrom fatty to lean, your call.
  • Sides that hold upCrisp coleslaw and potato salad.
  • Friendly counterWarm service at the front counter.
