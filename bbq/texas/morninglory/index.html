About

Slow mornings, lunch dates, everything between

A bright, plant-filled room with cage pendant lights and steel shelving, built for gathering. Creative dishes and familiar favorites, all day.

Quick, fresh food. Friendly, attentive service. Easy with babies and kids.

Come for the food, stay for the company. The coffee bar pours lattes, tea, smoothies and mimosas.
