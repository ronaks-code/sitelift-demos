Our story

Barbecue on Hoefgen Avenue

Central Texas barbecue with Mexican influence. Order and eat outdoors under string lights, on picnic tables and metal chairs.

Brisket first, then tortillas, house pickles and bread pudding. Friendly staff, and a line that keeps moving.

  • Smoked in houseBrisket, ribs, turkey, pulled pork and house-made sausage.
  • Fresh tortillasHomemade flour tortillas, soft while they're hot.
  • Worth the lineThe line moves quickly. Portions are full.
