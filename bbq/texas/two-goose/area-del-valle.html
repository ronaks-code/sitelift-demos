What we do well

Breakfast burritos, barbecue by the pound and trailer sandos, all smoked and made here.

1

Brisket breakfast burritos

The Big Honkin Burrito stacks smoked brisket, scrambled eggs, potatoes, cheddar and Sunrise sauce. Shareable and filling.

2

Barbecue by the pound

Traditional meat market style: brisket, turkey, pork belly, chicken and pulled pork with bread, pickles, onions and sauce.

3

Trailer sandos

Cheesesteak, banh mi and smoked chicken sandwiches built from the meat off the pit.
