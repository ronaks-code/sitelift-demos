Pull up a chair

Two friends spent years visiting Texas dive bars and honky-tonks, then built their own in Buda.

Opened July 4, 2014, among old live oaks that shade the cold drinks, the live music and the kids.

Order at the bar and the food comes to you. Dogs on a leash are welcome. Misting fans for summer, fire pits for winter.
