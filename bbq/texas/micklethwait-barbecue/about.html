Our story

An old church in East Austin

Smoking Texas barbecue since 2012. Now in an old neighborhood church among the pecan trees, with a deck up front and a shaded courtyard out back.

Relaxed even when busy. Picnic tables under umbrellas, friendly staff at the counter, and a little yellow trailer out in the yard.

  • Oak-firedOak-smoked meats from a little Michelin-recognized joint.
  • From scratchSides and baked goods made in house.
  • Room for everyoneIndoor seating, a shaded courtyard and a backyard patio.
