Before you book

Good to know

When are you open?

Thursday to Sunday, 11 AM to 5 PM. We are closed Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.

Can I order to go or ahead of time?

Not at this time. We cannot take to-go orders or pre-orders, so come by Thursday to Sunday.

What is the best way to reach you?

Text us. Peak hours are busy and we cannot always answer calls.

Do you do catering or private events?

Yes, send us a message about catering, private events and large orders.

What comes with meat ordered by the pound?

Pickles, onions, pickled red onions, pickled jalapenos and a side of sweet and tangy sauce.
