Our story

On the historic East Side

Under a black sign with teal script and a little orange flame. Lunch six days a week, until the meat runs out.

Great barbecue starts with the ingredients. USDA Prime brisket, heritage Duroc pork ribs and shoulders, and turkeys raised for a clean, honest taste.

Casual, busy and unfussy. Wood tables, red diner chairs and a menu board over the counter.
