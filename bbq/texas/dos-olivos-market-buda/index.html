About

Old World table, Main Street Buda

An original marketplace where Old World tradition meets Texas charm, with a dining room, a wall of wine and shelves of olive oils and gourmet foods.

We grew up with European food traditions. We look for makers with a real story, and want you to slow down and taste something new.

Relaxed and inviting, with friendly, attentive service. Indoor and outdoor seating, and live music.
