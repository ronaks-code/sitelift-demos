About B. Cooper

One pit, one trailer, since a farmer's market stand in 2013.

A trailer, a pit, patience

Blaine started as a farmer's market stand in 2013, then grew into a food trailer. It now sits at Inn Cahoots on East 6th Street.

His approach is simple: low smoking temperatures, live post oak fire and long hours tending the pit, in the traditions of Central Texas barbecue.

Seating is outdoors at picnic tables, in the shade. Parking is easy.

  • Live post oak fireLow temperatures and long hours at the pit.
  • Scratch made sidesPotato salad made fresh from scratch.
  • Outdoor picnic feelAll seating is outdoors, under the shade.
