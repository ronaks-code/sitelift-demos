Meet Brooklyn's

A family-owned restaurant in historic downtown Buda. From-scratch Southern comfort food, done right.

The corner has been a Buda gathering place for over a century. In 2017 it became Brooklyn's Down South.

Come in for lunch, brunch or a night at the bar.

  • From scratchBiscuits, grits and more, made here.
