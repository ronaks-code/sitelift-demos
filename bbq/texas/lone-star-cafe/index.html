Our story

A Texas welcome on the river

Serving locals and tourists since 1981. The Texas-sized balcony has shaded seating over the water.

Plain Texas cooking: steaks, burgers, smoked brisket, pork chops and chicken fried steak. Friendly staff in the kitchen and on the floor.

  • Big platesCountry fried steak and full plates.
  • Fair pricesLunch specials that are easy on the wallet.
  • Seats for the groupBirthdays, reunions, corporate dinners. Private dining and catering.
