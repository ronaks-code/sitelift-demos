A little smokehouse, a big story

Barbecue on a 40ft indoor pit with only mesquite, a dry rub and a slow cook. Brisket, sausage, pork ribs, beef ribs, lamb ribs and turkey.

In 2004 Tanya's father asked her to bring her restaurant experience to run the place. Texas Monthly named it one of the Top 50 BBQ Joints in Texas.

An old-school mom and pop place. Friendly staff, big brisket sandwiches and a sauce for any dry bite.
