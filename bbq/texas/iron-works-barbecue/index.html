The old Weigl iron shop

A former blacksmith tin building, once the Weigl family's ornamental iron workshop. The red corrugated front, stone base and iron work are still here.

Barbecued meats, led by beef ribs. Spicy and regular sauces are made in-house, and the dry seasoning goes into everything from the meats to the chili.

Low-key counter ordering, historical pieces to look at and outdoor seating above the Waller Creek path. Meat can sell out, so earlier is safer.
