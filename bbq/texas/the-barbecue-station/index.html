Come see Zac, Mark or Jay

A family place. The Peacocks bought the old Exxon station in 1992 and kept it simple: wood-smoked meat and traditional sides, made fresh daily.

Zac, Mark or Jay will pull your meat off the pit and slice it in front of you, under vintage gas-station signs.
