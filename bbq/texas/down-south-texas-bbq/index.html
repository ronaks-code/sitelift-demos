Our story

From our family table to yours

Started in 2014 with a simple idea: bring people together over Southern barbecue. Rico smokes the meat, his brother P makes the sides, their mom bakes the desserts.

Every dish on the menu is named for a friend or family member who ate at the table early on, tried new recipes and helped open the doors. That is why you will find Bobby's, Mrs. Forbes', Grandma Joyce's and Big Mama's on the board.

  • Smoked low and slowBrisket, ribs, pulled pork and turkey from the pit.
  • Family recipesRico's meats, P's sides and their mom's desserts.
  • Everybody's welcomeEvery guest is treated like family.
