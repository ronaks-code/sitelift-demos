About Curry Boys

Asian barbecue from three chefs, in a pink house.

Since the first pot of coffee

Southeast Asia meets Texas

Opened in October 2020, then moved across the street into the Pink Palace, a historic home in Tobin Hills, just north of Downtown.

Chefs Andrew Ho, Sean Wen and Andrew Samia built it around one idea: the Perfect Bowl of Southeast Asian curry and slow-smoked Texas meat.

  • Smoked over post oakPrime brisket, pulled pork, pork belly, chicken thigh and sausage.
  • Curry on everythingGreen, yellow, Penang and Massaman, plus curry queso.
  • Sides with a twistCold chili garlic noodles, curry creamed corn and herb slaw.
