About

A spot for every meal

Bloomington needed one spot for breakfast, lunch and dinner. The menu is comfort food with an international flair: fast, fresh and healthy.

Order and pay at the counter, then seat yourself, or order online for Grab-n-Go or delivery. Open room, community tables, succulents, and a roomy patio.

We recycle, compost and use locally sourced, clean ingredients. Eric is general manager, with Lizzie, Lena, Brody and Jocabed leading the house.
