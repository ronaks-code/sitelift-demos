Pizza buffet

All you can eat, 11 to 2 and 5 to 8. Pizza, breadsticks and a drink, soup and salad too. Kids 3 and under eat free with a paid adult.

Salad bar & soup

Open all day, 11 to 8. Greens, spinach, tomatoes, blueberries, pineapple and more to build your bowl, with soup alongside.

Breadsticks with plenty of cheese, garlic sauce, plus wings and sandwiches served with chips.
