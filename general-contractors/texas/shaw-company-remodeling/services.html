Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Closed on weekends.

How do I get started?

Call or send the form on the contact page. Your project type, location and a rough budget range are enough, and estimates are fine.

Do you start with a visit?

Yes. The process starts with an in-home walkthrough, so the house and the scope are clear before anyone is asked to commit.

Can I see materials in person?

Yes. The San Antonio showroom is a place to compare materials and make selections with a designer. Call to make an appointment.

Where do you work?

San Antonio, Stone Oak, Shavano Park, Alamo Heights, Boerne, Bulverde, New Braunfels and Schertz. San Antonio service is limited to select areas, so call if unsure.

What do you remodel?

Kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, additions and outdoor living areas, one room or several.

How much will it cost?

Every home is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a request and we will talk through your project.
