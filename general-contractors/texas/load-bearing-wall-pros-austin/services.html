Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed on weekends.

Where is the Lakeway office?

2802 Flintrock Trace #201, Lakeway. The crew serves the Austin metro, including Cedar Park, Round Rock, Georgetown and Pflugerville.

How fast does the work go?

Most projects are done in one day. Walls often come out in under 8 hours.

How does pricing work?

Same-day ballpark by phone, text or email, then a free site visit and final estimate. $6,000–$15,000 first beam, $2,500–$7,500 each additional.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, a written lifetime transferable structural warranty.

Is the work engineered?

A Professional Engineer on staff designs every project, and an independent materials-testing lab certifies the beams and supports.

What is not included?

Finish out such as HVAC, electric, plumbing, sheetrock, trim, texture and paint. These can be quoted separately.
