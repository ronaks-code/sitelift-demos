Good to know

Questions people ask

What do you work on?

Full interior and exterior remodels: tile, painting, carpet, fence, roof and electrical. Also showers, ceiling fans, TV mounting and charger outlets.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 7 PM, and Saturday and Sunday, 8 AM to 7 PM.

Where do you work?

In and around Converse, Texas. Farther out is sometimes possible, so call and ask about your address.

Who will I talk to?

Rhonda handles scheduling, payment options and invoices. Eloy, the project manager, visits the job and gives the quote.

How do I get a quote?

Call or send a short description of the job. Photos help.

Do you work on small jobs too?

Yes. Ceiling fans, TV mounting and car charger outlets, as well as bathroom remodels. Call and ask.

Planning a remodel?

Call or message KC Renovations. Open seven days a week.
