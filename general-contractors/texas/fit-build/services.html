Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I get a price before work starts?

Yes. You get an itemized, fixed-scope estimate to approve before work begins. No surprise fees.

Who runs my project?

A named general contractor and a supervisor who runs your project from demo to done, and answers your calls.

How long will my remodel take?

It depends on the project, and you get the schedule up front. Then the crew works to it.

Can you rework a layout?

Yes. Reworked layouts, and rooms converted into bedrooms with en suite baths.

Do you build walk-in showers?

Yes. Walk-in showers, tile, vanities and full bathroom renovations, built safe and built to last.

Where do you work?

Bastrop, Austin and the surrounding area. Call to check your address.
