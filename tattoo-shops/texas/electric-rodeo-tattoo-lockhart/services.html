Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Absolutely. Walk-ins are taken first come, first served. Come by Tuesday to Thursday 12 to 8, Friday and Saturday 12 to 10, or Sunday 2 to 6, or call (512) 668-4088 to check how busy it is.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 18 with a valid photo ID to get tattooed. A parent can't sign for you.

How do I book an appointment?

To guarantee a specific day and time, we take a non-refundable deposit that comes off the total cost of your tattoo. Need to reschedule? Let us know at least 24 hours ahead and the deposit moves to your new date.

Do you do piercing?

Yes. Our piercers Andy and Tita each bring thirty years of experience and are in open to close, Tuesday through Sunday, for piercing and jewelry.

Where is the shop?

116 E Market St, Lockhart, TX 78644, right on the courthouse square. Look for the saloon doors and the walls of flash.

What styles do you tattoo?

The shop is known for bold American traditional, plus custom pieces, black and grey, and color work. Bring your idea and the artists will draw it up.
