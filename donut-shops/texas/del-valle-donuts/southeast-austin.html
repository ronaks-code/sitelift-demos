Preguntas / FAQ

Southeast Austin questions

Is there a donut shop near the Austin airport?

Yes. Del Valle Donuts is at 12800 Pearce Ln, Del Valle, TX 78617, a short drive southeast of the airport and SH 71. We open at 5 AM Monday through Saturday, early enough for the first shift.

Do you have kolaches in Del Valle?

Yes. Sausage and cheese, jalapeño cheese, jumbo, jumbo jalapeño, pigs and fruit kolaches, fresh every morning.

Can I pick up donuts for a Del Valle school or office event?

Yes. Call (512) 290-1811 or send a request from our order page with how many dozen you need and when.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 5:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Closed Sunday.
