About Tim

Tim fixes all kinds of furniture, loose or broken: wobbly chairs reglued, broken legs and bases rebuilt, rockers, recliners and antique cane-back chairs put right, and new wood stained to match the old.

Our story

A Bloomington woodshop that fixes what you've got

Tim's Furniture Repair is Tim's woodshop on the east side of Bloomington, out on State Road 46 Bypass. Look for the blue sign with the dragons at the driveway. Tim does every repair himself, and fixing furniture so people can keep enjoying it is the part he loves.

Most of what comes through the door is chairs: dining and kitchen chairs that have gone wobbly, a wing-back with a leg snapped in a move, a recliner with broken wooden parts, antique cane-back chairs, rolling barrel chairs that sat in a barn for 15 years. Tim takes them apart, rebuilds what's broken to the original design, finds wheels that match and stains new wood so it blends in.

The shop itself is a comeback story. A plaque by the door thanks the local businesses and trades who helped get the wood shop back in business, from the concrete crew to the electricians and the tree service.
