About Original Look

Original Look Upholstery is a small husband-and-wife shop in Pflugerville. Wake boat interiors, torn leather car seats, worn-out dining chairs: bring it in, pick your material from the samples, and get it back looking like new, and often more comfortable than before.

Our story

A husband-and-wife shop in Pflugerville

Original Look Upholstery is run by Vera and her husband out of their shop at 1502 Massengale St in Pflugerville. Boats, cars and furniture all come through the same door, and the same two people do the work.

A lot of it is wake boats: Malibu, Tigé and Centurion interiors redone in marine vinyl with the original look, or a fresh color scheme. One Malibu owner said the work came out better than what came from the factory.

The rest is car seats and furniture: torn leather driver's seats, dining chairs that didn't hold up, carved heirloom chairs. Customers pick from a large selection of samples, and Vera helps them choose.
