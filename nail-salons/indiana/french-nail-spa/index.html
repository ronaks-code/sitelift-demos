What we do

Nails, waxing and hot stone

French Nail Spa is a nail and waxing spa at 601 N College Ave in downtown Bloomington, a short walk from the square and IU. Look for the hot-pink sign: inside it's warm wood, brick and a full wall of colors, with manicure stations up front and pedicure chairs along the windows.

Manicures & gel

Classic or gel polish, shaped and finished clean, in any shade off the color wall.

Full acrylic sets & fills

Full sets for you and your friends, from simple and clean to ombre, foil and stars. Fills too.

French tips

The one we're named for: classic white tips, or black tips with glitter, pearls and rhinestones.

Nail art

Accent nails, dots, swirls, stars and color pops, done by hand.

Pedicures

Settle into one of the pedicure chairs along the front windows and put your feet up.

Waxing & hot stone

It's right there on the sign: waxing and hot stone, in a quieter room at the back.
