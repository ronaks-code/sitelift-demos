Questions

Frequently asked

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 9:30 AM to 7 PM, Friday 9 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 6:30 PM. Closed Sunday.

Where is Lady Nails?

1100 E Palm Valley Blvd, Round Rock, TX 78664. Look for the pink LADY NAILS sign.

How long have you been open?

Lady Nails has served Round Rock since 1994. Some clients have been coming for more than 15 years.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. Busy times can mean a wait, so call (512) 218-9736 ahead and you'll be in and out much faster.

Do you do acrylic and SNS dip powder?

Yes. Gel, acrylic full sets and fills, and SNS dip powder, in any shape and length.

Do you do nails for kids and men?

Yes. We do nails for women, kids and men.

Can you do custom nail art?

Yes. Hearts, ghosts, sunflowers, polka dots, zebra print, cartoon characters, gold foil, 3D bows, charms and rhinestones. Bring a photo of the look you want.

Can I request a specific nail tech?

Yes. Ask for Linda, Trinity or Debbie by name when you call.
