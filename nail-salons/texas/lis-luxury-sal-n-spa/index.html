Bienvenidas · Welcome

Meet the salon

Everything under one roof.

Lis Luxury Salón Spa is a full beauty salon in Suite 113 on Spring Hill Ln in Pflugerville: mint pedicure chairs with gold frames, a wall of red roses, warm oak floors and that curved, glowing logo wall.

Clients come for long sculpted sets, French tips and nail art, pedicures, brows and lash extensions. What they talk about afterwards is the feeling: clean, calm, welcoming, a team that makes you comfortable from the moment you walk in, and a good cup of coffee.
