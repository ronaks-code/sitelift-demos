About Galaxy Nails

Galaxy Nails Spa is a neighborhood nail salon at 2051 Gattis School Rd in Round Rock: acrylic, solar and SNS dip sets, gel, Gel-X and builder gel, hot stone and fresh orange spa pedicures, kids manicures, and the 3D flowers, chrome, glitter and rhinestones that make a set yours. Open seven days a week, walk-ins welcome.

Our story

In Round Rock since 2019

Galaxy Nails Spa has been doing nails in Round Rock since 2019, in the shopping center at 2051 Gattis School Rd, Suite 510. We're open seven days a week and walk-ins are welcome. We love Round Rock, and that's why we're here.

Our regulars say it best: some have been coming in for more than five years, and they come back for the people as much as the nails. Clients ask for Lyana, Lina, Jennifer and Cyndy by name, and say the whole team is patient, accommodating and loves a good laugh. Nextdoor neighbors named us a Neighborhood Favorite in 2024 and 2025.

Bring an inspiration photo, a wedding date or a holiday idea. From a clean French or builder gel to a full set of 3D flowers and gold chrome, or a fresh orange pedicure with a hot stone massage, we'll take the time to get it exactly how you want it.
