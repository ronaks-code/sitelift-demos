What we do

Services

Galaxy Nails Spa is a neighborhood nail salon at 2051 Gattis School Rd in Round Rock: acrylic, solar and SNS dip sets, gel, Gel-X and builder gel, hot stone and fresh orange spa pedicures, kids manicures, and the 3D flowers, chrome, glitter and rhinestones that make a set yours. Open seven days a week, walk-ins welcome.

Acrylic sets & refills

Full sets and fills in any shape and length, from soft pink ombre to bold glitter tips.

Solar & SNS dip

Solar and dipping powder sets in natural, color, pink and white or ombre.

Spa pedicures

From a classic pedicure to hot stone, Dr. Teal's, milk and honey and fresh orange.

Nail art & 3D designs

Hand-painted florals, 3D flowers, chrome, charms and rhinestones, matched to your inspiration photo.

Gel, Gel-X & builder gel

Glossy gel color, Gel-X extensions and builder gel that grows out clean, without lifting.

Manicures & French tips

Classic and hot stone manicures, crisp French tips and kids manicures for little ones.
