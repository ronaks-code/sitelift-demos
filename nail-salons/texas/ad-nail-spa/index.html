What we do

Services

AD Nail Spa is Adri's one-chair studio at 110 S Lampasas St in Round Rock. She specializes in the Russian manicure, with precise cuticle prep for a cleaner finish and gel that lasts. She also does structured gel, extensions, waterless pedicures, French tips and finishes, all with sterilized tools.

01

Gel manicure

Russian-technique cuticle prep, then gel polish cured under UV for a high-shine finish that lasts. Clients say they've never had lifting or chipping.

→02

Structured gel overlay

Strength for fragile, brittle or uneven natural nails, with no added length. It's the “blindagem” you'll see all over Adri's Instagram.

→03

Regular manicure

Classic polish chosen from a whole wall of colors, from Colorama reds to soft nudes. It lasts up to 7 days and is easy to change.

→04

Waterless pedicure

No soaking. Disposable liners, callus smoothing, hydration and a foot massage, finished in regular or gel polish.

→05

Nail extensions

Instant length with Gel-X, or sculpted hard gel on dual forms for durable, lightweight, natural-looking nails.

→06

French & finishes

Regular or smile-line French, chrome, cat eye and simple nail art, added to any manicure or pedicure.

The A and D in AD

Meet Adri.

AD stands for Adriane Dallabrida, “Adri” (or “Dri”) to her clients. She is Brazilian, and she runs AD Nail Spa on her own from a private studio on S Lampasas St in downtown Round Rock. Her certificates hang above the polish wall.

Her specialty is the Russian manicure: careful, precise cuticle prep that gives a cleaner finish and makes gel last longer. Her tools go through cleaning, individual packaging and autoclave sterilization, and her pedicures are waterless, with disposable liners. Clients say she never rushes, she's charismatic and funny, and there's always coffee on.
