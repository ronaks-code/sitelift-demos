What we do

Services

Sparkle Nails is a North Austin nail salon at 1701 W Parmer Ln, open seven days a week. Manicures and gel, spa pedicures, SNS dip powder, solar full sets, French tips, chrome, cat-eye and custom nail art, done by techs who take the time to get your shape and color right.

01

Manicures & gel

Classic and deluxe manicures, plus shellac gel that needs no drying time and won't smudge.

→02

Spa pedicures

Classic, deluxe and spa organic pedicures in a spa chair, from a quick polish to the full treatment.

→03

Signature spa pedicures

Pick your fragrance from the Volcano Spa and Collagen Spa menus and settle in for a longer soak.

→04

SNS dip powder

Signature Nail System dip powder: odorless, lightweight and glossy, and it holds up between visits.

→05

Solar & acrylic sets

Full sets and refills in solar, pink & white or color powder, at the length and shape you want.

→06

Nail art & chrome

Chrome, cat-eye, glitter, gems and hand-painted lines, from one accent nail to a full set.
