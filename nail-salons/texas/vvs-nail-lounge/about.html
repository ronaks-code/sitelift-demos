About VVS

VVS Nail Lounge is Jasmine Truong's private nail studio inside MySalonSuite at 2711 La Frontera Blvd in Round Rock. Gel-X from $60, hard gel and dip, gel manicures, four spa pedicures and custom nail art, by appointment, 7 days a week.

Our story

A private nail studio where luxury meets precision

VVS Nail Lounge is Jasmine Truong's private nail studio inside MySalonSuite at 2711 La Frontera Blvd in Round Rock. Jasmine is the owner, a wife and a mom of three, and she built VVS around a simple idea: a clean, calm, elevated nail experience focused on detail, quality and healthy nail care.

Every service is done with intention, from meticulous cuticle care to perfectly balanced shaping. Clients say she takes her time and never rushes: Gel-X that looks clean and natural, stilettos filed perfectly even, and custom art that matches the picture in your head, or improves on it.

They come back for her, too. Reviews call her sweet, down-to-earth and easy to talk to, the kind of nail artist you start calling your nail bestie. Moms bring their daughters, friends send friends, and the 4.9 rating comes from clients who keep rebooking.
