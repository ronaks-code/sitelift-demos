About Pretty Nails

Pretty Nails is a warm, no-fuss nail salon on W University Ave in Georgetown. Settle into a massage pedicure chair, pick a shade from the polish wall, or bring a photo of the design you want and let the team paint it by hand.

Our story

Your neighborhood nail salon on University Ave

Pretty Nails is a neighborhood nail salon at 950 W University Ave, Suite 206, in Georgetown, open six days a week. Inside it's golden walls, a burgundy chair rail, a full wall of polish and a row of massage pedicure chairs under amber pendant lights.

Many clients have been coming for five or six years and bring their mothers, mothers-in-law and friends. One regular, who had her nails done in San Francisco for 25 years, calls it the very best in Georgetown. Another drives in from out of town just to see Cassandra.
