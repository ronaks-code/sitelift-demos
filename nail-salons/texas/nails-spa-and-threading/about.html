About us

Nails Spa & Threading is a family-run nail salon at 3010 Williams Dr in Georgetown. Manicures and deluxe spa pedicures, solar and color gel, acrylic, SNS dipping powder, pink & white, air brush and nail design, plus waxing and eyebrow threading, all under one red THREADING sign.

Our story

Family-run on Williams Drive

Nails Spa & Threading is a family-run salon in the strip center at 3010 Williams Dr in Georgetown. Christine and Richard own it and work it: Richard does nails (regulars swear by his pink & whites), and Christine does pedicures and the eyebrow threading. Some clients have followed them for more than a decade, from their first location to this one.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: the salon is spotless, the owners are hardworking and genuine, and they do their best to fit walk-ins in. Chair remotes stay wrapped in plastic, pedicure basins are emptied and sanitized right after each client, and disposable liners and personal care boxes are there on request.
