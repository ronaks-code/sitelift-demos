The Polished Pearl Nail Studio

About Stef

The Polished Pearl is a private one-artist nail suite in Pflugerville run by Estefania (Stef), a licensed, CMP- and e-file-certified nail technician who specializes in Russian dry manicures and dry pedicures, custom nail art and nail repair, with a real emphasis on healthy skin and nails.

Our story

Licensed since 2022, one cozy chair

The Polished Pearl is a private nail suite in Suite 203, Room 4, on Central Commerce Drive in Pflugerville, run by Estefania, or Stef to her clients. She has been licensed since the end of 2022 and is CMP and e-file certified. It's one chair and one client at a time, so nothing is rushed.

Russian dry manicures and dry pedicures are the specialty: precision e-file cuticle work, detailed prep and color that sits right at the cuticle line, with sterilized tools and a clean studio every time. Stef also does Japanese soft gel extensions, nail repair and paramedical foot care, and clients say their nails have stayed healthy and grown long.

Her favourite part is the nail art, from soft feminine designs to detailed trendy sets. The space is cozy on purpose, with snacks, a great playlist and plenty of conversation. Plan on taking some time: bring your laptop or put on a movie. Clients drive in from all over, one of them all the way from San Antonio.
