Questions.

Can I choose where my car gets repaired?

Yes. You have the right to have your vehicle repaired at the shop of your choice, regardless of who you are insured by.

Do I need to get several estimates?

No. By law you only need one repair estimate.

What warranty comes with the repair?

Our body and paint work carries a Limited Lifetime Warranty. Parts are covered by the manufacturer for 12 months or 12,000 miles.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

Cars, trucks, RV's and motorcycles, foreign and domestic. One reviewer recommends us for classic cars too.

Who do I call for a tow?

Our site points customers to Delta Towing.
