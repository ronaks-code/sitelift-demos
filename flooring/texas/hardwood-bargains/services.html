Good to know

Questions people ask

Where is the showroom?

Suite 200 at 10300 Metric Blvd in Austin. There are also showrooms in Cedar Park, Houston and Nashville.

Can I get samples?

Yes. Samples are free in store, and sample kits can be ordered online.

What do you sell?

Factory-made hardwood flooring, rigid core vinyl flooring and wall slats and panels, plus commercial projects.

Do you help find installers?

Our team can help with choosing installers. Ask about the installer network.

Do you work with builders and designers?

Yes. We take commercial projects and have a trade program for designers and builders.

How do I get pricing?

Clearance prices are posted at the time of writing. For anything else, call or send a message for a clear cost breakdown.

Not sure where to start?

Call, chat or stop by the showroom. Samples are better than pictures.
