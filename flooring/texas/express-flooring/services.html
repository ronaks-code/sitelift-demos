Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 8am to 10pm, Friday 8am to 9pm, Saturday and Sunday 8am to 7pm.

What does the in-home consultation cost?

It is free. A design consultant brings samples to your home so you can see them in your own lighting.

Do you move the furniture?

Yes, for free. The crew moves it and returns it when the job is done.

How fast can you install?

Many projects can be done as soon as the next day, because most of the catalog is kept in stock.

Is there a warranty on the installation?

Yes. Express Flooring offers a free lifetime installation warranty, and if there is ever a problem with the install, they take care of it.

Who will I deal with?

A design consultant guides your choices, then an installation crew does the work. The operations team checks in during the install.

Which towns do you serve?

San Antonio and nearby, including Converse, Schertz, Boerne and New Braunfels.

Is financing available?

Express Flooring offers financing. Ask your design consultant about options.

Ready for new floors?

Call Express Flooring any day. Hours run 8am to 10pm on weekdays and 8am to 7pm on weekends.
