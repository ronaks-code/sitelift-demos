Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed on weekends.

Where do you work?

We are locally owned and serve Austin, San Antonio and the surrounding areas, and we are based in Lakeway. Call to check that we cover your address.

Do you work on rentals and commercial spaces?

Yes. Landlords and property managers are regular clients, alongside commercial and residential work.

Yes. Same-week, next-day and sometimes same-day help. Call and tell us your timing.

Yes, carpet stretching is one of our services, alongside new carpet installation.
