Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you have a showroom I can visit?

We run a mobile showroom. We bring hardwood, tile, carpet and vinyl samples to your home so you can see them in your own light.

How do I get started?

Call or send the contact form. We will set up an in-home consultation.

Where do you work?

We serve Buda, Austin and nearby towns. Call to check that we cover your address.

What kinds of flooring do you offer?

Hardwood, luxury vinyl plank and tile, carpet, carpet tile and laminate.

Do you do installation?

Yes. Our installation crew is courteous, professional and tidy.

How much will it cost?

Every home is different. After the consultation you get a detailed quote that fits your budget.
