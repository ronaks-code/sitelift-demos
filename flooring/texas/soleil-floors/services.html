Good to know

Questions people ask

Can I take samples home?

Yes. We help you pick samples to take home before you decide.

Do you measure at home?

Yes. We measure in your home and help you choose the floor that fits how you live.

What floors do you install?

Carpet, luxury vinyl plank, tile and linoleum, plus bathroom remodels and patio resurfacing.

Do you work with builders?

Yes. Small jobs get the same attention as large ones.

Which areas do you serve?

Round Rock, plus North Austin, Pflugerville, Westlake, Georgetown, Steiner Ranch and Cedar Park. Not sure you are covered? Call the showroom and ask.

When is the showroom open?

Monday to Friday, 10 AM–5 PM, and Saturday, 10 AM–2 PM. Closed Sunday. Holiday hours may differ, so call ahead.

Where are you?
