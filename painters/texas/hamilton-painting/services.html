Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Seven days a week, 7am to 7pm.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Hamilton Painting offers a lifetime warranty on its work and puts it in writing.

What paint do you use?

Benjamin Moore, in several grades, including a scuff resistant line for busy areas.

Do you do drywall and door repairs?

Yes. Drywall and door repairs are done alongside the painting.

Will you move the furniture?

Yes. The service includes moving furniture and a thorough clean-up when the job is complete.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text, or use the form on the contact page. The crew usually gives a quote, not an estimate, after discussing the details.

Do you serve my town?

The shop is in Round Rock. Call to ask about Pflugerville, Georgetown, Hutto or Cedar Park.

Got rooms to paint?

Call or text Hamilton Painting any day, 7am to 7pm.
