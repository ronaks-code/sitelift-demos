Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. We provide free quotes on all our interior painting services. Call or send a message with a short description and photos.

Do you clean up each day?

Yes. Floors and furnishings are covered, and each night the work areas are kept clean and organized.

What if I find something missed after the job?

We do a final walkthrough together, and return touchups are always free.

How do you paint cabinets?

We mask off the area, then clean, sand, caulk, prime if needed and spray the cabinets rather than brush or roll them.
