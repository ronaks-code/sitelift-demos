Treatments

What we paint and fix

Interior, exterior and cabinets, plus drywall repair and popcorn ceiling removal.

  1. 1

    Interior painting

    Walls, ceilings and trim. Floors, furniture and appliances are covered, small holes filled, dark walls primed, and every surface gets two coats.

    • Prep and masking
    • Wall and trim painting
    • Final walkthrough
  2. 2

    Exterior painting

    Pressure washing, scraping, oil primer on bare wood, caulked cracks, then two top coats.

    • Pressure washing
    • Scrape, prime, caulk
    • Two top coats
  3. 3

    Cabinets

    Cabinets are cleaned, sanded, caulked, primed if needed and sprayed, never brushed or rolled, for a furniture-grade finish.

    • Kitchens and vanities
    • Doors and drawers
    • Paint or stain
