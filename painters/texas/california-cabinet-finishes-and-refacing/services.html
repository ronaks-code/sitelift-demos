How it works

How a cabinet refinish goes

  1. 1

    Doors and drawers come off

    On the first day they are removed and taken back to the shop.

  2. 2

    Wash, sand, fill

    The cabinets are washed and sanded, and any holes are filled.

  3. 3

    Spray and prime

    Doors and drawers are sprayed offsite, frames at your home, with 1-2 coats of primer.

  4. 4

    Finish coats

    2-3 coats of paint go on, then the doors go back up, with soft close hinges if you like.

Good to know

Questions people ask

How do you refinish cabinets?

Doors and drawers come off on day one and go to the shop. They are washed, sanded, filled, primed and painted. Frames are sprayed at your home.

How many coats do you use?

1-2 coats of primer and 2-3 coats of paint, so the minimum on the cabinets is 3. Some jobs need more, depending on the work required.

Which paint do you use?

Gemini EVO.

Can I get soft close hinges?

Yes, you can choose to have the doors and drawers installed with soft close hinges.

Who does the work?

Casey Jones, the owner. Nothing is outsourced.

Where do you work?

Austin and Central Texas, including Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Spicewood and Cedar Park. Call to check your address.

How do I get a quote?

Call or send the form. Casey comes out to assess the cabinets and gives you a free estimate.
