Services

Painted, stained or refaced

Three ways to renew the cabinets you already own.

01

Cabinet painting and refinishing

Doors and drawers are removed, washed, sanded, filled and sprayed in the shop. Frames are sprayed at your home.

  • 1-2 coats of primer
  • 2-3 coats of paint
  • Doors sprayed offsite
02

Cabinet staining

A professional new stain, or a custom paint, to bring tired cabinets back to life and give them years of longevity.

  • Custom stains
  • Special finishes
  • Oak and wood tones
03

Cabinet refacing

Replace dated doors and finish them right, at a fraction of a full remodel. Soft close hinges available.

  • New cabinet doors
  • Soft close hinges
  • Added cabinets for new appliances
