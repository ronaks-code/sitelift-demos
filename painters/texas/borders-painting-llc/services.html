Services

Painting and the prep behind it

What Borders Painting handles across Round Rock and Austin.

Treatments

What we paint

Homes and businesses: interior, exterior, cabinets, decks and fences, plus the repairs that come first.

  1. 1

    Interior painting

    Walls, ceilings, trim and doors, with the sanding, filling and masking done before the first coat.

    • Walls and ceilings
    • Trim and doors
    • Repainting
  2. 2

    Exterior painting

    Power washing, caulking and a full repaint. Trim and doors $1,200 – $3,000; full exterior $3,500 – $12,000+. Estimates only.

    • Full exterior repaint: $3,500 – $12,000+
    • Trim & doors only: $1,200 – $3,000
    • Soffits, gutters, fascia: $800 – $2,500
  3. 3

    Cabinet painting and refinishing

    Cleaning, sanding, priming and spraying for a smooth finish on kitchen and bathroom cabinets. Prices are estimates.

    • Small kitchen (10–20 doors): $2,000 – $3,500
    • Medium kitchen (21–35 doors): $3,500 – $5,000
    • Large kitchen (36+ doors): $5,000 – $7,500+
    • Vanities/built-ins: $500 – $2,000+
  4. 4

    Deck and fence staining

    Staining and sealing for decks, fences and pergolas, and patio projects such as repainting and rescreening.

    • Decks, fences, pergolas: $500 – $5,000
    • Back patios
    • Power washing first
  5. 5

    Commercial painting

    Painting for businesses and larger jobs, including ceiling repair and repainting.

    • Business interiors
    • Large projects
    • Ceiling repair
  6. 6

    Repairs and prep

    Drywall repair and texturing, pressure washing, repainting and color consultation, so the paint goes on a sound surface.

    • Drywall repair and texturing
    • Pressure washing
    • Color consultation

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you give written quotes?

Yes. Eric visits or meets virtually, then sends a detailed quote. Book an estimate.

Do you prep the surface?

Yes. Sanding, filling gaps, caulking holes and power washing come before paint.

What do cabinets and exteriors cost?

Typical ranges from past projects are on the services page. They are estimates, and the final price comes after an estimate.

Which areas do you serve?

Round Rock, Austin, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Hutto, Buda, Cedar Park, Leander and Sun City.

Do you paint commercial buildings?

Yes. Commercial painting is on the service list, including ceiling repair and repainting.
