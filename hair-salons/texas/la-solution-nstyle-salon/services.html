Questions

Frequently asked

Where is the salon?

Suite 131 inside The Marquise Salon Suites, 7010 State Hwy 71, Unit 150, Austin, TX 78735, in Southwest Austin near Oak Hill and Circle C.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM, by appointment. Closed Sunday and Monday. Call (512) 888-5188 to book.

Do you take walk-ins?

Lea works by appointment only, so every client gets her full attention. Call ahead and she will find a time that works.

How long does a braid or loc appointment take?

It depends on the style, size and length. Big braid and loc styles can be a half-day appointment, so book ahead and Lea will plan the time with you.

Can you change my style without starting over?

Often, yes. Lea looks at the work you already have and suggests the approach that gets the look you want while keeping what is already done.

Do you braid kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids’ braids and cornrows, with beads if they want them. Parents say Lea is patient and keeps a long sit fun.

Will my braids be too tight?

Lea makes sure nothing is too tight anywhere. Clients say they have no pain or headache afterward.

Is the suite accessible?

Yes. The building has an accessible entrance and accessible parking near the door.
