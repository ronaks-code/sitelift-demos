About Fairlady

Fairlady is the home of color correction and balayage specialists in Pflugerville, with expert care for curly, thick or fine hair. We take the time to explain your hair type and its condition, ask the right questions, and never rush the cut.

Our story

In Pflugerville since 2006

Fairlady Salon has been doing hair in Pflugerville since 2006. Owner Vilma Lopez leads the team from 1202 FM 685, under the blue Fairlady sign. Inside it's black leather chairs, marble floors and glass mosaic tile, and clients describe it as serene, chic and spa-like from the moment they walk in.

We're the home of color correction and balayage specialists, with expert care for curly, thick or fine hair. Fairlady is a Redken salon, every stylist is Brazilian Blowout certified, and walk-ins are welcome. Our stylists explain your hair type and its condition, suggest what will suit you, and take their time so everything is done properly.
