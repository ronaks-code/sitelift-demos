Questions

Frequently asked

What happens at a hair and scalp analysis?

Dr. Sharon uses trichoscopy to look closely at your hair and scalp, and talks through your medical history, diet, medications and lifestyle. You get a written summary with recommendations and a treatment plan made for you.

Can you help with thinning hair or alopecia?

Many clients come in with thinning, bald spots, traction alopecia or an inflamed scalp. Results take time and consistency, and clients describe new growth within a few months of following their plan.

What is the holistic head spa?

A relaxing scalp treatment with detox washes, botanical steam, scalp massage and herbal rinses, on a head spa bed that also gives a full-body massage. Choose the Express, Signature or Ultimate experience.

How do I book?

Book online any time through the studio's Vagaro page, or call (512) 202-5787. Extensions and loc installs need a consultation first. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Thursday 10 AM to 2 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, by appointment. Closed Sunday, Monday and Friday.

Where is the studio?

701 FM 685, Suite 120, Pflugerville, TX 78660, with free parking. It is a short drive from Round Rock, Hutto, Wells Branch, Manor and north-east Austin.
