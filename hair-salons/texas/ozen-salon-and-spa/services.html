Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

We're by appointment only. Book online through Vagaro or call (512) 249-8900. If you need something soon, call: we'll do our best to get you in on short notice.

Where are you, and where do I park?

107 S Lewis St, Suite 140, in downtown Round Rock: the yellow building with cedar posts. There's street parking and a private lot.

What are your hours?

Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, Thursday 1 PM to 8 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 3 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.

Can you fix hair another salon damaged?

Often, yes. Many of our clients came to Kerem after a bad experience elsewhere. He'll look at your hair, make a plan and take big changes in stages so your hair stays healthy.

Do you do both hair and lash extensions?

Yes. Kerem installs and maintains hair extensions, and Kara does lash extension full sets and fills.

How can I pay?

We take credit cards and Apple Pay.
