Our story

Your neighborhood salon on East 3rd Street

Hair Jordan Salon is in Suite C of Jordan Square at 1400 E 3rd St, a stone's throw from the IU campus. Look for the teal sign with the gold scissors over the red brick, the pink neon in the window and the bench out front with the flower pots.

Sharma owns the salon and has run it for more than two decades; his "SHARMA The MAN" plate sits on the front counter, under the Audrey Hepburn prints. Julie does women's cuts and color (and brings in homemade cookies and muffins for clients), and clients have been coming back to her for years. Hannah is the one people mention for trims that make a grow-out look good.

Students, locals, kids and grown-ups all sit in the same chairs. Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are appreciated: book online any time or call the salon. There is free parking right in front for Jordan Square customers, plus bike parking, free Wi-Fi, and cards are accepted.
