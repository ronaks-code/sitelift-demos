About Lunar Hare

Lunar Hare is a cozy, plant-filled hair salon at 236 N Morton St in downtown Bloomington, opened by stylist and barber Jayme Carney in 2021. Cuts and custom curly cuts, custom color and balayage, straight-razor shaves, blowouts, updos and extensions, in a down-to-earth space that's open to everyone.

Our story

Jayme Carney's salon on N Morton St, since 2021

Lunar Hare is Jayme Carney's salon at 236 N Morton St in downtown Bloomington. She opened it in 2021 "with hopes of growing and here we are!" Jayme is a licensed barber and cosmetologist, trained in classic cuts and classic color at the TIGI Academy in Manhattan, Deva certified, with advanced curly-cut training in Chicago.

Inside it's warm and lived-in: stained concrete floors, vintage armchairs, a floral front counter, a teal sofa (usually with the salon dog on it), a pink cabinet and plants on every shelf. It's a Green Circle certified salon, and clients say it best: a down-to-earth, welcoming vibe, a stylist who listens, and a wash and head massage worth the trip on its own.
