Cuts and custom curly cuts, custom color and balayage, straight-razor shaves, blowouts, updos and extensions, in a down-to-earth space that's open to everyone.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I book at Lunar Hare?

Book online through our booking page any time, or call (812) 340-5052. We're at 236 N Morton St in downtown Bloomington.

What are your hours?

Wednesday 12 to 8 pm, Friday 10 am to 6 pm and Saturday 10 am to 4 pm. Closed Sunday, Monday, Tuesday and Thursday.

Do you cut curly hair?

Yes. Jayme is Deva certified, with advanced curly-cut training in Chicago. A custom curly cut starts at $75.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Child cuts (12 and younger) start at $35, and reviewers mention stylists gently coaxing a six-year-old through a haircut.

Do you do fades and shaves?

Yes. Jayme is a licensed barber too: short cuts, fades, beard trims, traditional straight-razor shaves and hot lather head shaves.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice. Cancellations within 24 hours are charged 50% of the appointment, and after three cancellations in a row we ask for a non-refundable deposit.
