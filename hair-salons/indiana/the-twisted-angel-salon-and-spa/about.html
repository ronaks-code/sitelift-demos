About The Twisted Angel

The Twisted Angel Salon & Spa is a full-service salon and spa at 4653 W Richland Plaza Dr in Bloomington, owned by licensed aesthetician Lauren Mobley: color and vivids, extensions, cuts and styling, facials and skin treatments, brows, lashes, waxing, nails and spray tans, from a team clients say feels like friends.

Our story

California luxury, in the middle of Indiana

The Twisted Angel Salon & Spa is owned by Lauren Mobley, a licensed aesthetician, and sits in Richland Plaza on Bloomington's west side. Inside it's black walls, glowing LED mirrors, pampas grass, disco balls and a longhorn skull over the shampoo bowls. In the salon's own words: California luxury, but in the middle of Indiana.

The neon over the wash station says it best: a touch of heaven, with a hint of rebellion. On the hair side, clients come in for all-over color, rainbow vivids, icy blondes and extensions up to 30 inches. On the spa side, Lauren's menu runs from scent facials (Toasted Marshmallow, Pumpkin Spice Latte, Twisted Peppermint) to dermaplaning, peels, microneedling, brows, lashes and spray tans.

What reviewers mention most is the people. They talk about a team that treats you like a friend, catching up while you're in the chair, and stylists like Chloe, Taylor and Lauren who make a first visit feel like coming home.
