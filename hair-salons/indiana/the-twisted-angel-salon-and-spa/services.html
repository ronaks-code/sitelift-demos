Questions

Frequently asked

How do I book at The Twisted Angel?

Book online any time through the salon's booking page, or call or text (812) 508-9446. The salon is appointment only.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday and Sunday 9 AM to 6 PM, by appointment.

Where is the salon?

4653 W Richland Plaza Dr, Bloomington, IN 47404, in Richland Plaza on the west side of Bloomington, an easy drive from Ellettsville and Spencer.

Do you do vivid and fashion colors?

Yes. From rainbow root melts to icy silver blondes, bold color is a big part of what the team does. Bring a photo of what you have in mind.

What kind of extensions do you offer?

Tape-in, sew-in, and I-tip or K-tip extensions, plus move-ups and removals. Book an extension consultation first if you're not sure which method suits your hair.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations inside 48 hours are charged 50% of the service, and a card on file is needed to book.
