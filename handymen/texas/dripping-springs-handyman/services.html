Questions people ask

Will you come out to my property?

Yes. We drive out to properties in unincorporated areas near Dripping Springs. Call to check your address.

What kind of work do you do?

Decks, painting, staining, porch and stair railings, masonry and custom carpentry, plus general repairs.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday 9am to 7pm, Sunday 9am to 6pm.

Can I send photos of the job?

Yes. Use the form on the contact page and add what you can describe. Photos help.

Every job is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a request and we will talk through the work.
