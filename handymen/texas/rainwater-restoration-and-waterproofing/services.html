Leak detection and repair

Wind-driven water can travel sideways before it shows. We find the entry point, tell you what we recommend and photograph the fix.

  • Find the entry point
  • Photos of the source
  • Photos of the finished work
Windows and mortar

Caulking and sealing windows and mortar cracks that let water into the walls.

  • Window caulking
  • Mortar cracks
  • Stucco and walls
Roofs and chimneys

Sealing exposed fasteners, boots, vents and flashing, replacing windblown shingles and repairing chimneys before the next storm.

  • Roof inspection and repair
  • Windblown shingles
  • Chimney repair
