About

A construction family's company

Eric grew up in Corpus Christi around his father's construction business, then started Ryse Construction & Handyman in 2020.

Based in Schertz, the team works across San Antonio, New Braunfels, Seguin and the Hill Country. Uniformed crews, background checks, a 5-year warranty.
