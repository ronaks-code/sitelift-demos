Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 6 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 8 AM to 5 PM and Sunday 9 AM to 5 PM. Holiday hours can differ, so call first.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes. For urgent jobs Ryse starts as soon as it can, often the same day.

Is there a warranty?

The company backs its work with a 5-year warranty. Ask for the details when you get your estimate.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates on home repair and handyman jobs are free.

Who will be at my house?

A uniformed crew. Ryse requires background checks and drug screenings for its team members.

Where do you work?

Schertz, San Antonio, Canyon Lake, New Braunfels, Seguin, Boerne, Bandera, Bulverde, Helotes and the surrounding areas. Call to confirm your address.

Do you offer discounts?

Ryse offers discounts for military personnel, first responders, nurses, teachers and seniors.
