Preguntas Good to know

Before you pull in

Do I have to get out of the car?

Nope. Pull into the lot and a server comes to your window with the menu, takes your order and brings it out. Prefer to sit? Grab a picnic table under the umbrellas.

What are your hours?

11 AM to midnight Sunday, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday, and 11 AM to 1 AM Friday and Saturday. We're closed on Tuesdays.

Can I call ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call (830) 312-9395 and your order will be ready when you pull in. You can also place a pickup order online and pay ahead.

What comes with the mini tacos?

They come four to an order with avocado, cheese, or both, plus cilantro, raw onions, lime, grilled onions and grilled jalapeños on the side.

What is the Mi Rancho Charolazo?

Our big tray: 30 mini tacos with all the fixings on the side. It's the move for a group, a party or a river day with the whole crew.

Do you have anything meatless?

Veggie mini tacos, bean & cheese and potato & egg breakfast tacos, elotes, mangonadas, fresas con crema and nine flavors of aguas frescas.

When can I get a burger?

The hamburgesa menu runs 11 AM to 3 PM.
