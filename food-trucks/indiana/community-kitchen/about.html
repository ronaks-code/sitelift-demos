Our story

Feeding Monroe County

A nonprofit working to end hunger in Monroe County through direct service, education and advocacy. Our main kitchen is at 1515 S Rogers St.

Meals are for everyone. We don't ask for proof of need or identification, and we protect the anonymity of the people we serve.

We lean on volunteers of all ages from 10 up, and we run Backpack Buddies to feed children and families.

  • Anyone is welcomeNo fees, no eligibility requirements, no questions asked.
  • Dignity firstRespect for everyone who comes in.
  • Made with careWarm, balanced meals, with thoughtful options for different dietary needs.
