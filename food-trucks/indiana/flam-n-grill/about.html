Good to know

Questions

Where is the truck today?

Our address is 303 E Kirkwood Ave near the IU campus, and when we're not at an event we're usually parked near College Mall (2748 E 3rd St). We also roll out to Food Truck Friday and events around town, so the quickest way to know is to call (812) 803-8162 before you head over.

Is the meat halal?

Yes. Every item on our menu is halal, from the chicken and lamb over rice to the Philly cheesesteak and the chopped cheese.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call (812) 803-8162 with your order and a pickup time, or use the order page to send it ahead.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. The falafel over rice, the falafel sandwich, the fries and the onion rings are all meat-free, and the hot sauce and white sauce go on anything.

Can you customize my order?

Absolutely. Extra white sauce, no lettuce, mild instead of spicy: just tell us at the window and we'll build it your way.

Do you cater or come to events?

We bring the truck to events around Bloomington, including Food Truck Friday. Send a catering request with your date, headcount and spot, and we'll get back to you.
