What we do

Services

Leticia Braiding Hair is a braiding studio in Suite 104A at 600 W 28th St, in West Campus by UT Austin. Clients send an inspiration photo, sit back with a snack and the TV on, and leave with exactly the style they asked for, often the same day they called.

01

Knotless & box braids

Small, medium and large knotless or classic box braids with neat, even parts.

→02

Boho & goddess braids

Braids with soft curly pieces left loose, in black, honey, copper or blonde.

→03

Feed-ins, Fulani & lemonade

Sleek feed-in braids, Fulani patterns and side-swept lemonade braids.

→04

Cornrows & kids braids

Straight-backs and curved cornrow designs, for grown-ups and kids.

→05

Twists, crochet & faux locs

Two-strand, passion and Senegalese twists, crochet styles and faux locs.

→06

Locs & sew-ins

Loc retwists and maintenance, starter locs, loc extensions and sew-ins.
