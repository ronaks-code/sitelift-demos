Hair Braiding And Weaves

About the studio

Hair Braiding And Weaves is a small braids and weaves studio at 1600 E Riverside Dr in southeast Austin. Clients come in for knotless braids, stitch and feed-in cornrows, twists, loc styles, sew-ins and u-part wig installs, and leave with a look that blends right in with their own hair.

Our story

Braids and weaves on East Riverside

Hair Braiding And Weaves is a braids and weaves studio at 1600 E Riverside Dr in southeast Austin, open seven days a week from 8 in the morning.

The work speaks for itself: curved stitch cornrows, long knotless and boho braids, twists, crisscross loc styles and copper sew-ins. Clients say the stylist is attentive, takes her time to understand exactly what you want, and the final look comes out even better than they imagined.
