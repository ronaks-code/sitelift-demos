About ClarSim

ClarSim Hair Braiding is Clarisse's braiding studio in Suite A144 at 7801 N Lamar Blvd in North Austin. She does knotless and box braids, boho braids, Senegalese and kinky twists, Fulani and lemonade braids, cornrows and sew-in extensions, for women, men and kids, and clients leave talking about how neat the work is and how comfortable they felt the whole time.

Our story

Clarisse, braiding on North Lamar

ClarSim Hair Braiding is Clarisse's chair in Suite A144 at 7801 N Lamar Blvd, inside the Sadi's Beauty Works salon in North Austin, with parking right out front. Inside it is calm and bright: black chairs, framed mirrors and cream walls.

Clarisse has more than 20 years in the beauty industry. She braids for women, men and children, from knotless and box braids to twists, Fulani and lemonade braids and cornrows, and she also does sew-in extensions for extra length and volume.

What clients say most is that the work is neat and the pace is right. She doesn't rush, pays close attention to every part, and still gets you done in good time. Clients come back, and they bring their kids.

Meet your braider

The hands behind ClarSim
