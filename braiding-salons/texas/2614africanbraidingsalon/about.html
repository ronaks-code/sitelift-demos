About 2614

Boho knotless and regular knotless, box and micro braids, French curl, Senegalese, passion and island twists, soft locs, stitch braids, cornrows and loc retwists, done in our private suite off I-35 in Round Rock. Braiding hair is included on regular knotless, and every style is booked through Booksy.

Our story

A bright little suite for big, beautiful braids

2614 African Braiding Salon is a bright private suite inside the salon suites on the I-35 frontage road in Round Rock: round black mirrors, a hooded dryer, and a wall of colorful butterflies, many with a client's name written on them, that you will want a picture in front of. Boho knotless braids are what we are known for, in every color from burgundy and honey to copper and mint.

Clients ask for Indira and Fancy by name, and some drive up to two hours each way because the parts are that clean and the braids come out exactly the way they asked. Braiding hair is included on regular knotless, and for boho you can bring your curly hair or buy it from us. Book on Booksy or call, and follow the latest installs on Instagram and TikTok.
