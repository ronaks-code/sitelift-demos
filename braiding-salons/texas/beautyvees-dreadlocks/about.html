About Vee

Beautyvee’s Dreadlock’s is Vee's private loc studio at 2000 Ferguson Ln in northeast Austin. She makes human-hair and synthetic dreadlock extensions by hand, in your choice of color, size and length, and installs them permanent or temporary. Her most popular service is a retwist, style and color, and she also does detox, takedowns and removal, faux locs and yarn wraps.

Our story

Handmade locs from a private studio in northeast Austin

Beautyvee’s Dreadlock’s is Vee's private loc studio in Suite A at 2000 Ferguson Lane in northeast Austin: warm wood floors, window light, a couch, and one chair where your locs get her full attention. No crowd, by appointment.

In her words: “Our business specializes in making handmade human and synthetic dreadlock extensions that you can get installed by us as well. We offer a wide selection of colors to choose from including size and length.”

Clients call her the “dread doctor.” Her most popular service is a retwist, style and color, and she also does detox, takedowns, faux locs and yarn wraps, from fire red and copper tips to a full rainbow.

What reviewers mention most: a clean, well set-up space, appointments that run right on schedule, impeccable work, and walking out feeling like their hair is healthy, styled and new.
