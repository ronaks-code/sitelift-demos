About Salima

Salima African Hair Braiding & Beauty Cuts is Awa's braiding salon in suite 106 at 1100 Grand Ave Pkwy in Pflugerville. Her team does knotless and box braids, microbraids, Senegalese, kinky and passion twists, cornrows, locs, crochet and weave extensions, with a wash and braiding hair right in the shop.

Our story

Awa's salon, where clients stay for years

Salima African Hair Braiding & Beauty Cuts sits in suite 106 of the stone-front strip center at 1100 Grand Ave Pkwy in Pflugerville, under the green metal roof. Inside: taupe walls, cherry-wood stations, tall mirrors, a row of shampoo bowls and a shelf of braiding hair, so you can pick the right hair on the spot.

The salon is run by its owner, Awa. Clients say she is spot on when you show her a picture, and that her braiders are kind, respectful and careful with edges. Some have been coming back for more than 10 and even 16 years, and plenty drive in from Austin and out of town.
