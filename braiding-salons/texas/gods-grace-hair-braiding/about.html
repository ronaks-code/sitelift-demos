Our story

More than 8 years of braiding

God's Grace Hair Braiding is a braiding salon at 612 E University Ave #6 in Georgetown, with more than 8 years of braiding behind it. The motto is right there on the logo: fast, neat and cute.

Clients keep saying the same things: the braiders listen to what you ask for, they work quick without rushing, and they're gentle. They braid to your hair's density instead of piling on extra hair, so braids come out light and don't pull. And the shop is friendly, with none of the mean-girl energy some braiding salons are known for.

Every kind of African braiding is on the menu, from knotless and Fulani to twists, locs and kids' beads, and the salon also teaches hands-on braiding classes for anyone who wants to learn.
