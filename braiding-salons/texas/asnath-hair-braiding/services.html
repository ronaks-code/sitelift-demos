Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins and same-day appointments are welcome. Customers walk in and get seated right away, or call or text (737) 395-1114 in the morning to get fit in the same day.

What are your hours?

Open every day from 6 AM to 9 PM. Holiday hours can change, so call (737) 395-1114 if you are not sure.

How much do braids cost?

Starting prices: cornrows from $60, box braids from $130, knotless and jumbo braids from $140, micro braids and Senegalese twists from $150. The final price depends on size, length and the hair used, so call, text or email for an exact quote.

How long do braids take?

It depends on the style and size. Reviewers say cornrows took about an hour, natural twists on thick hair took under 2 hours, and small braids took about 4 hours.

Do you braid men's and kids' hair?

Yes. Cornrows and designs for men, and knotless braids and cornrows for kids. Parents often get done alongside their kids.

How do I book?

Book online on Booksy, call or text (737) 395-1114, email asnathbraidinghair@gmail.com, or just walk in. Bring a photo of the style you want and say exactly what you need.

Where are you?

2204 W Anderson Ln, Suite 4, Austin, TX 78757, in North Austin near Burnet Road. An easy drive from Round Rock, Pflugerville, Cedar Park and Leander.
