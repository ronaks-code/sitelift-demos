Our story

Meet Elisabeth “Mommy” Ekanga

Queen Hair Braiding is run by Elisabeth Ekanga, the woman every client calls Mommy. She has been braiding African hair for more than 30 years, and today she runs this Black-owned shop at 6448 US-290 in northeast Austin with a team of braiders. If you are not sure what to get, she is the one who will help you choose.

The gospel music is always on and the ladies keep the room laughing. Some of our clients have been coming for more than 15 years, and some still drive up from San Antonio to sit in our chairs. As one longtime client put it: they are not just my braiders, they are family.
