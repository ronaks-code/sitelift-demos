Questions

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is open. If you want a set time with a specific barber, book online or call (512) 351-9703.

Do you take cards?

No, Wyatt's is cash only. There is an ATM in the shop if you forget.

What comes with a haircut?

Every haircut, fades and bald fades included, is finished with a straight razor shave on the back of the neck. Plan on about 30 minutes.

Where do I park?

The shop has its own parking lot right out front at 3401 E 7th St, with easy access off E 7th St.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8 AM to 8 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 1 PM. Closed Sunday.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Parents bring their kids in, from little ones to teens, and our barbers take the time to get it right.
