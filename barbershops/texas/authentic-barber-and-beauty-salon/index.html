Lo que hacemos · What we do

Barber on one side. Beauty on the other.

Authentic Barber & Beauty Salon is a family-friendly shop on Caldwell Lane in Del Valle: black walls, gold mirrors, the game on the TV, and stylists who take their time. Bring a photo of what you want and they will bring it to life.

Haircuts & fades

Regular cuts, tapers and clean fades. Show a photo of what you want and they bring it to life.

Kids' cuts

A relaxed, family-friendly chair for the little ones, from curls to clean lineups.

Beard & lineup

Beard trims, razor-sharp edges and a clean lineup to finish the cut, never rushed.

Color & highlights

Color, highlights and soft blonde balayage, all the way to electric blue and custom designs. Color work as good as the cuts.

Hair extensions

Extensions blended in for length and volume.

Lash extensions

Full, natural-looking eyelash extensions.
