Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome. On busy days you can call (512) 448-1024 to check the wait. One reviewer called just after 8 AM on a Saturday, walked in 10 minutes later and sat down almost right away.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 6 PM, and Sunday 8 AM to 3 PM. Holiday hours can differ, so call ahead on holidays.

Where is the shop?

4315 S 1st St, Austin, TX 78745, in the limestone strip center. Look for the barber pole on the window. Parking is right out front.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids are welcome, the shop has fun kids capes, and parents say the barbers keep the little ones talking and laughing. One mom wrote that her son with special needs was welcomed with open arms.

Do you do color?

Yes. Hot pink, platinum, blonde and more. Bold color is one of the things the shop is known for.

Do you take cards?

Yes. Cash and credit cards are both accepted.
