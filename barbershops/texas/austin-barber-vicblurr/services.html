Questions

Frequently asked

How do I book with Victor?

Book online through Victor's Booksy page, or call (512) 797-2705.

How much is a haircut?

A haircut is $45 and a haircut + beard is $60. A Precision Shear Cut (P.S.C) is $65, or $80 with a beard. Prices are as listed on Booksy.

Haircut or P.S.C, which should I book?

Book a Haircut to keep your current cut clean: a fresh fade, cleanup and light shear work. Book the P.S.C if you want reshaping, length taken off or detailed scissor work, especially if it has been 4+ weeks.

What are the hours?

Monday 12 PM to 5 PM, Tuesday to Friday 10 AM to 7 PM and Saturday 10 AM to 5 PM. Closed Sunday. Holiday hours can differ, so check Booksy.

Where is the shop?

2426 Webberville Rd, Unit A, Austin, TX 78702, in East Austin. Look for the red door with the barber pole beside it. Parking is available.

Do you take cards?

Yes, credit cards are accepted. Booksy also offers packages if you want to buy cuts in bulk.
