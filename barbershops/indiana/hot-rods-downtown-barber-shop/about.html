About Hot Rod's

Haircuts, fades, beard trims and straight-razor shaves at 220 N Walnut St in downtown Bloomington. Friendly barbers who take their time, a shop full of antiques, and a fair price for a great cut.

Our story

Hot Rod's on Walnut since 2014

There has been a barbershop at 220 N Walnut St for a long time. Grigsby & McAdams cut hair here for about 25 years, then Hyscel Ward took over in 1980 and ran it as Ward's Downtown Barber Shop.

In July 2014 master barber Rodney "Hot Rod" Pheifer, who already ran Hot Rod's Barber Shop in Monrovia, stopped in and bought the place two days later. He renamed it Hot Rod's Downtown Barbershop and kept everything that made it special.

Inside it's red chairs, white porcelain sinks and walls covered in hubcaps, old Indiana license plates, straight razors and IU basketball history. Part barbershop, part hot rod museum.

The barbers are friendly, take their time and make sure the cut is right before you get up. IU students, downtown regulars and families with kids all end up in the same row of chairs.
