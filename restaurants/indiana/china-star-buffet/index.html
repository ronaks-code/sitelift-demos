CUSTOMER NOTESChina Star Buffet

I love this place and have been eating their food for almost two decades now. It was my first introduction to Chinese food. While classified as Chinese American, it is still great food. It has lots of options with some occasional differences between lunch and dinner. They are quick to refill the buffet as well. Service with waiters can be a bit hit or miss but for the most part it is a very solid place to eat.

Teylor C.★★★★☆

By far, one of the best Chinese buffets in the area normally I go there for lunchtime however I went there This evening, near closing time they did not rush me. They were very polite. I did the takeout buffet for a reasonable price and the food was delicious andstill hot.

The restaurant was busy with dine-ins, to-go orders, and call-in orders but the food was fresh and tasty. There are five buffet bars, including ice cream. Great price for everything you get. One of the only China buffets that I know is open during the pandemic and plastic gloves for the buffet.
