Our Story

Layali means “nights” in Arabic. It’s the long, generous evenings around a shared platter that we cook for.

Who we are من نحن

Home cooking, made to share

Layali Feast is a locally owned, 100% halal kitchen in downtown Bloomington, cooking homemade Middle Eastern food from family recipes. The menu brings together the rice-and-meat feasts of Saudi Arabia and Yemen, Levantine mezze and Lebanese sweets.

Guests say it best: “It reminds me of my mom’s cooking.” Big portions, fair prices, and a staff happy to explain every dish if you’re new to it.

How we got here رحلتنا

From ghost kitchen to Grant Street

2025

A late-night start

Layali Feast began as a ghost kitchen, cooking in a borrowed downtown kitchen after closing time for Bloomington to order in.

DECEMBER 2025

A room of our own

Grand opening inside the Coffee Beanery at 228 S Grant St: coffee by day, a feast house by night.

TODAY

4.8 stars, 171 reviews

Students, families and late-night regulars, and a dessert case that keeps growing: kunafa, Layali Lubnan and the Dubai chocolate cup.
