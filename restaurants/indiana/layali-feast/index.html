Good to know أسئلة

Questions, answered

Where exactly are you?
Inside the Coffee Beanery at 228 S Grant St, Bloomington, IN 47408, downtown near the IU campus. Look for the Layali Feast sign behind the counter.
Can I eat in at lunch?
Lunch is takeout: order online or at the counter and we’ll have it ready. Dine-in starts at 5 PM, once the Coffee Beanery closes for the day, and runs until close.
Should I order ahead?
On busy nights, yes. Order online or call (812) 803-0180 and your food will be ready when you get here.
How late are you open?
Until midnight Tuesday through Thursday and until 1 AM Friday through Sunday. Closed Mondays.
Is the food halal?
Yes, 100% halal. Saudi and Yemeni rice plates, Levantine mezze and Lebanese desserts, all halal.
Do you have meatless dishes?
Yes: hummus, baba ghanoush, fatoush, tabbouleh, the falafel sandwich and roasted veggies. Some dishes contain peanuts, tree nuts, milk, eggs, wheat, soy or sesame, so please ask us about allergies.
What’s the difference between mandi and kabsa?
Both are rice-and-meat feast plates. Mandi is the Yemeni classic with fragrant yellow rice; kabsa is the Saudi favorite with rice cooked red-gold in tomato and warm spices. Can’t decide? Ask us, we love this question.
