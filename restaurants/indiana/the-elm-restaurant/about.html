Our story

Named for Elm Heights

The Elm belongs to Martha and David Moore, longtime Bloomington residents. They opened it as a gathering place for community and culinary adventure.

The name honors the Elm Heights neighborhood. Inside: a clean, modern room, an open kitchen, a bar worth sitting at and attentive service.

  • SeasonalThe dinner menu changes with the seasons.
  • HospitalityAttentive servers and bartenders.
  • For every occasionCoffee at the counter, after-work cocktails or an anniversary dinner.
