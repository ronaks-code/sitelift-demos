Good to know

Questions folks ask

When is the buffet on?

Tuesday through Sunday. The pizza and breadstick buffet runs 11am to 2pm and again 5pm to 8pm, and the salad and soup bar runs 11am to 8pm. Pizza buffet $11, salad and soup bar $10, or both for $14 with your drink. Buffets are dine-in only.

Are you open on Mondays?

No, we're closed Mondays. Tuesday through Sunday we're open 11am to 9pm for the dining room, carryout and the drive-thru.

Is there a drive-thru?

Yes. Call ahead at (812) 876-2279 and pick it up at the window, hot and ready.

Do kids eat free?

Kids 3 and under eat the buffet free with a paid adult. Ages 4 to 10 are $7.50, and that includes a drink.

Do you have gluten-free crust?

Yes, a cauliflower gluten-free crust in the 10" size for $4.25 more. Our kitchen is full of flour, though, so we can't guarantee anything is gluten free.

Is it just pizza?

Not even close. Strombolis and subs, wings, our famous fried mushrooms, breadsticks, spaghetti, a shrimp dinner and the salad and soup bar.

What payment do you take?

Cash, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cards carry a 3.99% service charge, so cash saves you a little. Gift cards are available too.

Where are you?

5690 W State Rd 46, Bloomington, IN 47404, on State Road 46 at the edge of Ellettsville, a short drive west of Bloomington.
