Tradition since 1971

Our original pizza

“Creating our original pizza since 1971.”

That's been the deal for over fifty years. The dough is made in house and hand rolled every day, the crust comes out thin and crispy, and regulars will tell you the pizza has tasted the same for decades, in a good way.

Folks come in for the pizza buffet, the salad and soup bar, our famous fried mushrooms and a stromboli with bread and butter pickles, and they come back for the people who greet them at the door. Grab a seat, swing through the drive-thru, or carry it home.
