常見問題Good to know

Questions

Do you take cards?

At the counter we take cash or Zelle. Please have one ready when you pick up. Call (812) 333-5262 if you have questions about paying.

How fast is pickup?

Call (812) 333-5262 with your order. Regulars say even big orders are usually ready in about 20 minutes.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Lots. Fried tofu with bok choy, three cup tofu, mala potatoes, bok choy with garlic, tomatoes and egg, salt and pepper eggplant, curry vegetables, vegetable lo mein and a whole list of stir-fried greens. Mapo tofu can be made without the meat on request.

Are you open in the summer?

Our regular hours are Tuesday to Sunday, closed Monday. Some summers, while IU is out, we cut back to weekends only, so call (812) 333-5262 to double-check before you come.

Can I eat in?

Yes. Order at the counter and grab a table in our dining room. Most people take it to go, and the portions are big enough for leftovers.

Is it Sichuan or American Chinese?

Both. You'll find the takeout classics like General Tso's, orange chicken and lo mein, plus Sichuan dishes like mapo tofu, mala potatoes and beef in spicy Szechuan hot sauce.
