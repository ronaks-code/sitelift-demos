Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Lots. Grilled eggplant, bolani, baba ganouj, red lentil soup, the vegetarian special, namouna and our combination pasta are all meat-free, and there are vegan options too. Just ask your server.

When is the lunch buffet?

Thursday and Friday, 11 AM to 2 PM. Dinner is served Monday to Saturday, 5 to 9 PM. We're closed Sunday.

Is the bread really free?

It is. Dinner comes with a basket of fresh bread and our grilled eggplant with carrots, plus your choice of red lentil soup or salad.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Request a table online or call (812) 331-3761. Same-day tables and big groups are easiest by phone.

Can I get takeout?

Yes. Call (812) 331-3761 to order and pick it up on 6th Street.

Can we sit outside?

In good weather, yes: there are tables on the sidewalk under our awning and string lights on 6th Street.

Where are you, exactly?

100 W 6th St in downtown Bloomington, on the Courthouse Square and a short walk from IU's campus. Look for the red SAMIRA sign.

Do you cater?

Ask us about catering for your office, department or party. Call (812) 331-3761 or send a request from our reservations page.
