Questions

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Sunday, 11:30 AM to 10 PM. We're closed on Mondays. Holiday hours can differ, so give us a call if you're not sure.

Do you have a lunch special?

Yes. Tuesday through Friday until 3 PM: bibimbap, rice bowls, noodles, soups and dosirak lunch boxes at lunch prices, plus any two sushi bar rolls for $12.99.

Where are you, and is it near IU?

We're at 409 E 4th St, on the East 4th Street restaurant row a few blocks from the Indiana University campus and downtown Bloomington. Look for the lit KOREA sign and the porch with the black iron railings.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Bibimbap, fried rice and most noodles can be made with tofu, and there are vegetable and avocado rolls at lunch. Please tell us you're vegetarian when you order, because some banchan (like fish cakes and kimchi) contain seafood.

Can I get takeout or reserve a table?

Both. Call (812) 339-2735 with your order and a pickup time, or send it from our order page. For a big group, call ahead and we'll get tables ready.

Can we sit outside?

Yes. When the weather is nice we have tables out on the front patio, with string lights in the evening.

Is this a good place to try Korean food for the first time?

Definitely. Start with the hot stone bibimbap, a kimchi pancake for the table or the bulgogi, and we're happy to walk you through the menu and the banchan.
