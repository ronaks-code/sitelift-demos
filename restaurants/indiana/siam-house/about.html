About us

A Thai house on 4th Street

Our story

Come in, sit anywhere

Our home is one of the converted houses on East 4th Street, at the far end of the restaurant row. There are several dining rooms, some with walls of windows, filled with Thai decor, chandeliers and brocade. In the warmer months there's a patio and a big open bay-window porch.

The food is what brings people back: flavourful, cooked well and generously portioned, on our blue-and-white porcelain. Choose your protein and your heat, from Mild to Thai Hot.

Good to know

Questions

How spicy is spicy?

Every dish can be made Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot or Thai Hot. Guests tell us our heat runs a little higher than other Thai places in town, so if you're unsure, start one level lower. Dishes ordered hot can't be refunded, so ask us if you'd like a recommendation.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Most dishes, including the Chef's Specials, can be made with tofu or vegetables instead of chicken or pork, and starters like the Golden Tofu, Vegetable Thai Dumplings and Vegetable Tempura are meat-free.

Are you open between lunch and dinner?

Monday to Friday we close between 3 PM and 4:30 PM. Saturday (12-10 PM) and Sunday (12-9 PM) we're open straight through.

Can I sit outside?

We're in one of the converted houses on 4th Street, with several dining rooms, a big open bay-window porch, and a patio in the warmer months.

Do you do takeout and delivery?

Yes. Call (812) 331-1233 for pickup, or order through the delivery apps.

How far are you from IU and the square?

A short walk. We're at the end of the restaurant row on East 4th Street, between the downtown square and the Indiana University campus.
