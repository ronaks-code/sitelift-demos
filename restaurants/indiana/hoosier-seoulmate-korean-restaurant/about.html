Meet the owners 사장님

Hi, we're Myung Sook and Jong Hyo Jung!

We moved to the United States in 2000, hoping to create more opportunities for our children. After operating a Korean restaurant in Fort Wayne, we moved to Bloomington and opened Hoosier Seoulmate in 2020. Over the years, Indiana has become home to our family, and now our restaurant.

At Hoosier Seoulmate, we serve Korean recipes that have been in our family for generations. It's the same food we cook and share around our own dining table, made with care and shared with the Hoosier community that has supported us.

Come enjoy a meal and take a photo by our “Let's Be Seoulmates” neon sign. Whether you're new to Korean food or grew up eating it, we hope you'll feel right at home here.
