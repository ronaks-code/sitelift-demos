From taqueria to Acapulco

Bloomington knew us as Taqueria Acapulco. Now we're Acapulco Mexican Restaurant & Bar, at 254 N Walnut St.

Same food, bigger home

The move downtown

When the old spot closed, a lot of people worried. It was just a move. The family-owned kitchen, the birria, the tacos and the vegetarian options our regulars came for made the trip downtown, and the new place added a full bar, more seats and nights that run until 2 AM.
