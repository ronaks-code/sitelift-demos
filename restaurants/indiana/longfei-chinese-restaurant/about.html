龍Our story

The red sign, the porch lights, the food

Longfei (龍飛, “flying dragon”) is a cozy restaurant in a converted house at 113 S Grant St, downtown, about a ten minute walk from the Indiana Memorial Union. You'll know it by the glowing red characters over the porch and the string lights along the deck.

The menu travels all over China: Sichuan mapo tofu and fish in chili oil, Hunan stir-fries, Shanghai wontons, Nanjing salted duck, Northeastern pickled cabbage pots, Xinjiang big plate chicken, and the iron hot pots people drive back for. There's American Chinese too, like orange chicken, General Tso's and crab rangoon, plus a full bubble tea menu.

Our owners and servers speak Chinese and English. A lot of our regulars are students a long way from home, and we like it when a meal here tastes like one.

问FAQ

Good to know

How far is Longfei from IU campus?

We're at 113 S Grant St, downtown, about a 10 minute walk from the Indiana Memorial Union.

Do you speak Chinese?

Yes. Our owners and servers speak Chinese and English, so order however you're most comfortable. 我们说中文。

Is the food spicy?

Plenty of it is, like the mapo tofu, the iron hot pots, the 冒菜 spicy pots and the 3 pepper cilantro chicken. Look for the red chili tag on the menu, and ask if you'd like a dish milder.

Do you have American Chinese food too?

Yes. Orange chicken, General Tso's, sesame chicken, crab rangoon, lo mein, fried rice and pad thai are all on the menu.

Are there vegetarian dishes?

Yes. There's a vegetarian plates section (including a vegetarian mapo tofu), a vegetable section, a vegetable spicy pot and vegetable fried rice or lo mein.

Can I order online or get delivery?

Yes. Order online for pickup, call (812) 219-0688, or get delivery through Fantuan (饭团外卖) and HungryPanda (熊猫外卖).

Is there room for a group?

Yes, there are plenty of seats, and the iron hot pots are made for sharing. For a big group, call ahead and we'll get tables ready.

What are your hours?

Thursday to Tuesday, 11 AM to 9:30 PM. We're closed on Wednesdays. Holiday hours can differ, so call if you're not sure.
