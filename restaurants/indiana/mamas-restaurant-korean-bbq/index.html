자주 묻는 질문Questions

Good to know before you come

Do I have to cook my own food?

Only if you want to. Grill tables are for cooking meat yourself, right at your table. Everything else on the menu, from bibimbap and japchae to the stews, comes out of Mama's kitchen ready to eat.

Is banchan included?

Yes. A spread of small side dishes comes with your meal, the traditional way.

Can I get takeout or delivery?

Yes. Call (812) 333-8071 for pickup, or order delivery through Grubhub.

Where do I park?

Right out front. There's a big lot, so no downtown parking hunt.

Do you serve Korean beer and soju?

Yes: Cass, Terra and soju, which go very well with BBQ.

Can I reserve a grill table?

Yes. Call (812) 333-8071 or send a request online, especially for bigger groups on Friday and Saturday nights.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: vegetable soft tofu soup, japchae with tofu, steamed edamame and more. Ask your server what can be made without meat.
