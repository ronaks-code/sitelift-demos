Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you really serve breakfast all day?

Yes. Breakfast is on from the minute we open at 6 AM until we close at 2 PM, along with sandwiches, salads, quesadillas and Greek favorites like the gyro pita plate and the Athenian skillet.

What are your hours?

Wednesday through Monday, 6 AM to 2 PM. We're closed on Tuesdays.

Did you move?

We did. Cozy Table is now at 3870 W 3rd St on Bloomington's west side, toward the back of the shopping center. If an older listing sends you to 2500 W 3rd St or the old 339-5900 number, our new home is 3870 W 3rd St and our number is (812) 322-9958.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Plenty: the vegetarian omelette, the Spartan and spinach-and-feta omelettes, the vegetable skillet, crepes, waffles, pancakes, the veggie quesadilla and our salads.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes, for kids 12 and under: Mickey Mouse pancakes, silver dollar cakes, a one-egg combo, French toast and egg, chicken strips, a burger or a grilled cheese.

Can I call in a to-go order?

Sure. Call (812) 322-9958 and we'll have it ready for pickup, or send a request from our order page and we'll call to confirm.
