Good to know 常見問題

Questions, answered.

When is the lunch buffet?

Weekday lunch, 11 AM to 3 PM. It's $12.95 plus tax, all you can eat, and the dishes change every day. We're closed on Mondays, so call ahead if you're not sure.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Sunday, 11 AM to 10 PM. We're closed on Mondays. Holiday hours can change, so give us a call if you're not sure.

Is there a lunch menu?

Yes. Lunch portions of the American-Chinese dishes are served until 3:30 PM, and dinner portions are available all day.

Where do I park?

There's free parking behind the restaurant. We're at 216 S Indiana Ave, a short walk from the IU campus.

Do you do takeout and delivery?

Yes. Call us for pickup, or order delivery through DoorDash, Delivery.com or HungryPanda.

Which dishes are spicy?

Anything printed in red on our menu is spicy, and it's marked with a chili on this site. Ask us to turn the heat up or down when you order.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes. There's a whole vegetables and tofu section, vegetable and tofu versions of the noodles, fried rice and rice bowls, and a steamed special diet menu with sauce on the side. Some soups use a meat broth (like the tofu vegetable pho), so tell us what you need and we'll steer you right.

Can I bring a group?

That's what the round tables are for. Every big table has a lazy Susan in the middle so everyone can share. Call ahead for parties of 8 or more.
