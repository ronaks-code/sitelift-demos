Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is K-bap, and where do I park?

We're at 340 S Walnut St, Suite 4, in downtown Bloomington. Look for the green CAFE sign and the patio umbrellas. There's plenty of street parking nearby.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Tofu and vegetable bibimbap (regular or in a hot stone bowl), vegetable fried rice and edamame. Ask us and we'll point you to more.

What is banchan?

Banchan are the little Korean side dishes that come to the table, like kimchi, pickled radish, fish cake and seasoned vegetables. Ours taste fresh and homemade, and regulars say they're half the reason they come back.

What's the difference between bibimbap and dolsot bibimbap?

Same rice, vegetables and protein. Dolsot comes in a sizzling hot stone bowl, so the rice on the bottom turns golden and crispy.

Which dishes are spicy?

Tteokbokki and jjamppong (spicy seafood noodle soup) bring the heat. Royal tteokbokki is the soy sauce and beef version, with no chili.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 11 AM to 9 PM, Sunday 9 AM to 9 PM. We're closed on Mondays.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call us at (812) 369-4224 or send a request on our order page, and we'll have it hot and ready. You can also find us on DoorDash and Uber Eats.

Can you work around a food allergy?

Tell us when you order. We cook to order, and we're happy to help you pick dishes that work for you.

Is K-bap wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The entrance, seating and restroom are wheelchair accessible.
