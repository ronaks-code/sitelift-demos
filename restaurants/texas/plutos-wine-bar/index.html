Our story

A chef and a wine director

Veteran-owned and family-run by John Formica, the chef, and Liz Formica, the wine director, in downtown Bastrop.

John has run kitchens from Los Angeles to the Central Coast. Liz ran beverage programs. Pluto's is their first place of their own.

Take a bottle home from the shop, join the wine club, or stay the evening. Friendly staff, happy to suggest a pairing.

  • Dry-aged steaksA rotating selection from the restaurant's own dry-ager.
  • Small-production winesA shop, a wine club and recommendations from Liz.
