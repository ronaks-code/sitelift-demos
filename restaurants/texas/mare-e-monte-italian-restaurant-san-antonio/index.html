Behind the table

A table that feels like home

Mare e Monte means sea and mountain: seafood paired with land-based ingredients. We always welcome you home.

The owner makes the rounds to ask how everything is. Lorik Mehmetaj is head chef and co-owner. Pastry chef Melissa Buus takes custom cake orders.

Before you book

Good to know

Do you take reservations?

No. We do not offer reservations but welcome walk-ins daily. Guests who arrive without a table go on a short waitlist.

When are you closed?

Mondays. The rest of the week we open at 11 AM for lunch and dinner.

Do you have a wine list?

Yes, a curated list chosen to go with the food, from sparkling wines to bold reds, with sommelier favorites marked.

Do you cater?

Call the restaurant and ask. Have the date and headcount ready.

Is it a quiet room?

It is lively and a little snug, good for a night with family and friends. It can get loud.
