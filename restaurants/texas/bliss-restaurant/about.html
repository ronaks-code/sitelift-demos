Our story

Chef-owned, just south of downtown

Chef-owned and operated in Southtown, near the King William Historic District and the Blue Star Arts Complex.

Chef Tony Hernandez cooks seasonal, fresh local ingredients. Mary Swiger and Marcy Fernandez lead the front of house. Relaxed, elegant, genuine.

  • Seasonal menuIt changes with the season, with room for creativity.
  • Wine and beerA long wine list, eclectic beers, optional pairings.
  • Rooms for every tableDining room, chef's table, private room, back patio.
