Before you book

Good to know

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday and Sunday 11am to 9pm. Friday and Saturday 11am to 10pm.

Do you do catering and parties?

Yes. Ask about catering, or book a holiday party.

Can I order to go?

Yes, you can order online for takeout.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes. The menu marks vegetarian (V) dishes, and some pastas can be made gluten-free. Ask your server.

What if I have an allergy?

Tell your server. Dishes may be made with allergens.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, you can reserve a table online or call.
