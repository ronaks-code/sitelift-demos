Under the yellow sign

Look for the bright yellow sign, home of the original jalapeño fried chicken. Inside: worn wood, blue checkered tablecloths and long shared tables.

Quick service and big, hearty plates. Soft buns with crispy edges on the breakfast sandwiches. Pies that change by the day.
