Our story

A Galveston kid's kitchen

Chef Jeff Balfour's seasonal menu draws on the coastal classics of his Galveston upbringing, married with southern comfort cooking.

Ground floor of the Pearl brewhouse, built in 1894. Exposed brick, tile floors, Edison bulbs, an open kitchen counter and a custom brewery.

Attentive service without the hovering. Friendly staff, and a room that stays comfortable even when it's full.
