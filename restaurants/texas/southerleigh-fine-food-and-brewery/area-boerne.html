Plates worth the trip

Favorites from a menu that shifts with the seasons.

Coastal classics

Chef Balfour's Galveston roots on the plate. Snapper throats, gumbo with tasso, alligator and crawfish, and cracker-crusted redfish.

Southern comfort

Deviled eggs with bacon jam, sweet-and-spicy cornbread and crisp fried chicken.

Brewed on site

A custom brewery inside the Pearl brewhouse, 15 beers on tap, including Silver King, Pearl on the Patio and Gold Export lager.

Southerleigh was hands down one of the BEST meals we had in San Antonio! From the first bite to the last, everything was outstanding! The deviled eggs were to die for, and the cornbread disappeared almost as soon as it hit the table. The beer was ice cold, the redfish topped with lobster was absolutely incredible, and the fried chicken is an absolute must-order - crispy, juicy, and packed with flavor!!
