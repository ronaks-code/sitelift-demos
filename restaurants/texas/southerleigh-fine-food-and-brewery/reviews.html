Said at the table

Southerleigh was hands down one of the BEST meals we had in San Antonio! From the first bite to the last, everything was outstanding! The deviled eggs were to die for, and the cornbread disappeared almost as soon as it hit the table. The beer was ice cold, the redfish topped with lobster was absolutely incredible, and the fried chicken is an absolute must-order - crispy, juicy, and packed with flavor!!

Lindsay F.

We really enjoyed Southerliegh. The space is unique and well done. Despite being a large open space it didn't seem overly loud. The service was awesome. We started with the Snapper Throats and Spiral Cut Cucumbers. The Snapper was different and a little bit of a challenge to eat, but we enjoyed them nonetheless. I highly recommend the Cracker Crusted Redfish and you can't go wrong with the Fried Chicken.

Jonathan L.

An amazing experience... I was walking through the Pearl with my family, and we literally just stumbled into the place. I was surprised to see a Michelin star on the wall outside next to a set of double doors, but the entrance was rather non-descript! Inside, we sat and enjoyed a few beers, a couple appetizers, and an entree each. Our server, Jasmine, was great; very attentive, but not overbearing.
