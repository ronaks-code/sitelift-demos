Said at the table

Shan Soup Dumpling is the most authentic Sichuan restaurant I've found in the Lakeway / Lake Travis area. The ingredients are clearly fresh, and everything tastes home-cooked, like real Chinese home cooking rather than Americanized takeout. The flavors are bold and properly Sichuan, You leave satisfied but not too heavy. The soup dumplings (xiao long bao) are a must: thin wrappers and a rich broth inside.

Highly recommend this restaurant if you love Chinese food, especially Sichuan cuisine!
