Behind the table

A Thai kitchen at the crossroads

Find us near the junction with Highway 21. A hidden gem with easy parking, though the crossroads traffic can be the hardest part.

Friendly Thai staff happy to explain each dish. Order from a tablet to set the spice level and keep orders straight.

A handy stop on the way to Austin. There is a second location at Arbor Food Park in Austin.

Before you book

Good to know

Can I reserve a table?

Yes. Reserve ahead and the team can keep the pace smooth, especially for families, friends and colleagues.

Do you host parties?

Yes, the dining room with its red booths and woven pendant lights is set up for birthdays, reunions and group occasions. Book in advance.

Can I choose how spicy it is?

Yes. You order from a tablet and set the spice level to your liking.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes, the menu includes vegan options, and many dishes can be made with soft tofu.

Can I order takeout or catering?

Yes. Orders are available for your own schedule, and the team caters noodle dishes, fried rice, curries and salads.

Is there another location?

Yes, there is one at Arbor Food Park in Austin.
