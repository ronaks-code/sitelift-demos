Our story

Tin roof, chalkboard, Highway 21

A weathered tin building with Coca-Cola signs, a wooden deck out front, dark wood inside and room for a crowd.

Everything is made to order. Vegetarian menu, big salads, thick shakes, beer and wine. Friendly service and a drink station where you fix your own.
