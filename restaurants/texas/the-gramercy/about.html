Our story

A hundred years in the making

The Gramercy is a New American restaurant, bar and lounge at Ranch Road 620 South in Lakeway, with art deco touches and the spirit of the 1920s.

The name means great thanks, which is how the Hunt family wants every guest to feel. Hospitality runs in the family.

Executive Chef Hector Landaverde, trained at Le Cordon Bleu, leads the kitchen. A friendly team runs the floor and the bar.

  • Hospitality firstFriendly, welcoming staff.
  • A late kitchenFood served late into the evening.
  • Easy for groupsBig parties handled without a hitch.
