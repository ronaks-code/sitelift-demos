Nuestra historia / Our story

Mi Trailita: my little trailer

It started in 2011 with an orange food trailer called Taqueria Mi Trailita, parked beside a gas station on Manor Road. David Salinas and his mom, Maria, a native of Guerrero, ran it together, and Windsor Park lined up for breakfast tacos, tortas and gorditas.

In January 2022 they opened this dine-in spot half a mile up Manor, close to where the family has lived for years. That's the "y más": tables, a counter, and a bigger menu with enchiladas, flautas, chile rellenos and plates. In 2025 the trailer retired and everything moved under one roof. You'll find her painted across our dining room wall.

"From a food trailer to a dine-in location. A dream come true for a mother and son duo that's been serving the Windsor Park area for over 10 years."Mi Trailita Y Mas
