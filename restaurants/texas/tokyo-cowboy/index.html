About

A Texan room, a Tokyo menu

A classic Texan look with an Asian-inspired menu of Japanese street food and whisky cocktails, from Chef Ben Cachila.

Moody lighting, banquette seating and servers who explain the menu and the plating service. Food arrives fast and hot.
