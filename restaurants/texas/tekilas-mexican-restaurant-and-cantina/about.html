Nuestra historia

A strip-centre cantina with patio

Te'kilas sits at 5000 Hudson Bend Road, in a small strip centre with a patio. The logo is a flame, and the kitchen and bar both run warm.

Breakfast tacos, omelettes, ceviche, street corn ribs, fajitas and enchiladas Potosinas, a signature dish from Rio Verde, San Luis Potosí.

  • Made warmFriendly, attentive servers and bartenders.
  • Authentic platesAuthentic food, corn tortillas and churros to order.
  • Enchiladas your wayMole, green, poblana, Tex-Mex, guajillo or cheese sauce.
