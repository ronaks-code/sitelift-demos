About

Our mission: fall in love with Mexico

Mixtli is the Aztec word for cloud. The menu works the same way: it starts on the coast, works inland, then moves on and begins again.

This season's menu, Mexican Art: Traces Through Time, runs November 7 to January 30. Each course tells an art period through color, texture and winter ingredients.

Chefs Diego Galicia and Rico Torres lead the kitchen with Chef de Cuisine Alexana Cabrera. Lauren Beckman runs the bar. Warm, professional staff.
