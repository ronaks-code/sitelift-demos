What to expect

Taste the menu

Tickets only, Tuesday to Saturday, with a menu that moves through Mexico season by season.

The tasting menu

A multi-course menu that follows one theme through Mexico, each dish explained by the cook who made it.

Wine, cocktail and mocktail pairings

A reserve wine pairing, plus cocktail and mocktail pairings. Sommelier Hailey Pruitt holds a Court of Master Sommeliers certification.

The sweet finish

The menu closes with a Chicano art course of corn cake, charred lime curd and cherry pâte de fruit, then mignardise.
