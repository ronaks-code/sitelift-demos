Fantastic. Mixtli changes up its menu every few months, and this one is all about how Lebanese immigrants influenced Mexican food. Cool concept, and they pulled it off really well. We started with mezze and tea. The hummus, tabbouleh dolma and kibbis were all great. The scallop aguachile with coconut and pomegranate was bright and fresh.

Wow, just wow! Those of us fr CA have our preconceived notions of what Mexican food is and how Tex-Mex (or any Mexican food in TX) just isn't the same...or right. Well, colour me wrong. The dinner here at Mixtli took a few bites, but then I was suddenly convinced and overwhelmed by the soulfulness of their dishes. We were there for the spring La Vecindad menu.

A E. ★★★★★
3

We celebrated our anniversary at Mixtili with the nine-course Mexican Pacific menu. The menu rotates every few months, and the chef who made the dish explained each course as it was delivered. We arrived 10 minutes before our reservation time, so I tried the Mixtli Margarita in the Bar at Mixtili. It was absolutely fantastic, probably the best I’ve had.

Wendi W. ★★★★★
4

Mixtli is so much more than a restaurant, it is a dining experience where you savor the flavors from regions afar. The presentation of the food is so pretty. It beckons you to try everything. There are so many interesting aromas and flavors. Service is impeccable and I enjoyed hearing about the origins of the cuisine we enjoyed during our visit. Plan well in advance if you want to enjoy this experience.
