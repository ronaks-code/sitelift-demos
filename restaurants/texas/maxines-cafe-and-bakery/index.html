Downtown at 905 Main

A yellow storefront with a painted brick arch. Inside, framed photos and memorabilia cover a brick wall, and records hang over the dining room.

Pull up a seat at the liars' table. Friendly waitresses, a full bakery, and catering from Aunt Fanny's.
