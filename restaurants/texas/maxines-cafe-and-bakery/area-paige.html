Maxine's favorites

Made from scratch, just like your mama used to make it.

Griddle cakes & waffles

Maxine's Legendary Griddle Cake is a 12-inch platter, with blueberry, chocolate chip or pecan praline. Belgian waffles and French toast too.

Benedicts, migas & biscuits

Texas Benny with jalapeno hollandaise, eggs Benedict and Florentine, migas, breakfast tacos and Jack's Eye Opener on a home-baked biscuit.

Southern lunch & dinner

Chicken fried steak with cream gravy, fried catfish, pork chops, mashed potatoes, buttered corn and butter beans. Save room for pie.
