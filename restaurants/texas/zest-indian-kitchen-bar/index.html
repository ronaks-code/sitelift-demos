Under the sign on Main Street

In the Oaks at Lakeway shopping center, under backlit letters on a pale brick front, with sidewalk tables and a black awning.

Traditional Indian food with a modern twist. The bar pours wine, beer, spirits and cocktails, and the patio is pet friendly.
