Meet the crew

Lauren and Chris, in Bastrop

Lauren and Chris Rios are Texas born and raised. They met in 2012, fell for craft coffee on their travels, and settled in Bastrop.

Horizons is their coffee trailer at home: espresso, drip, teas and fruit smoothies from quality beans sourced around the world.
