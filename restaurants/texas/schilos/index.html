Our story

San Antonio's oldest, still serving

German-Texan food since 1917, from family recipes. Split pea soup, a Reuben with our own hot mustard, fresh pretzels, homemade root beer.

A rustic, vintage room with smiling, attentive staff. The Texas Room and Trophy Room are open for parties and private events.
