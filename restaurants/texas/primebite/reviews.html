Said at the picnic tables

If you're looking for a great burger in Bastrop, put PrimeBite Burgers on your list! We stopped by today and were seriously impressed. My husband ordered the Le Sencilla, and I had the Smash Champ. All of their burgers are served as combos, so they include fries and a drink. Everything was fresh, juicy, flavorful, and grilled with love.

Erin C. ★★★★★

Stopped by Prime Bite and had some delicious food! We got the kids cheeseburger meal and a norteña burger. The burgers are super juicy, the fries are crisp and seasoned to perfection, and their sandia agua fresca hit the spot on a warm day while sitting at the outdoor picnic tables. Even appreciated the little peppermint candy on the tray! Will definitely be back, 5/5 stars!

Wendy A. ★★★★★

I came to a meeting in Bastrop for work and had to stop by a local food truck making smash burgers as they are my favorite! This spot is all outside, but kept clean and has the entire area shaded with a pavilion. This spot had friendly and fast service and I got the Champ burger. Had jalapeños, onions, and two well seasoned and juicy smash patties.
