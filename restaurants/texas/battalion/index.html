Kind words

Celebrated early Mother's Day. Battalion is my mom's favorite restaurant and for a reason. Mother and son dinner We had a reservation at 7pm, got there a bit early but got seated immediately by the hostess who was very welcoming. Sarah was very friendly and took her time to explain the menu to us. The portions were just perfect. Never had to ask for refills. The manager came to check on us several times.

Jorge U. ★★★★★

This restaurant is located in an old firestation with the outside façade being largely preserved. Inside you can see the old fire pole which we were sat next to. The menu's brevity is refreshing as it allows for easier ordering. The menu does change, although there are some staples that are usually available. There is a wide variety of cocktail and wine selections, as well as Ritual non-alcoholic spirits. We had an aperol spritz which was excellent. For our meal we ordered the bread service with whipped ricotta which was fair, although the bread was harder than I'd prefer. For our appetizer we had the spinach dumplings which were phenomenal. I would certainly order them again. For our entrees we had the branzino and spaghetti di gamberi, both of which were excellent. The flavors were complex and unexpected in the best way. The branzino was cooked perfectly, and notably herbaceous.
