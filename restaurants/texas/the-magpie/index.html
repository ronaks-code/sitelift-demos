One of my favorite places in San Antonio. I love hole-in-the-wall ish restaurants with amazing food and this is one of them. To be honest, I’m always so excited to eat that I forget to grab photos but my recommendation is always to get the milk bread as an appetizer it is just the perfect size. This time, we ordered the smoked duck and it was perfect with the right amount of chew.
