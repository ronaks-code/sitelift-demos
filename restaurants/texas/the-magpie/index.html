Notes from the table

One of my favorite places in San Antonio. I love hole-in-the-wall ish restaurants with amazing food and this is one of them. To be honest, I’m always so excited to eat that I forget to grab photos but my recommendation is always to get the milk bread as an appetizer it is just the perfect size. This time, we ordered the smoked duck and it was perfect with the right amount of chew.

Karen

Our second visit to Magpie, and our taste buds danced! We shared the panzanella salad, ricotta gnocchi, grilled red snapper, and dak galbi alongside a beautiful bottle of Côtes du Rhône GSM. Everything was delicious, with colorful, inviting presentation. The ricotta gnocchi was a standout. My husband isn’t usually a big pasta guy, but he loved it and kept going back for the sauce, that told me everything!

Crystal M.

Pleasant experience with a group of 7 Friends moved away from SA and came back to visit. Insisted on coming here for dinner. Kenzie (our server) was fantastic: offered good and useful advice and feedback. Also took the extra effort of marking our menus to account for allergies to dairy (1 guest) and gluten (1 guest), enabling our friends to order with confidence. Food was delicious!

A dark, styled room with pink lights. Attentive service, clean plates for each dish, and menus marked for allergies.
