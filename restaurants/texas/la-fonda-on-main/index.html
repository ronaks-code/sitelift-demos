About

Since 1932 on Main Avenue

San Antonio's oldest Mexican restaurant, serving authentic interior Mexican cuisine and Tex-Mex since 1932. Part of the Lawton Family of Restaurants.

White stucco walls, a red tile roof, blue trim and arched gates. Seating indoors and outdoors.

Fresh salsa, fajitas with rice and beans, empanadas and attentive servers. Weekend brunch, social hour and to-go family meals too.
