Our story

Not fancy. Just good food.

A family owned and operated restaurant, in business since 2011. One block off Highway 71 in Bastrop.

Simple seating and an open kitchen, so you can watch your sandwich get made. Friendly, hard-working staff, big portions and fair prices.
