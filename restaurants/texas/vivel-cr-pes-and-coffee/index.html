Our story

Celebrating life everyday

Vivel fell in love with crêpe culture in France and Europe. The first café opened in Maui, Hawaii in 2001, then Houston, and now Lakeway.

Crêpes were peasant food in 15th century Brittany. Vivel keeps some of those traditional recipes and time-honored techniques, then adds modern flair and flavor.

A cozy, relaxed café with friendly, efficient service. Coffee and tea that bring out the flavor of the crêpes.
