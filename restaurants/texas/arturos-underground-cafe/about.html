our story

Eclectic Austin fare since 1996

Arturo's Underground Cafe has been serving eclectic Austin fare since 1996. Open only for breakfast and lunch, we're a neighborhood staple for the local office set, students and tourists alike.

During the week you can step up to the counter for fresh-made breakfast tacos, egg sandwiches and breakfast plates until 11am, then quesadillas, hot and cold sandwiches and hearty salads until 2pm. On weekends we invite you down for brunch: original benedict recipes, the Diablo and Caribbean Toast, hearty scrambles, sandwiches and salads.
