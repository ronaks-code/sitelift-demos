Behind the table

Italian market vibe, on Tampa Rd

Bright, energetic, and set up like an Italian market: red and green chairs, framed family photos, flags overhead, and a gelato and dessert coffee bar.

Friendly staff who explain how to order. The Mandola family started Mandola's Italian Market near Austin and now runs locations in Texas and Florida.

  • Fresh and flavorfulMargherita, chicken parmesan, and focaccia made fresh.
  • Gluten free optionsGluten free bread, pasta, and pizza crust.
  • Family friendlyPersonal pizzas for kids, a gelato cup, clean family restrooms.
