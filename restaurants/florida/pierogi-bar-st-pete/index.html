Hello, we're

From home kitchen to Central Ave

We began as a cottage kitchen run from home, delivering Ukrainian comfort food from family recipes: borscht, deruny and the food we grew up with.

It took nearly a year to find the right place. Then we paired Ukrainian staples with one of America's iconic cocktails, the martini.

  • Made from scratchPierogi, soups and sides from family recipes.
  • Martini barUkrainian Dirty Martini, Mahia and Tipsy Cherry.
  • Private diningBirthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings.
