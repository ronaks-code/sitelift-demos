Our story

The second coming of Cafe Ponte

The newly built successor to Cafe Ponte, which served Tampa Bay for over 18 years. Chef Chris Ponte trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris.

A family business: Chris runs The Ponte Group with his wife Michelle and sons JT and Andy Mahoney. Big chandelier, white tablecloths, attentive service.
