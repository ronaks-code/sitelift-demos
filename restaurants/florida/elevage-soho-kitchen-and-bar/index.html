Our story

Raised up, like a vintage

Élevage grew out of Bern's Steak House traditions. The name is a winemaker's term for raising a vintage, and the kitchen treats old recipes the same way.

Executive Chef Jonathan Atanacio leads the kitchen, with nearly 20 years of cooking behind him, much of it in New York City and at Bern's.

  • Bern's rootsA kitchen built on Bern's Steak House traditions.
  • Simple, well madeSimple seasoning, hot plates.
  • A room worth the stopWarm wood, rope lights and a cellar of vintage wines.
