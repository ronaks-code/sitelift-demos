Our story

Hospitality first

A New American dinner menu that opens with pull-apart milk bread, caviar service and composed salads.

Michael Stewart is the owner, Kal Harris the general manager and Robert Hesse the executive chef.

The dining room is light and crisp, with brass chandeliers, velvet banquettes and marble tables. Valet is offered, and there is street parking nearby.
