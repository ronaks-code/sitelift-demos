Our story

One of Westchase's originals

A bar and restaurant for families, friends and sports fans. One of Westchase's original restaurants, serving wings for more than 25 years.

The menu comes from founder John "Popa" Castaneda. Popa's Red Chili, his original recipe, has been on the menu since 1998.
