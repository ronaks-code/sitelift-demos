Our story

From bakery to bistro

La Creación Bistro grew out of La Creación Bakery, a Puerto Rican bakery serving the Tampa community for seven years.

Chef Armando Casanova, born in Puerto Rico, has 25 years in the kitchen and cooks island heritage alongside classic American dishes.

  • Puerto Rican rootsHeritage meets classic American dishes.
  • Brunch and dinnerTwo menus, one kitchen.
