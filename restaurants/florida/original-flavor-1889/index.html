About

Original Flavor, with a twist

Our pizzaioli from Naples cook a contemporary Neapolitan pizza: long fermentation, high hydration, a light and airy crust.

Our chef brings 20 years of restaurant experience. A wine bar first, with pizza, pasta, salumi and cheese.

  • 1Long fermentationDough built for a light, airy crust.
  • 2Italian ingredientsSan Marzano tomatoes and Fiordilatte, imported from Italy.
  • 3Cooks from NaplesOur pizzaioli come from Naples, Italy.
