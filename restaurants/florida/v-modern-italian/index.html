About

A room made to be social

Dark walnut, marble and velvet. Large PichiAvo murals, a sculptural olive tree and a glowing granite bar anchor the room.

Open dining room, intimate seating, two private spaces and an open kitchen. The music is loud, like a club.

  • 1Dinner with a soundtrackA DJ makes it feel like a party.
