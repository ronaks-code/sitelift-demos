Romania to Hyde Park

The family has run restaurants in Romania for over 12 years. Their first in the States opened in June 2020.

Family owned and operated. The grandma cooks here every day, and the dining room feels like home.

  • Generous platesBig portions, with leftovers to take home.
  • Warm serviceAttentive, never hovering. Chefs come out to say hello.
