Nuestra historia

Family flavor since 2020

Mojo Grill was born in 2020 from one idea: put honest, punchy Latin flavor in front of the community and back it up every day.

Luis is at the grill. Olimar is in the kitchen, with the house mojo marinade and tostones smashed to order.

  • Made freshMojo pork, empanadas and bowls, every day.
  • Family flavorLuis at the grill, Olimar in the kitchen.
  • CateringTrays for parties, offices and game days.
