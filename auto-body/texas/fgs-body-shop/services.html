Good to know

Questions people ask

Can my insurance company make me use their shop?

FG's says no: your insurance company cannot choose your shop, you can. The shop handles the insurance communication for you.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Call, or send the request form with your vehicle and what happened.

What vehicles do you work on?

All makes and models.

Do you do dent repair without painting?

Minor dents may qualify for paintless dent repair. The shop will assess and tell you the most cost-effective option.

Do you fix rust?

Cosmetic rust on hoods, roofs and body panels, yes. Frame or structural corrosion is not part of it.

Who will I talk to during the repair?

Adan Jr. is the main point of contact and stays with you through the process. Obed handles insurance claims.
