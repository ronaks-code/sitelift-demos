Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you repaint the panel?

No. Paintless repair works the dent out of the panel, with no repaint and no fillers.

Can I get an estimate by phone?

For non-hail dents and plastic bumper repair, yes. Call or text with the location of the dent.

How do hail estimates work?

We book an appointment so we can see the vehicle in person.

Is there somewhere to wait?

Yes. There is a waiting area with chairs.

Got a dent?

Call or text for a phone estimate on dings and bumpers. Hail gets booked for a look in person.
