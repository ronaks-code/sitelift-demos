Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer a warranty?

Most work carries a 3 year, 36,000 mile nationwide warranty. Terms are available on request.

Do you do oil changes?

Yes: standard, semi-synthetic blend, full synthetic and diesel. Tire rotation can be free with an oil change.

Will you push extra repairs?

No. You get a report of suggested repairs, and we do only what you schedule.

How will I know my car is ready?

We text, email or call when your car is done and ready for pickup.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed Sunday.
