Good to know

Questions people ask

What tint do you use?

STEK ceramic and graphene window films, chosen for cooling and clarity, backed by 30 years of tint experience.

How long does tint take?

About half a day, with no bubbles or defects.

Do you fix dents without repainting?

Yes. Paintless dent repair is one of our services, including hail damage.

Can you fix a windshield chip or crack?

Yes, we repair chips and cracks. If a crack is better left alone, we'll tell you.

What does cut and polish cost?

Call the shop for pricing.

Which towns do you serve?

Bastrop, Smithville, Cedar Creek, Webberville, Paige and the surrounding area.
