Good to know

Questions people ask

Can you fix a car that will not start?

Yes. Tow it in and we will diagnose it, often with a quick turnaround. Call and describe what happens when you turn the key.

Do you replace window motors?

Yes. We test first, since it is often the motor and not the switch. Parts are ordered in and fitted.

Do you work on imports?

Yes. The sign says domestic and import, and we work on both.

Do you do spark plugs?

Yes. Spark plug replacement is part of our regular work.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
