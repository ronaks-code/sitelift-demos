About the shop

Lakeway's family body shop

Family owned and operated since 1989. The Whitakers have raised their families in Austin and Lakeway for over 36 years.

Technicians restore each vehicle to pre-accident condition. Paint goes on with PPG Envirobase waterborne paint, which makes this one of the few green body shops.

  • Lifetime warrantyThe shop stands behind its work for good.
  • Drop-off and pick-upComplimentary, with a rental car arranged if you need one.
  • Green paintPPG Envirobase waterborne paint, easier on the neighborhood.
