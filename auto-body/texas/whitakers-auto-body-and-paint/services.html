Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes. The shop handles accident claims for most insurance companies. Call with your claim details.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Free auto body estimates, given quickly and priced fairly.

Do you stand behind the work?

Whitakers stands behind its work with a lifetime warranty.

Can you pick up my car?

Yes. The shop offers complimentary customer drop-off and pick-up, and can arrange a rental car to be waiting for you.

What paint do you use?

PPG Envirobase waterborne paint, which is why the shop calls itself one of the very few green body shops.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. The crew can wait a little past 5:00, and Saturday is possible by appointment.
