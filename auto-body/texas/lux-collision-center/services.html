Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed on weekends.

Where is the shop?

702 SW Loop 410, San Antonio, right off Loop 410 near Hwy 151.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. We handle supplemental estimates and keep you updated while the insurer works through the claim. Send your claim and photos through the contact page.

Do you offer estimates?

Yes, free upfront estimates. Send photos of the damage or stop by.

Can you tow my car?

Yes, towing is available and the shop can arrange a rental car.

Which cars do you repair?

All makes and models, including Volkswagen, Tesla, Audi, BMW, Porsche, Mercedes-Benz and Toyota.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, the shop lists a lifetime warranty on its repairs.
