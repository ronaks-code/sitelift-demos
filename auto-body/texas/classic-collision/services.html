Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The Buda center lists free estimates, and its site offers a photo estimate. Call the shop to ask which fits your damage.

What does the shop repair?

Collision damage, auto glass, and ADAS calibrations and electronics.

Is there a warranty?

The center lists a lifetime warranty. Ask the shop how it covers your repair.

Do you help with tow or a rental?

The Buda center lists tow assistance and rental assistance. Call to ask how it works for your claim.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed Sunday. Call ahead for Saturday.
