Good to know

Questions people ask

What do you work on?

Brakes, tune-ups, oil changes, state inspections, check engine diagnostics, auto AC, collision, paint, dent removal and hail damage. All major makes, domestic and import.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Book buttons on the site, or call the shop during hours.

Will I know what is going on with my car?

Yes. You get regular updates during the repair, and the work is explained before it starts.

Is the work under warranty?

Long's is Technet certified and backed by Technet's 24-month, 24,000-mile nationwide warranty.

Is financing available?

Yes, through Affirm. Ask when you book.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
