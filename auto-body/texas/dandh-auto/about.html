Slavko and the D&H family

D&H Auto is a family owned and customer oriented repair shop in Round Rock, stone front and red sign, at 300 Old W Dr. It specializes in international and domestic vehicles, and many of its customers moved with it from the earlier Pflugerville location.

Slavko is honest, knowledgeable and straightforward. Families bring us their cars for years.

We photograph what we replace so you can see the work. When the dealer can't find parts, we track them down.

  • Honest repairsUpfront about what the car needs.
  • Proof in photosThe team takes pictures of everything they replace.
  • Under dealer costWork costs much less than the dealer.
