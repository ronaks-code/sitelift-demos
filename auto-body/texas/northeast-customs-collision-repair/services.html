Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes. The shop works with your insurance company every step of the way and makes sure the damage is addressed right.

Can I get a collision estimate over the phone?

Collision estimates are done in person. Damage often hides underlying issues that only a detailed inspection finds, so bring the car in and we will look it over.

Can you quote custom work online?

Yes. For custom work and cosmetic modifications, the shop is happy to discuss your vision and quote online or over the phone.

How fast can you get my car in?

It depends on the damage and the schedule. The shop will try to squeeze you in and wrap up quickly.

What kinds of custom work do you do?

Custom paint, full body kit installs, antique restorations and other custom projects, including a widebody kit and full paint on a 2020 Lamborghini Urus.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Northeast Customs says all vehicles are backed by its limited lifetime warranty. Ask the shop for the details when you bring your car in.
