About the shop

Midsize, with a personal touch

Established in 2010, with more than 25 years of combined experience. Kept midsize so the team can talk through the accident and your expectations.

Edgar Vitela is general manager. David Kemenesi runs sales. Welcoming, quick to answer questions, and good with progress updates.

Lusso Carrozzeria is our second brand for exotic Italian cars. Discount Body Shop was a 2019 Best of the Best Television National Award winner.
