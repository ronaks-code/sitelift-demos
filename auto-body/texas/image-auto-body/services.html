Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I have to use my insurance company's shop?

No. The shop's own page says you are not required to use the shop your insurance company suggests. In Texas, the choice is yours.

Will you deal with my insurance?

Yes. We talk to your adjuster and help arrange a rental car.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes, complimentary vehicle pickup and delivery.

Which brands are you certified for?

The shop lists OEM certification for Honda and Acura, Chevrolet, Buick, GMC and Cadillac, Ford, Chrysler, Dodge, Ram, Jeep and Fiat, Nissan and Infiniti, Hyundai, Kia and Subaru.

Do you replace windshields?

Yes. The shop offers glass repair and windshield replacement, at the shop or by mobile service in Austin.
