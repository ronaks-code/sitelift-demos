Shop notes

Family owned since 1954

Family owned and operated, the oldest collision repair center in Austin by our own account. The original shop opened in the early 1940s at 6th and Neches.

The Kyle shop sits under the blue awning with the checkered flag. Friendly, professional, knowledgeable staff.

Honest quotes, regular check-ins, rentals sorted early. Stop in for a walk-in estimate or send photos.

  • Upfront and honestA family run shop that tells it straight.
  • Kept in the loopDaily check-ins during your repair.
  • Insurance and rentalWorks with all major insurers and coordinates rental and towing.
