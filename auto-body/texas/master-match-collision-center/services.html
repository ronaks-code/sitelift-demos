Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. The shop works with your insurance to restore your vehicle after a wreck.

Can I choose my own repair shop?

Yes. Vehicle owners have the right to choose the repair shop they prefer, even if an insurer recommends another.

What is paintless dent repair?

A way to remove dents without disturbing the original paint, used when the paint is not cracked or chipped. Many minor repairs take a few hours.

Can I get an estimate online?

Yes. Use the form on the contact page, or call the shop.

What makes and models do you repair?

All makes and models.
