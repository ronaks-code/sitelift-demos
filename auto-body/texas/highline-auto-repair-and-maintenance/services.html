Questions people ask

What cars does Highline work on?

The shop specializes in high-end European vehicles, particularly Mercedes-Benz and Porsche, plus other luxury performance brands like McLaren. Classic Mercedes are welcome too.

How is Highline different from a dealership?

Dealership-level expertise at lower prices, with personal service, clear communication and flexible repair planning.

Do you use factory parts?

Yes, OEM parts whenever possible, and you are offered alternatives if you want a more budget-friendly option.

How is pricing determined?

After diagnostics you get a detailed proposal covering recommended parts, labor hours and projected timelines. Work is invoiced on completion, so you pay only for approved services.

Can I bring my own parts?

You may. The shop first checks compatibility and warranty implications, and will talk through the option that makes the most sense.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

When possible. There may be an extra fee beyond ten miles from the shop, and off-site diagnostics carry a fee. Call or email to ask.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, Highline is by appointment only.
