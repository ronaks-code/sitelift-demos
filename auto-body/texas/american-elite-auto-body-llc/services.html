The repair, step by step

How a repair goes

  1. 1

    Swing by or call

    Drop in during shop hours. The team looks at the vehicle within minutes.

  2. 2

    Assessment and estimate

    A full look at the damage so the plan and the estimate are clear before work starts.

  3. 3

    Repair and updates

    Parts ordered, body and paint done, messages along the way. Ask to see the old part next to the new one.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment for an estimate?

No. Drop in during shop hours for an estimate. Monday to Friday 8 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 2 PM.

Can you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. The shop helps with insurance claims and can also help you arrange a replacement vehicle while yours is being repaired. Call with your claim details.

What do you repair?

Collision damage, bumpers, scratches, paintless dents, hail damage, car painting, frame straightening, windshield replacement, wheel alignment and suspension work.

Will I hear how the repair is going?

Yes. You get messages step by step, and the team stays helpful throughout.

Do you offer towing?

The shop offers towing. Call and ask about getting a damaged vehicle to Sunrise Rd.

Do you work on trucks as well as cars?

Yes. We repair cars, SUVs and pickups.
