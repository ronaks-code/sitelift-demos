Good to know

Questions people ask

What do you repair?

Body repairs, bumper repair and paint work on cars, trucks and SUVs. The sign out front also lists polish, paint and prep, and a frame machine.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. The shop is bilingual.

Will I hear how the work is going?

Yes. Javier keeps you updated as the work moves along.

Is it worth driving in from Austin?

One customer comes all the way from Austin and says they plan to bring all their future work here.

Can you fix a bumper that has been ripped off?

Yes. Call with your car's details and what happened.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

How do I get an estimate?

Call the shop or send a request on the contact page with your vehicle and a short description of the damage.
