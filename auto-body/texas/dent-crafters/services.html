Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. All insurance is accepted, and Dent Crafters handles the paperwork with your insurance company.

Will my car need repainting?

No. Dents are massaged out by hand without body filler, chemicals or repainting, so the original finish is kept.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are done in 24 hours or less.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Dent Crafters travels across its service area, from San Antonio to Waco and Columbus to Dripping Springs.

Can I get an estimate from photos?

Yes. Send photos of the damage and we'll work you into the schedule. Estimates are free.

Does the work come with a warranty?

Yes. Dent Crafters backs every repair with a 100% warranty.
