About the shop

A family shop on Highway 71

Family owned and operated since 1998. A full service center for most foreign and domestic vehicles, with factory scheduled maintenance.

Friendly, knowledgeable people who honor their commitment. Fair prices and work done in days, not weeks.

A full 1-year warranty on most service. No one can promise every fix will do the job. Every service includes a free vehicle inspection.
