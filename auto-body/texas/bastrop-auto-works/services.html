Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. A full 1-year warranty on most service. No one can promise every fix will do the job, so ask when you call.

Will you try to upsell me?

No. We only recommend what your car needs, and we honor our commitment to your work.

What kinds of cars do you work on?

Most foreign and domestic vehicles, with engine, transmission, brake, A/C and factory scheduled maintenance work.

My check engine light is on. What do I do?

Stop by and we will run a diagnostic check.

Are inspections included?

Every service comes with a free vehicle inspection.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
