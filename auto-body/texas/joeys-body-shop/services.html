Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday.

Will the final work match the estimate?

Yes. The work should match the estimate for time and cost. Call or stop by for an estimate before any work starts.

Can you help if my car can't be driven?

Call us. We can arrange a tow to the shop.

How long will parts take?

Some parts have to be ordered, which can take a while. Call and we'll tell you what to expect for your vehicle.
