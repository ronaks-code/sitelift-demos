Shop notes

The shop on Ponderosa Road

Bobby's Automotive is family owned and run in Bastrop, on Ponderosa Road, since 2009. The door says it: Take it to Bobby's.

Loyal drivers, fast fixes, fair prices. Brake trouble or a dead alternator, we get you back on the road.

The shop lists ASE Senior Master Technicians on its team. Waiting on a job that takes a couple of hours? There is a lounge with leather couches.
