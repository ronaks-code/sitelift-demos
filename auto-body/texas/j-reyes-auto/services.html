Good to know

Questions people ask

What do you work on?

Maintenance, A/C repair, alignments, brakes and diagnostics for all makes and models. Body work and collision repair too.

Will you explain the repair first?

Yes. We explain what is wrong before starting, so you know what you are paying for.

Can you check an error light?

Yes. We run diagnostics to find what is behind the light, whether it is a battery or something else.

Do you do oil changes?

Yes. Oil changes, new filters and checks for noises underneath the car.

Do you fix A/C?

Yes. Technicians check for leaks and other system problems.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Saturday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed Sunday.

How do I get an estimate?

Call the shop or send a request on the contact page with your vehicle and the problem.
