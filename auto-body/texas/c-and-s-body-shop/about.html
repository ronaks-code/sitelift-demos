About C & S

A family-owned body shop in Buda since 2009.

05 / About

Named for Christian and Samantha

Lupe Gonzalez founded C & S in 2009. The name comes from his children, Christian and Samantha.

Lupe started in a small shop, moved to downtown Austin, then relocated to Buda. Some still make the drive.

The mission is simple: treat people with care and honesty. Lupe and Robert explain the repair and the insurance process.
