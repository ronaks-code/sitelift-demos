Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you tow vehicles?

No. Texas Pride is an automotive shop and is not a towing company.

What do you work on?

Domestic cars, trucks and imports: brakes, alignments, A/C, exhaust, engine and transmission, diagnostics and oil changes.

Can you reset sensors after a bumper or windshield replacement?

Yes. The shop has equipment to reset the bumper and windshield sensors, including lane departure calibration.

Do you do alignments?

Yes, four-wheel alignments for all makes and models.

Who do I talk to?

Call the shop. Brian Perry has over 30 years of experience in automotive repair.
