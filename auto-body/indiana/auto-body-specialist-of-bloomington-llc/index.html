What we do

Collision repair, start to finish

We repair collision damage on cars, trucks and SUVs: doors, panels, frame work on our in-house rack, and matched paint.

Collision repair

After an accident, we take the car in, repair the damage and get it back to you looking right.

Frame straightening

Our own frame rack and pulling setup, right here in the shop, for damage that goes deeper than the panels.

Door & panel repair

Dented, creased or crushed doors and panels, repaired so the damage disappears.

Paint & refinish

Repaired panels painted to match the rest of the car, with a clean, glossy finish.

Insurance claim repairs

We work with your insurance company and handle the paperwork with you.

Estimates

Call or send photos of the damage online. Accurate estimates, and we stick to them.
