Engines Xtreme is an engine remanufacturer and machine shop at 523 E Church St in Lewisville. We do block preparation, grind crankshafts, resize connecting rods and remanufacture cylinder heads, short blocks and long blocks, along with a line of internal engine parts. Every product we build is taken apart, cleaned and checked for defects, then machined to factory specifications. It goes back together with new pistons, rings, bearings, timing components, lifters, oil pumps and gaskets, along with reground camshafts. Customers have given us a 4.7 rating from 32 Google reviews. Have a special order, a custom application or a performance question? Call or email us and we will talk it over.
