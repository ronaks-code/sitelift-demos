Good to know

Questions people ask

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Most repairs carry a 5-year, 50,000-mile warranty.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome and same day service is offered, and you can also schedule service ahead of time.

Can I get a ride or a loaner car?

Free loaner cars and a shuttle are available, and no-contact drop-off is an option. Ask when you book.

What makes and models do you work on?

Domestic and import cars, pickup trucks, SUVs, minivans, hybrids and crossovers. Toyota and Honda repair is a specialty.

Can you do my state inspection?

Yes. The shop does DMV certified vehicle inspections and state emission inspections, along with any repairs they turn up.

Where are you and when are you open?

8601 Burnet Rd, Austin, TX 78757, Monday to Friday 7 AM–6 PM, closed Saturday and Sunday. Sister shops are on Buell Ave and in Cedar Park.
