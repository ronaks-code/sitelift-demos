About the shop

One builder, rebuilding transmissions since 1988.

The shop

One builder, Daniel DeCastro

Daniel DeCastro opened D C Transmissions in Dripping Springs in 2009. He graduated from the Chicago School of Automatic Transmissions in 1988 and has rebuilt transmissions ever since.

He works on standard and automatic units, heavy duty Allisons, diesels and vintage vehicles.

The shop is an ATRA member and follows its code of ethics. Rebuilds carry a free ATRA National Warranty of 12 months or 12,000 miles.
