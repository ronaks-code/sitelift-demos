Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Saturday is by appointment and Sunday is closed.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. The shop offers a free inspection or diagnosis. Stop by, or call to set a time.

Is there a warranty on a rebuild?

Rebuilds come with a free ATRA National Warranty of 12 months or 12,000 miles. Other lengths are offered, and abnormal situations may need different terms.

Will you pull my transmission out right away?

No. The shop will not remove a transmission unless it is absolutely necessary and you approve it.

Can you beat another shop's quote?

Bring a written estimate from another legitimate shop. The shop says it can beat it.

Do you work on standard transmissions?

Yes. Daniel works on standard and automatic transmissions, heavy duty Allisons, diesels and vintage vehicles.

Where are you?

On US-290 in Dripping Springs, in the Texas Hill Country. Customers also come from nearby towns.

How much will it cost?

Every transmission is different, so no prices are posted here. Call for a free inspection and your options.
