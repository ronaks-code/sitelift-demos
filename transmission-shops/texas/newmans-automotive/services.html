Good to know

Questions people ask

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Almost every repair is covered by a 36-month, 36,000-mile warranty. Ask about V.I.P. membership for a limited lifetime guarantee on services.

How much is a brake job?

It depends on pads versus rotors and your vehicle. Typical range $250 to $450, with an itemized estimate first.

How much does A/C repair cost?

A basic recharge is on the lower end, and compressor or condenser work costs more. Typical range $150 to $350, and we give a clear estimate first.

How much is a timing belt replacement?

It varies by vehicle. Typical range $600 to $1,000, with an estimate first.

Do you offer a shuttle or loaner car?

Yes. There is a free shuttle during service and loaner cars, and a waiting room with free WiFi if you would rather stay.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes, seven days a week. Monday to Friday 7 AM–6 PM, Saturday 8 AM–4 PM and Sunday 8 AM–3 PM.

What cars do you work on?

Foreign and domestic: Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Ford, BMW, Mercedes, Lexus, VW and more.

Car trouble? Call the shop.

We are open seven days a week. We will tell you what is wrong before we fix it.
