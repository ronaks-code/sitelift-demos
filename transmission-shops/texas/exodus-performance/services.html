Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm, by appointment. Closed on weekends.

Which vehicles do you work on?

Late-model GM: Corvette, Camaro, Cadillac V-Series and GM trucks and SUVs.

Do you have a dyno?

Yes, an in-house Dynojet roller dyno for car dyno tuning. Supported GM trucks and SUVs use street tuning.

Can I watch my car while it is in the shop?

Yes. Active-build customers get passcode-protected Secure Live View with privacy zones.

What should I send before I come in?

Year, make and model, current modifications, fuel, intended use and your goal, so we can confirm the scope.

Do you work on vehicles from outside Austin?

Yes. Call to discuss the build and arrange transport. We confirm scope, scheduling and transport first.

How much will it cost?

Every build is different, so we do not post prices here. Send your details and we will explain the scope and cost.
