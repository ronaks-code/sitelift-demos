Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins and drop-offs are welcome, Monday to Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. You can also schedule a drop-off on the contact page.

Will I get an estimate first?

Yes. We explain the recommended repairs, give a written estimate, and get your authorization before work begins.

Do you offer a warranty?

Eligible parts and labor carry a standard 2-year / 24,000-mile warranty, unless otherwise specified.

Do you do emissions inspections?

Yes, Texas emissions inspections with no appointment needed.

What cars do you work on?

Domestic, Asian and European vehicles, from daily commuters to work trucks.
