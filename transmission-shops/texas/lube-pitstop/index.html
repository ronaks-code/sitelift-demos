Still on South Lamar

Matt Carson opened Lube Pitstop in 1978 while working as an Austin firefighter. He brought the 10 minute oil change home from California.

The shop once had seven locations. South Lamar is the one still running, led by manager Donny Smith and assistant manager Chris Carson.
