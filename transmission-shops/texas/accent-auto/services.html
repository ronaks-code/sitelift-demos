Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are typically required, and work is delivered when promised. Call to set a time.

Do you work on foreign cars?

Yes. The shop lists BMW, Porsche, Tesla, Cadillac, Maserati, Fiat and all domestic vehicles.

Will I see what is wrong?

Yes. Pictures and videos of the problem, and a detailed inspection report with photos after a brake job.

Do you handle warranty and insurance claims?

Yes, extended warranty and insurance claims. Repairs are covered nationwide through TechNet.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
