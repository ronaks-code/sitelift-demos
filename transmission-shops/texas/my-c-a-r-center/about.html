The people behind My C.A.R Center.

The harness

A family shop on Caribou St

Jack Carrera started My C.A.R Center after years in repair shops and dealerships. He is an ASE Master Technician and the founder.

Steve Carrera has over 30 years in diesel, mostly Ford Powerstroke and Dodge Cummins. Blake Neel is a technician. Karen Carrera runs customer service and speaks Spanish.

The team calls and texts with updates. Wait in our room with free Wi-Fi and refreshments. Drop-off and pick-up within 5 miles.
