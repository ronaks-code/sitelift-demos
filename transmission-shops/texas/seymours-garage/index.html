(04) About

Family owned, open since 1968.

Hastings and Ida Seymour started the shop in 1968. Mark Colaw bought it in 1985 and opened the Speedway Drive location in 1988.

ASE-certified technicians and service advisors work the bays. Melissa greets you at the front desk, and a porter can give you a ride.
