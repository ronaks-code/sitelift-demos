(04) About

Family-owned, woman-run

Opened in 2016 in a steel building under a rust-colored sign. Locally and family-owned, woman-run, with over two decades of automotive knowledge.

Dealer-trained technicians, OEM parts, and Liqui Moly, Motul and Castrol fluids. Free estimates, and a 24-month/24,000-mile warranty on every repair.

A calm lobby, a team that explains the work, and a ride home when a job runs long.

Straight about cost
Accurate quotes and timelines. No pushed upsells.
Trusted with hybrids
Confident hybrid and BMW work, and we work with your warranty.
Same day when possible
No appointment? Often fixed the same day, every step explained.
