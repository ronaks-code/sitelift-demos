About MADNESS

Enthusiast-run since 2008, with the Texas flagship in Spicewood.

The crew

A garage run by car people

MADNESS Autoworks is a privately owned performance and customization company. Since 2008 it has helped drivers personalize their cars with aftermarket parts, expert installation and hands-on support.

The Spicewood shop is the Texas flagship: main warehouse plus a full-service workshop, from exhaust and suspension to tuning, audio and turnkey builds.

Clear updates, same-day appointments and a tech you meet before work begins. The lot hosts the odd meetup.
