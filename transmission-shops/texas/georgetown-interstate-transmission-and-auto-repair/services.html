Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you stand behind your repairs?

Yes. Qualifying repairs carry a 2-year/24,000-mile warranty.

What vehicles do you work on?

Domestic, Asian and European cars, trucks and SUVs, plus fleet vehicles.

Do you do state inspections?

Yes, the shop holds a Texas Official Vehicle Inspection License. Call first and ask about the wait.

Do you work with fleets?

Yes. We offer fleet pricing, accept all fleet account payments and work with insurance and warranty companies. Estimates by the next business day.

Are there any discounts?

Yes, for teachers, military, first responders and AAA members. Ask when you call.

What can I do while I wait?

There's free Wi-Fi, bottled water and coffee, and a playroom for the kids.

Are you open on weekends?

No, we're open Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM–5:30 PM. Holiday hours may differ, so call ahead.

Car acting up?

Call and tell us what you're noticing. We'll tell you plainly what's going on and what to do next.
