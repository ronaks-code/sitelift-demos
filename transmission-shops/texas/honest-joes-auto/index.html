Who we are

Joe, and a name to live up to

Honest Joe's Auto is a completely mobile mechanic service. As the name says, Joe's promise is simple: I will take care of you honestly.

Joe has 15+ years of experience and is factory-trained with Chrysler, Jeep, Dodge and Toyota. Steering, suspension and brakes are his specialty.

If a job needs a shop, he says so instead of attempting something that will not hold.
