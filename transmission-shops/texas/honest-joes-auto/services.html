Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Joe comes to your home, workplace or roadside location in Bastrop County, so there is no towing and no shop wait.

What does the diagnostic include?

An on-site evaluation with scan tools and a hands-on inspection, a clear explanation and a written quote before any work. $150 flat fee.

Is there a travel charge?

The diagnostic covers the base service area. Beyond it, a charge of $25 per additional 30 minutes of drive time may apply, and it is discussed before booking.

What if my car needs a shop?

Joe tells you honestly instead of attempting a repair that will not hold.

What cars does Joe work on?

All makes and models. He is factory-trained with Chrysler, Jeep, Dodge and Toyota, and also repairs small engines and farm equipment.

What are the hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
