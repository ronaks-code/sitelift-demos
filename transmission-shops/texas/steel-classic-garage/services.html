Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. The shop is small and fills up fast, so it works by appointment only.

Can you give an estimate over the phone?

No. Without seeing the car in person there is no way to give an accurate estimate, so they do not quote by phone.

What cars do you work on?

British, European and American classics, including Triumph, MG, Chevrolet, Ford, Dodge, Plymouth, Oldsmobile, Sunbeam, Volkswagen, Datsun and Pontiac.

Do you do engine swaps and conversions?

Yes: classic to classic swaps and modern conversions like GM LS and Ford Coyote.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Closed on weekends.

Where are you?
