Good to know

Questions people ask

What are the signs my transmission is going bad?

Delayed or slipping shifts, rough shifting, strange noises, a burning smell, fluid leaks or a warning light. Have it checked before more damage is done.

Should I repair or rebuild?

It depends on the condition. The shop does thorough diagnostics first, then explains your options so you can decide.

Do I get an estimate first?

Yes. The shop gives a detailed estimate before any work begins.

Is it safe to drive with transmission problems?

Slipping, delayed engagement or warning lights should not be ignored. Bring it in as soon as you can for an inspection.

Do you work on manual transmissions?

No. The shop focuses on modern automatic systems, for cars and for trucks.

Do you work on trucks?

Yes. Chevy, RAM, Ford and GMC trucks, including diesel.

Se habla español?

Sí. Hablamos Español. Call the shop and ask.

What if I need to drop off after hours?

There is a secure key drop box at the shop.
