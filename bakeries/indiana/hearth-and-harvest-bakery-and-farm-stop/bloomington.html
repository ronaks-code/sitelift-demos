Bloomington questions

Good to know

How far is Hearth & Harvest from Bloomington?

We’re in Ellettsville at 202 W Temperance St, about 15 minutes from Bloomington (west on IN-46). Parking is right out front.

What should I grab on my first visit?

A loaf of cinnamon raisin bread, one of the giant chocolate chip cookies and a bottle of Twin Springs chocolate milk. Add focaccia if it’s out.

Can I order ahead so it’s ready when I arrive?

Yes. Send a pre-order or call (812) 269-8616 and we’ll set it aside.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday 11 am to 5 pm and Saturday 9 am to 4 pm. Closed Sunday.
