Questions people ask

Do you fix flats?

Yes. Fix flats is first on the sign out front. Call ahead and we will tell you how long it will take.

Do you sell used tires?

Yes. BR Tire Shop sells new and used tires. Call with your tire size and ask what is in stock.

Do you balance tires?

Yes. Computer balancing is on the sign. Tires can be removed, replaced and balanced in one visit.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes. Check the hours on this page, and call ahead on holidays.

Do you do brakes and oil changes?

Yes. Brakes, drums and rotors, and oil changes are all on the list.
