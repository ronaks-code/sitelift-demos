Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you patch flat tires?

Yes. Patches are quick, and sometimes you don't have to get out of your car. Call ahead with your tire size.

Can you fix a cracked rim?

Yes. We repair rims, or put your tire on a new rim if needed.

Do you sell new tires?

Yes. Tell us your vehicle and tire size. Next-day tires are often possible.

Do you do mufflers?

Yes. The storefront says Custom Mufflers, and the shop is a muffler and tire shop.

Are you open Sundays?

Yes. Sunday 8 AM to 5:30 PM. Monday to Saturday 8 AM to 6:30 PM. Holiday hours may differ, so call ahead.
