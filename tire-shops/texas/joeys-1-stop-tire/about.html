About Joey's

New tires from all the brands and used tires, flat and wheel repair, rotation and balancing, emissions inspections, headlights, wipers and bulbs, and trailer repair. No appointment needed: pull up to the red-and-white shops under the oak tree at 2103 N Bell Blvd and we'll take a look.

Our story

Family owned and operated since 2021

Joey's 1 Stop Tire is a family-owned and operated tire shop at 2103 N Bell Blvd in Cedar Park, and has been since 2021. Look for the JOEY'S 1 STOP sign on the limestone pillars by the road, then the two red-trimmed metal shops and the limestone office under the big oak tree, with new tires stacked out front.

We sell new tires from all the brands and keep racks of used ones inside. We fix flats and wheels, rotate and balance, do emissions inspections, replace headlights, taillights, wiper blades and bulbs, and handle trailer repair. No appointment needed.

Friendly people. Quick service. Fair prices. Most visits take under half an hour.
