Good to know

Questions people ask

How fast can you patch a tire?

Usually 5 to 20 minutes.

Do you sell used tires?

Yes. We sell new and used tires, and do brakes and oil changes too.

How do I find my tire size?

It is printed on the tire sidewall. Many vehicles also list it on a sticker inside the driver's door.

Can you help me pick a tire?

Yes. Eric helps you find the exact tire for your vehicle.

Where are you?

1688 State Highway 71 in Cedar Creek, Texas. Look for the yellow building with the red base.

How much will it cost?
