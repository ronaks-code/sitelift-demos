The shop on the frontage road

Dave's Ultimate Automotive sits on the south I-35 frontage road in Round Rock. The sign out front says Repair, Maintenance, Inspections and Tires.

Friendly staff who laugh while they work. We test before we replace, and we say so when the fix is simpler than expected.

No talking down to anyone who only knows car basics. Straightforward about what actually needs fixing.

  • Tested first

    We test first, so you replace only what's needed.

  • Plain talk

    We explain the car's condition and the price up front.

  • No appointment

    Walk-ins are welcome. Calling first is quickest.
