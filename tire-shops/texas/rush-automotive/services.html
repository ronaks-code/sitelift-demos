Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you do alignments?

Yes. Alignments are done on their own or with other work, in the time quoted.

Can you replace a tie rod?

Yes. Tie rod replacement is one of the jobs drivers mention by name.

Do you repair A/C?

Yes. We repair A/C, including on European cars.

Will you push work I do not need?

No. If a repair can wait, we say so.

Can I book an oil change online?

Yes. You can schedule an oil change online, or call the shop to book.

Are you open on weekends?

No. We are open Monday to Friday, 8 AM–6 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may differ.
