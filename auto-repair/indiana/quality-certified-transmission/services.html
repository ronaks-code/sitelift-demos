Questions people ask

What do you repair?

Automatic transmissions, manual transmissions, CV joints and axles, drivetrain work and rebuilt transmissions, including CVT services.

Do you install rebuilt transmissions?

Yes. The shop installs transmissions rebuilt by Jasper Engine and Transmission.

Do you work on diesel transmissions?

Yes. The team provides upgrades such as billet steel input and output shafts, high capacity clutch packs and billet multi-disc torque converters.

Which towns do you serve?

Bloomington, Martinsville, Ellettsville and Spencer, Indiana.

No. Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
