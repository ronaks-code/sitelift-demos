Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment?

Oil and filter changes are by appointment. For anything else, call the shop or send a request and we will tell you what to expect.

Can I drop off after hours?

Yes. There is a safe, secure after-hours drop-off. Call the shop and we will explain how it works.

Which makes and models do you work on?

Most of them, including Ford, Chevrolet, GMC, Toyota, Honda and Subaru, from compact cars to trucks and SUVs.

Will you tell me what can wait?

Yes. We list what needs attention now and what can wait, with a clear estimate, and you decide.

Can you service a car that is under factory warranty?

Yes. Factory scheduled maintenance at an independent shop is part of what we do.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes, 5% off your repair for military service members. Mention it when you book.

Can I pay over time?

Flexible financing with Buy Now, Pay Later is available. Ask us about it.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
