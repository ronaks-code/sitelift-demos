Good to know

Questions people ask

What cars do you work on?

All makes and models, with deeper expertise in European, BMW and JDM platforms.

Do you do custom tuning?

Yes. Tuning is done in house, with custom calibrations built around your car and its modifications.

Can you fabricate a custom part?

Yes. The shop has in-house CAD, CNC and welding for custom components, turbo setups and engine swaps.

Will I hear how my car is going?

Yes. You get updates throughout, including during a parts delay.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 9 AM–7 PM, and closed on weekends. Call ahead around holidays.

Do you handle everyday maintenance too?

Yes. Oil changes, fluid service and scheduled maintenance are part of the work.
