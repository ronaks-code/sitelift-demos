(04) About

Built around German cars

Open since 2005 and German cars only. The team is ASE-certified and uses manufacturer-specific diagnostic equipment and German parts.

Owner James Weinfurt is a hands-on technician and a Porsche track driver. Methodical, and straight about what a car needs.

Qualifying repairs carry a 2-year, 24,000-mile warranty. Financing and military discounts are available, and the waiting room has Wi-Fi.
