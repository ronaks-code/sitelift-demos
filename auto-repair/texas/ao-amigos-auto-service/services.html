Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work on my kind of car?

The shop works on most makes and models: domestic, import, classics and trucks.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. AO Amigos serves customers in English and Spanish.

Do you give quotes before the work?

Yes. You get a clear price and no upselling. Call with your car's year, make and model and what it is doing.

Can you help if I am stranded?

The shop offers roadside assistance and lockout service. Call to ask what is possible where you are.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sunday.
