Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you work on all makes and models?

Yes. The shop lists all makes and models, including gas, hybrid and electric vehicles.

Is there a warranty?

The shop offers a 36-month/36,000-mile nationwide warranty. Ask what applies to your repair.

My check-engine light is on. What now?

Bring the car in. The team finds the cause first, then explains what it needs.

Do you do state inspections?

Yes. State inspections are on the shop's service list, along with oil changes.

Are you open on weekends?

No. The shop is open weekdays and closed Saturday and Sunday. Call ahead around holidays.

Where are you?

On State Highway 71 in Bee Cave. The same team also runs shops in Austin, Leander and Dripping Springs.
