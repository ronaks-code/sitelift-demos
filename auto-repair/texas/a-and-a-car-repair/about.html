Our story

A neighborhood shop on Sunrise Rd

A & A Car Repair is a neighborhood auto repair shop at 4100 Sunrise Rd in Round Rock. Look for the hand-painted A & A sign with the old Beetle on it, right next to the open bay.

Customers mention Francisco by name again and again. He looks the car over, shows you what's going on, and tells you straight what it needs. If you bought a part you don't need, he'll say so. If a job is outside what the shop does, he'll point you to someone who can do it.

That's why people keep coming back. One customer has brought every car repair and maintenance job to A & A for about five years and says they're the only ones he trusts with his car.
