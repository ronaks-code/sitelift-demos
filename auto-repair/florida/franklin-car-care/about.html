The shop

Do it right, or twice

Our saying is "do it right or do it twice." The technicians aim to fix the problem the first time.

Friendly, honest service. Repairs are explained, and an estimate comes before work begins.

The shop is a NAPA AutoCare Center, so qualifying repairs carry a 24 month, 24,000 mile warranty that other NAPA shops honor if you are traveling.

  • Explained up frontYou hear exactly what the car needs before we start.
  • Fixed fastQuick turnaround, including AC repairs.
  • Backed by a warrantyNAPA AutoCare: 24 months or 24,000 miles, qualifying repairs.
