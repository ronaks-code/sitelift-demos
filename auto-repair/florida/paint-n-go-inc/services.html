Good to know

Questions people ask

What can be repaired on site?

Cosmetic bumper, fender, door, trunk and quarter-panel dents, scrapes and paint damage, on eligible vehicles.

When does a car need a body shop?

Structural or frame damage, deployed airbags, mechanical damage or a car that is unsafe to drive usually needs a traditional shop.

How do I get an estimate?

Send photos of the whole damaged area and a close-up, plus year, make and model. We text you a written estimate.

Do I have to send photos first?

No. Photos are optional when you submit a request, and you can send them later by text.
