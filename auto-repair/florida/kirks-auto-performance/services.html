Questions people ask

How do I reach you?

Call the shop or send the message form. A phone call is the fastest way to get help.

Can you repair a seized engine?

Sometimes. If the pistons and crankshaft are not damaged, a rebuild can bring it back. Call and tell us what happened.

Do you work on exotics?

Yes. Tell us the car and the concern so we can source parts and schedule a drop-off.

What do you tune?

Mopar muscle and other platforms: dyno tuning, ECM tuning and upgrades built around your goals.

What does an oil change include?

A vehicle inspection comes with every oil change.
