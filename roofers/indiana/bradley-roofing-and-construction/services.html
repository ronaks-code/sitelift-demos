Good to know

Questions people ask

What do you work on?

Roofing, siding and gutters, plus decks and pergolas. Cedar siding repairs too.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes. We walk you through the claim process and point out damage you might miss.

Do you work on storm damage?

Yes. The company assesses wind and hail damage on houses and outbuildings such as metal barn roofs.

Who runs the company?

Joe Bradley founded it over 35 years ago, and his son Sam Bradley carries it on.

How do I get a quote?

Call or send a message with a short description of the job. Photos help.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM–5 PM, closed weekends. Holiday hours may differ, so call ahead.
