About

The people behind Pritchett Brothers.

The crew

A Bedford family business since 1963

Three brothers, Richard, Charles and Earl Pritchett, started it. From 1996 to 2007 we were Phase I roofing contractor on the West Baden Springs Hotel restoration.

Today: roof replacement and repair, gutters, siding and storm damage restoration. We are a GAF Master Elite contractor.
