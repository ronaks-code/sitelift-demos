Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 3:30 PM. Closed on weekends.

How do I get a quote?

Call the office or send the request form on the contact page with a short description of the job. Photos help.

Do you work on storm damage?

Yes. Storm damage restoration is a specialty, with quick tarps and help working with your insurance.

What kinds of roofs do you do?

Asphalt shingle, metal, TPO and flat roofs, for homes and for commercial buildings.

What siding do you install?

Vinyl, LP SmartSide and James Hardie fiber cement, plus repair of storm-damaged panels.

Do you do windows and doors?

Yes. Pella replacement windows and entry, patio and sliding doors.

Where do you work?

The office is in Bedford, Indiana, and crews serve southern Indiana including Bloomington. Call to check that your address is covered.

How much will it cost?

Every roof and house is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a request for an estimate.

Roof or siding giving you trouble?

Call Pritchett Brothers. Family-owned and working in southern Indiana since 1963.
