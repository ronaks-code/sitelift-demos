Good to know

Questions people ask

What areas do you serve?

Greenwood and the Indianapolis area, plus Bloomington and Worthington, and nearby communities.

Do you offer free inspections?

Yes. A free roof inspection comes with no obligation.

Do you handle storm and hail damage?

Yes. The team inspects for hail and wind damage, documents it for insurance claims and offers emergency tarping and temporary patches.

What materials do you use?

GAF and Malarkey roofing materials.

Do you do commercial roofs?

Yes. TPO, PVC, EPDM, metal and asphalt roofs, including churches.

Do you do more than roofing?

Exterior services include siding, gutters, windows and insulation.

How much will it cost?

Every roof is different. Ask for a free inspection and an estimate.
