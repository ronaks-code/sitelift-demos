About

A family-owned crew

Equity Builders Roofing is family owned and says it has been Bloomington's roofer for over three decades. Doug Schmidt owns the company, and AJ is president.

Two in-house crews handle roofing and remodeling, from asphalt shingles, cedar shakes and metal to rubber and low-slope roofs.

No money up front. We fund the project and invoice when the job is done and the cleanup passes, then fix any problems later.
