Services

Tune-ups, soft wash and preservation

Three ways to get more years out of the shingles you already have.

01

Roof tune-up and repair

Sealing, nailing and replacing what is worn so small problems do not become leaks.

  • Chimney flashing re-sealed
  • Loose shingles sealed down
  • Popped nails fixed
  • Dry-rotted pipe boots replaced
02

Renueit 17 preservation

A shingle preservation treatment applied after the tune-up and soft wash, meant to extend the life of qualifying asphalt roofs.

  • Detailed assessment first
  • 17 years of coverage, one application
  • Wood, siding and metal too
03

Roof soft wash

Low-pressure washing to clear algae, moss and dirt from shingles before any treatment goes on.

  • Algae and moss
  • Dirt and black streaks
  • Low-pressure technique

How it goes

From first look to treated roof

  1. 1

    A detailed assessment

    The crew inspects the roof and shares what they find, so you know if Roof Savers is the right fit.

  2. 2

    Tune-up and soft wash

    Flashings and loose shingles sealed, popped nails fixed, worn pipe boots replaced, then the roof is washed.

  3. 3

    Treatment applied

    Once the roof is prepared, the Renueit 17 restoration treatment goes on.
