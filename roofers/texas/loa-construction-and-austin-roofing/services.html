Good to know

Questions people ask

Is the roof inspection free?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation inspections and pressure-free estimates.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We assist with insurance claims for hail damage and guide you through the process.

What roof types do you work with?

Asphalt shingle, metal, clay tile and flat roofing for homes. Metal, TPO, EPDM and built-up systems for commercial buildings. We also apply roof coatings.

Do you do commercial roofs?

Yes. We install and service commercial roofing systems.

Do you offer a warranty?

Our site advertises a lifetime workmanship warranty. Ask us for the details when you book your inspection.

Can I call about an emergency?

Yes. We are available 24/7 for emergency calls.

Where do you work?

Greater Austin and Central Texas, from Round Rock to Dripping Springs to Bastrop. We also take jobs in Houston, San Antonio and Dallas.

How much will it cost?

We do not post prices. We inspect the roof, give you an estimate and let you decide.

Hail or storm damage?

Get a free inspection, and call any time for an emergency.
