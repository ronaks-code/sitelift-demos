About

Bastrop's longest-running roofer

Working on Bastrop homes since 2008. Rick started the business, going door to door after storms and staying active in the Rotary Club and Chamber of Commerce.

Dan owns it now. He keeps the same habit: the team takes small jobs that solve a problem for the customer.
