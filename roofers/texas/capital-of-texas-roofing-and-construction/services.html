Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 5:30pm.

What if I have storm damage or an active leak?

We offer 24/7 emergency support for urgent storm damage, tarping and leak repair. Call the office to be prioritized.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. The estimate is free and carries no obligation. A local project manager will schedule your inspection first.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes, the team can help you navigate an insurance claim, and the inspectors are Haag Certified for wind and hail damage.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. The company backs its work with a lifetime labor warranty.

Do you only do roofs?

No. The crew also does siding, gutters, decks, patios, outdoor kitchens and driveways, and repairs, replaces and maintains commercial roofs.

Where do you work?

Bastrop and the surrounding area, including Cedar Creek, Elgin, Smithville, Paige and Wyldwood, plus Greater Austin and Central Texas.

How much will it cost?

Every home is different, so no prices are posted here. Call or send a request and you will get a quote after the inspection.

Roof leaking or storm damaged?

Call or send a message. The office is open Monday to Friday, and urgent storm damage gets emergency support.
