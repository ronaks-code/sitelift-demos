Good to know

Questions people ask

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Ace offers free estimates. Call or send the form and someone comes to look at your roof.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. The team goes through the paperwork and works with your insurance company after hail damage. Ask about your claim.

What kinds of roofs do you do?

Asphalt and fiberglass shingles, clay and concrete tile, metal, and single and multi-ply flat roofs.

Do you do commercial roofs?

Yes. Ace does residential and commercial roofing.

Do you do gutters and siding?

Gutter repair, gutter replacement and siding are on Ace's list of services. Ask when you call.

Can I talk to past customers?

Yes. We give references on request.

We are on Burnet Road in Austin and work across the Austin area. Call to check your address.
