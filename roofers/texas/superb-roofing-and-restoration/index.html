About us

A family business named for family

Josh and Brianna Janczewski run Superb Roofing & Restoration from New Braunfels as a Christian, family-owned company where faith, family and community come first.

Brianna manages the office, Emmanuel coordinates projects and George is the inspector and solar specialist. The whole team works with your insurance company.
