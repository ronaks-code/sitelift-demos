Good to know

Questions people ask

Is the roof inspection free?

Yes. Call or send a note and the team will set up a free roof inspection.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We work with your insurance company to make the process painless.

How far do you travel?

New Braunfels, Marion, Cibolo, Spring Branch, Canyon Lake and the surrounding areas, within about 60 miles of the office.

Do you do metal roofing?

Yes, along with shingle roofs, repairs and solar installation.

What if a storm is coming and my roof is open?

Call. We can tarp an exposed area right away, then come back to patch it.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, closed Sunday. Holiday hours may differ, so call ahead.

Worried about your roof?

Call or send a note. The first inspection is free.
