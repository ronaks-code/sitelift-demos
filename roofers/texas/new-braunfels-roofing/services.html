Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer free inspections?

Yes. The shop invites you to schedule a free inspection. Call or send a request with your address.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We write a detailed report for the adjuster, can be at the house for the visit, and work through the claim with you.

What roofing materials do you install?

Asphalt shingles and metal roofing, plus seamless gutters.

Do you replace gutters too?

Yes. Seamless gutter installation, repair and maintenance are part of the work.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Where do you work?

New Braunfels and surrounding areas, including Seguin, Schertz, San Marcos and Converse. Call if you are not sure you are covered.
