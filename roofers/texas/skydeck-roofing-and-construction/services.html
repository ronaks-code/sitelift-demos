Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, and Saturday 9am to 4pm.

Where do you work?

We are based in Kyle and serve Buda, Austin, Lakeway, West Lake Hills, Manor, San Marcos, Pflugerville, Round Rock and Dripping Springs.

Do you offer free inspections and quotes?

Yes. We schedule a free on-site inspection, then prepare a quote.

Can you help after a storm?

Yes. Storm restoration is one of our services. Call and describe the damage.

Do you only do roofs?

No. We also build and repair decks and fences, and handle gutters, gazebos, pergolas and sheds.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes, residential and commercial.

How much will it cost?

Every job is different, so we do not post prices. Request a free quote and we will talk through the options.

Roof leaking or fence down?

Call or text SkyDeck and Oscar's crew will set up a free inspection.
