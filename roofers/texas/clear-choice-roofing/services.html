Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you do emergency roofing?

Yes. Emergency roofing is available 24/7, and we are open 24 hours.

Do you repair as well as replace roofs?

Yes. Chimney flashing, temporary leak patches and full replacements.

What roof types do you work on?

Shingle, metal and tile roofs, plus commercial systems such as TPO, snap lock and double lock panels.

Do you install gutters or insulation?

Yes. Seamless gutters and attic insulation are on the service list, often paired with a new roof.

How much does a roof cost?

It depends on the size and materials, so we do not post a number here. Call for a free quote or inspection.

Where do you work?

Austin and the surrounding area, including Del Valle, Creedmoor, Manor and Bee Cave.

Who do I talk to first?

Call the office or send the request form. The office process is easy and smooth.

Leak or storm damage?

Call Clear Choice Roofing any hour. The line is open 24/7 for urgent roofing.
