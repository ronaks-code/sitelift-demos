Good to know

Questions people ask

How do I get a roof inspection?

Call or send the request form. Inspections are free, and the team often uses a drone to check the whole roof.

Will you tell me if I do not need a new roof?

Yes. If a roof has only minor issues, we say so instead of pushing a replacement.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. WDR works with all major insurance companies and documents hail, storm and leak damage for your claim.

Are you open weekends and nights?

The phone is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and emergency response is available for storm damage and leaks.

What roof types do you work on?

Asphalt shingle, metal, tile and flat roofs, for homes and commercial properties.

Do you also handle water damage inside the house?

Yes. WDR does water damage restoration, so one company can repair the roof and the ceiling.

How much will it cost?

Every roof is different, so we do not post prices. Request a free inspection and we will walk you through the estimate.

Leak or storm damage?

Call WDR any hour. Free roof inspections, and a straight answer on what the roof needs.
