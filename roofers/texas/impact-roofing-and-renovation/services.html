Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you charge for an inspection?

The inspection is free. The team goes up on the roof themselves, explains what they see in plain English and does not pressure you into anything.

How much will a new roof cost?

It depends on the measured roof area, pitch, access, tear-off, decking, ventilation, flashing and material. Ask for a free, detailed written estimate for your house.

Do you work with insurance claims?

If insurance is involved, the team provides photos and construction documentation and keeps its roofing scope clear.

Is there emergency service?

The team lists 24/7 emergency response for active leaks and storm damage. Call and explain what is happening.

Do you do more than roofs?

Yes. The sister brand Impact Renovations handles kitchen and bathroom remodels, additions, painting and siding.

Where do you work?

Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Spicewood, Cedar Park, Westlake, Round Rock, Austin and the Texas Hill Country.
