Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you do free inspections?

Yes. Free roof inspections and estimates. We check the whole roof and explain what we find.

Will you tell me if I only need a repair?

Yes. If a repair will do, that's what we recommend. No upsell on a replacement you don't need.

How much does a small repair cost?

Small roof repairs start at $250 and most run $250 to $1,200, quoted before work starts. Replacements are estimated after an inspection.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes. We help with insurance claims after hail and wind storms.

Will I see how the job is going?

Yes. We send drone photos of the roof at each stage while the crew works.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every project carries a 2-year labor warranty, plus manufacturer warranties on materials.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 4 PM. Closed Sunday.
