Good to know

Questions people ask

Are you veteran-owned?

Yes. Rooftop is veteran-owned, SBA certified as a service-disabled veteran-owned small business, and takes part in the GAF Roofs for Troops program for military families.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes, as your contractor. With your permission the team sends photos, the repair estimate and construction documents to your insurer or adjuster. Not public adjusting.

What about storm damage?

We offer emergency tarping, leak control, shingle replacement and full roof inspections after a storm. Call to ask what is available.

Do you do more than roofs?

Yes: gutters, siding, windows, fascia and soffit, patio covers, pergolas, fencing, painting, kitchen and bath remodels and garage conversions.

Is financing available?

Yes. Flexible financing and monthly payment plans are available. Ask when you request an estimate.

Where do you work?

San Antonio, Schertz, New Braunfels, Cibolo, Converse, Helotes, Universal City, Garden Ridge, Alamo Heights, San Marcos, Selma, Live Oak and more. Call to check your address.

How much will it cost?

Every project is different, so we do not post prices. Ask for a free estimate and you will get a clear written one.
