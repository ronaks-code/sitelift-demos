Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 2 PM. Closed on Sundays.

Do I need plans before I call?

No. Many conversations start with an idea. Call or send a message and we will work out the next step.

Do you do both outdoor living and roofing?

Yes. Two specialties, one standard: design-build outdoor living, and residential and commercial roofing under the same ownership.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We apply for and close out electrical, water and building permits. Requirements differ by jurisdiction, so we confirm them property by property.

Do you install motorized screens?

Yes. TWT is an authorized MagnaTrack dealer for motorized and retractable screens.

Where do you work?

Lakeway, Rough Hollow, Bee Cave, Westlake, Dripping Springs, Cedar Park, Leander, Georgetown, Round Rock and Austin. Call to check your address.

How much will it cost?

Every property is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a message and we will talk through what you want to build.
