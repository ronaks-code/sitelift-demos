Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you fix leaks that other roofers couldn't?

Yes. We trace the real source, even on flat roofs that past repairs missed.

Do you test the repair?

Yes. The crew runs a hose over the roof to confirm the leak is gone.

Yes. We help with the claim and the appraisal process, even after a denial.

Yes. We can patch a roof ahead of heavy rain.
