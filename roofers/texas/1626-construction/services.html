The method

From first visit to final walkthrough

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    We visit your home, look at the space and talk through your goals and budget.

  2. 2

    Design and selections

    We help choose materials, finishes and layout, with 3D tools to preview the result.

  3. 3

    Written estimate

    A line-item scope with materials and a clear payment schedule.

  4. 4

    Demo and build

    Our crew handles demo, prep and construction and keeps the site clean.

  5. 5

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished space with you and address every punch list item.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Who will I work with?

Rudy, your responsive project manager. He sets clear expectations on timeline, scope and cost, and works with your designer.

Will I get a written estimate?

Yes. You get a detailed written estimate with line-item scope and materials before work begins.

Where do you work?

Austin, Buda, Kyle, San Marcos, Dripping Springs, Lakeway, Wimberley and surrounding Central Texas communities.

What can I send with my request?

Photos or a Pinterest idea of what you picture. We also ask for a phone number and project address.
