Good to know

Questions people ask

What happens in a roof walkthrough?

We inspect your roof, take photos and video, then walk you through what we found, one area at a time.

Will you push me to replace the roof?

No. You get notes on must-do versus recommended repairs, so some work can wait.

Can I see the work after it is done?

Yes. An online portal shows the work done on your roof.

Do you handle storm damage?

Yes. Storm damage repair is one of the listed services. Call or send a message and describe what you have noticed.

What is a roof report?

Roof reports are one of the listed services. Ask the team what yours would include.

Do you offer financing and warranties?

The team has a page about warranties and financing. Call and ask what applies to your project.

Not sure what your roof needs?

We will walk it with you, show you what is going on and help you decide what makes sense.
