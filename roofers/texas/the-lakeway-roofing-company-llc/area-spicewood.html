What we do

Repair, replace, metal and commercial

Shingle, metal and tile roofs for homes and businesses around Lake Travis.

  1. 1

    Roof repair

    Leaks, hail and wind damage, failed flashing and cracked pipe boots, traced to where the water really gets in.

    • Leak tracing
    • Flashing
    • Storm damage
  2. 2

    Roof replacement

    A full tear-off to the deck, new underlayment, flashing and ventilation, with Class 4 impact-rated shingle among the options.

    • Full tear-off
    • Deck inspection
    • Class 4 shingle
  3. 3

    Metal and tile roofing

    Standing-seam metal for hail and wind resistance, plus careful repair of clay and concrete tile.

    • Standing seam
    • Clay tile
    • Concrete tile
  4. 4

    Commercial roofing

    Flat and low-slope systems for businesses and multi-family property, from TPO to modified bitumen.
