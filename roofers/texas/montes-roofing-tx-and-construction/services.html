Good to know

Questions people ask

How do I get a quote?

Call or text, or send the request form on the contact page. Tell us what the roof is doing.

Where do you work?

San Antonio and the surrounding areas. We are based in Live Oak, TX.

What kinds of roofs do you work on?

Asphalt, clay tile, concrete tile, wood shakes and slate, plus flat roofs.

Do you repair leaks?

Yes. Roof leak repair is one of our main jobs.

Will you push work I do not need?

No. Manuel explains what needs fixing and what does not. Ask him to walk you through the repair.

Is there a warranty?

Repairs can come with a 1 year warranty. Ask about it when you call.

How much will it cost?

Every roof is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a request and we will talk through it.
