About

Family owned, 13 years on roofs

Montes Roofing is a family-owned roofing company with over 13 years of experience. We work on homes in San Antonio and the surrounding areas, from Live Oak.

We do residential roofing, flat roofs, roof leak repairs and custom installs. We work with asphalt, clay tile, concrete tile, wood shakes and slate.

We explain the work, keep communication clear and take pride in every job. Small jobs get the same care as big ones.
