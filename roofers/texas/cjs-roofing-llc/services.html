Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer a free quote?

Yes. The inspection is free, and the professional quote is the price you pay. Call or send photos.

Can you tarp my roof after a storm?

Emergency tarps are offered within 24 hours. Call as soon as you see damage.

Are you GAF Certified?

Yes. CJ's Roofing is GAF Certified, and its site says the company is licensed, bonded and insured.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. Carlos helps work with your insurer so the settlement is accurate.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Se habla español.

What areas do you serve?

San Antonio and Central and South Texas, including Converse, Schertz, Boerne and New Braunfels. Call if you are not sure you are covered.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sunday.
