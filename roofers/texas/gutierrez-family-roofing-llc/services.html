Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7am to 6pm, Saturday 8am to noon, closed Sunday.

Do you work on metal roofs?

Yes. We repair metal roofs and can add maintenance. Call to ask about yours.

Do you only do big replacements?

No. We also do smaller jobs: maintenance, wind repairs and dryer vent flashing. Call and describe what you have.

Will I see what you found?

Yes. Victor takes photos of everything and keeps you updated at each step.

Where do you work?

Gutierrez Family Roofing is based in Manchaca, Texas and serves the area around it. Call to check your address.

How much will it cost?

Every roof is different, so we do not post prices. Call or send a message and we will talk it through.
