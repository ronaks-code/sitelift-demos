Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 7 PM, and Saturday 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed on Sundays.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes. Every inspection is free and comes with photos, a 4 page PDF report and a straight repair-or-replace recommendation.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Replacements start at $7900+ and are priced on actual scope: roof size, pitch, materials and condition. You get a documented quote after a free inspection.

Can my roof be repaired instead of replaced?

Often, yes. Repairs start at $395+, and repair is the first recommendation whenever it will solve the problem.

Can you help after hail or storm damage?

Yes. We photo-document the damage and write a detailed estimate to share with your insurer. We can meet your adjuster on the roof.

Which shingle brands do you install?

Certified installer for IKO, Owens Corning, CertainTeed and TAMKO, and any major shingle brand your home or insurance scope calls for.

Where do you work?

Austin and the Hill Country, including Lakeway, Cedar Park, Leander, Georgetown, Round Rock, Bee Cave and Marble Falls. Call to check your address.

Not sure about your roof?

Call or text for a free inspection and a straight answer, even if the answer is that your roof is fine.
