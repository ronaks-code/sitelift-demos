Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Every project starts with a complimentary consultation call about your timeline, budget and whether insurance is involved.

What roofs do you work on?

Composition asphalt shingle roofs, residential and commercial properties around Round Rock.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Roof Masters is HAAG Certified, which equips the team to assess storm damage accurately and support insurance documentation. Say on the consultation call if a carrier is involved.

Do you do work inside the house too?

Yes. Sheetrock repairs, interior painting and exterior painting, so storm damage is handled in one project.
