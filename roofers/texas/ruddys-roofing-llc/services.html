Questions people ask

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. There is no charge to come out, and if the roof is fine, that is what you are told.

Do I pay anything to start?

No. Ruddy's needs no money to start. Your written agreement says when payment is due.

How long does a replacement take?

Most roofs are done in one day, depending on the weather.

Should I sign with a door knocker after a hail storm?

Their advice: do not sign anything a door knocker hands you, it is a contingency agreement. Have them look before you file a claim.

Is the work warranted?

Workmanship is warranted for two years from the day the job is done. Extended warranties are available on request.
