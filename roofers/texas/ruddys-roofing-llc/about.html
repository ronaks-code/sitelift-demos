The crew

A father and son crew

Ruddy's Roofing is a father and son company out of Round Rock. Frank answers the phone. Jake comes out to look, and runs the job.

The name is a nickname. Frank "Ruddy" Hargis has carried it since boyhood, when his big brother said his cowlick looked like a Ruddy Duck.

One main crew, never storm chasers. Frank has been heard on KLBJ and ESPN Radio.

  1. 1Honest scope

    Four shingles, not a whole roof, when that is all it needs.

  2. 2Clear communication

    Quick calls. You know when the crew is coming.

  3. 3Clean finish

    Most roofs done in a day, magnet pass after.
