Questions people ask

Is the roof inspection free?

Yes. We offer free roof inspections, honest and no pressure.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. For storm or hail damage, we help document it and work with you through the claim, including paperwork for insurance discounts.

Do you repair roofs or only replace them?

Both. We do quick repairs and full replacements, and we inspect too.

Where do you work?

We are based in Kyle and serve Kyle, Uhland, Buda, San Marcos and surrounding Central Texas communities.

What are your hours?

Our site lists 7am to 7pm, seven days a week. Call or text to be sure.

Will I see what you did?

Yes. We send photos of the roof before, during and after the work.

We don't post prices. We look at the roof and give you a quick, detailed quote.
