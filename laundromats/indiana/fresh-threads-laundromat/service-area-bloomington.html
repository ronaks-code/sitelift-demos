Fresh Threads Laundromat

Laundromat in Bloomington, IN

Fresh Threads Laundromat is a self-service laundromat at 223 S Pete Ellis Dr on Bloomington's east side, an easy drive from Bloomington. Big washers, Speed Queen dryers, card and QR pay and a friendly attendant, open every day 6 AM to 11 PM.

What's inside

Machines for Bloomington neighbors

Big washers

Front-load and top-load washers for everyday loads, towels and bulky bedding.

Speed Queen dryers

A long wall of stacked Speed Queen dryers, so you are rarely waiting for one.

Card, QR or quarters

Pay with a credit or debit card, scan a QR code with your phone, or grab quarters at the change machine.

Folding tables & carts

Mid-century folding tables in tomato red and sage green, plus rolling carts and hanging racks.

Friendly attendant

An attendant on the floor most days to help with machines, change and first-time questions.

A comfy wait

Retro molded chairs by the windows, a TV, a Coke machine and a clean restroom.
