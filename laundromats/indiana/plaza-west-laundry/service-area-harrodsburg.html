Laundromat in Harrodsburg, IN

Plaza West Laundry is a self-service laundromat on S Leonard Springs Rd on Bloomington's west side, an easy drive from Harrodsburg. Big machines, card pay and parking at the door, open 7 days a week.

What's inside

Machines for Harrodsburg neighbors

Top-load washers

Rows of Speed Queen top-loaders.

Big front-load washers

Dexter Express 30 lb front-loaders for the big loads: bedding, towels and a week of everything.

Dryers

A full wall of stacked dryers that keep turning on and off after the cycle ends, so clothes don't wrinkle.

Card pay & rewards

Swipe a debit or credit card right at the machine, or load a laundry card and earn reward points.

Soap & supplies

Forgot something? The vending center has detergent, bleach, softener and laundry bags.

Folding & seating

Long folding tables, red chairs to wait in, outlets on the front wall and a restroom on site.
