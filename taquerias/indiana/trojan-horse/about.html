About the Trojan Horse

A locally owned Greek restaurant and tavern in Bloomington.

A neighborhood Greek tavern

Locally owned and operated. Greek specialties and American favorites in a friendly, neighborhood room.

Come for a beer or a full meal. Pub vibes, an accommodating staff, and a quiet, lovely upstairs.

The Trojan Horse also supports local programs and charities, and looks over requests for funds, gift cards or food for an event every two weeks.

  • Friendly staffAn accommodating team that looks after you.
  • Fair pricesReasonable menu prices.
  • Gives backSupports local programs and charities all year.
