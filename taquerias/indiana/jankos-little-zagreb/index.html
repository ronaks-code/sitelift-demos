Our story

Downtown's old steakhouse

Janko's (say yawn-koes) opened in 1973 as the Choo-Choo Cafe, serving Eastern European food. The menu settled on steaks, with the spicy meatballs still here.

The grill is open to the front of the room, so you smell steak at the door. Casual, family friendly, with room for groups.

  • Hand cutUSDA Choice steaks, cut daily and grilled.
  • Spicy meatballsA house favorite from the Eastern European days.
  • Since 1973Over 40 years in the heart of downtown Bloomington.
