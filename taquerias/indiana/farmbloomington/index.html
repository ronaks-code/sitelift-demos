Behind the table

Founded in 2008, Oddfellows building

Opened in 2008 in the old Oddfellows building. The design reuses original artifacts, so a piece of downtown history sits in the dining room.

Chef and owner Daniel Orr and the FARMteam cook with Southern Indiana ingredients and foraged herbs. Downstairs, the Root Cellar Lounge.

  • Local ingredientsProduce and products from Indiana farmers, sourced close to home.
  • Sustainable since day oneCompostable to-go containers, plus recycling of glass, tin, plastic and paper.
  • Hoosier hospitalityParties from 2 to 600. Attentive, friendly servers.

Before you book

Good to know

What days are you open?

Tuesday through Sunday. We are closed Mondays.

When is brunch?

Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM.

When does dinner start?

Dinner service starts at 5 PM, Tuesday through Sunday, with a small plates menu that changes every day.

Do you host private parties?

Yes. Our FARMteam takes care of parties from 2 to 600, and the chefs can create a special tasting menu for a celebration.

How does Thanksgiving work?

$65 per plate, plus 20% service charge and 8% tax. Ticketed and non-refundable, booked on OpenTable. For carryout, email our events coordinator.

Where do we park?

There is no dedicated lot. Guests park in the paid lot across the street.
