Around the table

Lunch, brunch and dinner

The menu changes with the seasons, and there are always daily specials.

1

Lunch in the Market Café

House-blend coffees, FARMtacos and a BLT, Tuesday through Friday from 10:30 AM to 3 PM.

2

Weekend brunch

Saturday and Sunday from 9 AM to 3 PM: 4th Generation Buttermilk Biscuits with apple butter, Indiana Benedict and Hoosier Poutine.

3

Dinner and small plates

Dinner from 5 PM, Tuesday through Sunday. Small plates change daily, plus a Lugar Burger made with lean local bison.

4

Wood stone oven pizza

Hand-rolled pizzas served hot from the in-house wood stone oven.

5

Garlic fries

A massive, family-sized bowl, with a chili garlic and turmeric aioli.

6

Private parties

Good honest cooking for parties from 2 to 600. The chefs can build a special tasting menu.
