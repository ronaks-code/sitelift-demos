Kitchen notes

Back on North Walnut

Yogi's became a Bloomington mainstay in 1992, a bar where students, families and alumni all felt welcome, until the original location was bought by Indiana University in 2017.

Finney Hospitality Group brought it back on North Walnut Street: the same comfort, fun and community, with red neon and string lights.

Laid-back and easy for a night out with friends. Fair prices, short waits, a solid kids menu.

  • Cream and crimsonPennants, neon and IU spirit all over the bar.
