Nuestra historia

The yellow one on Winslow

A yellow building with a teal roof, carved masks by the door and red umbrellas over the patio. The sign says Authentic Mexican.

Inside, parrot murals, painted chairs and tile floors. Weekly drink specials are posted on Facebook.
