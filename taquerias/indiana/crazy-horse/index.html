What's on the menu

Eat, drink, cheer

Three reasons to come through the door on Kirkwood.

From the kitchen

Wings with house-made sauces to start, then salads, sandwiches, burgers and entrées. Ask your server about the latest dessert.

The back bar

A full bar with local, domestic and imported beer, and a nightly special from Mule Monday to Sunday Funday.

Game day and events

Hoosier game days draw a crowd. The party room and banquet menu cover work events, showers and rehearsal dinners.

A building with a past

A brick building from around 1900, built as a hotel for the train station across the way. Etched glass above the patio doors still shows its past.

Owned by Two Rats, Inc. since April 1987. Laid-back and open to all ages before 9 pm.
