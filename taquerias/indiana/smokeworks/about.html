Our story

Indiana barbecue, done right

SmokeWorks is an Indiana hickory-smoked barbecue joint on North College Avenue, with bold flavors, slow-smoked meats and a bar stocked with bourbon, whiskey and moonshine.

Brisket, ribs, turkey, chicken and sausage are house-smoked in a signature dry rub for up to 18 hours. Southern sides are made from scratch.

  • Smoked slowUp to 18 hours over Indiana hickory.
  • Made from scratchSouthern sides from family recipes.
  • Bourbon barBourbon, whiskey and moonshine.
