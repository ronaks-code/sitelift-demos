Visiting from Schertz? Mi Corazón Street Tacos is on E Commerce St in San Antonio, near the Riverwalk. Street tacos and cheese crusted quesadillas, Wednesday to Saturday from 7 PM.

What everybody orders

Favorites

Tacos, quesadillas and a bowl of chips, with your pick of green or red salsa.

Street tacos

Soft yet crispy mini corn tortillas with onions, cilantro, limes and green or red salsa. Plenty of meat.

Cheese crusted quesadillas

A thin crisp cheese crust outside, Monterey Jack and pico de gallo inside, with salsa or roasted poblano crema.

Chips & salsa

A tray of chips with meat, peppers and fresh toppings, made for sharing on the Riverwalk.
