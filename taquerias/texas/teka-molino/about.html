Our story

The people behind Teka Molino.

Under the red sign

The red TEKA MOLINO letters over the clay-tile roof have marked the spot since 1937. Everything is made from scratch, from puffy tacos to bean and guacamole cups.

Good beans, full plates and friendly service. Netflix's Taco Chronicles featured Teka in its second season.

  • Made from scratchThe Teka family cooks everything themselves.
  • Generous platesFull plates, and the chips come free.
  • Friendly crewSmiles at the counter.
