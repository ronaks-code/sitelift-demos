Hecho aquí

A family kitchen on Medical Drive

Taqueria Datapoint was founded by a family with a deep love for cooking, with one mission: share the authentic taste of Mexico with the local community.

Real, authentic Mexican food, done right. Different from Tex-Mex, and really good.

A wooden cart counter, a hand-written chalkboard of specials and a welcome banner out front. Staff keep moving, clearing tables and checking in.

  • Early to lateOpen from 6 AM. Late on Friday and Saturday.
  • Portions to shareFull plates. Fair prices.
