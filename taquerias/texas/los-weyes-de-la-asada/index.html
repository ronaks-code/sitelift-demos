Said at the table

This is my go-to spot for authentic carne asada tacos, I highly recommend coming here! Their flavors really come alive with the homemade tortillas and salsas, grilled pineapple, and that signature mesquite aroma from the sizzling asada. My favorites are the Chingon and Mamon signature tacos 🔥 The atmosphere is great, too! A lovely picnic area at a fun food park that has a playground for kids.

If you’re going with friends or got a heavy appetite order the nachos chingones , they won’t disappoint. Don’t forget to grab that aguas Fresca too! The service was friendly and the owner was cute so that was a plus.
