What to order first

Breakfast tacos, bowls and small plates, all made for the early risers of San Antonio.

Specialty tacos

2

Chilaquiles and bowls

Crispy chips with green, red or ranchero salsa, refried beans, pickled red onion, queso fresco and crema.

Antojitos and quesadillas

Oaxaca cheese quesadillas on nixtamal corn tortillas, huarache, flautas and guacamole with tostadas.

Nuestra historia

Quick service, made with love

Authentic Mexican breakfast: tacos, quesadillas, soups, chilaquiles, molletes, coffee and agua frescas.

Every taco gets its own name and a sticker on the wrap. Friendly counter, helpful recommendations, and a patio under shade sails and string lights.
