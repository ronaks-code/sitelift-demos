Hello, we're

A sweet shop with chocolate roots

The downtown home of Bloomington Chocolate Company. Founder Linda Armes grew up making candy, pies and cookies with her grandmother, then began making truffles.

The chocolate business launched in 2010 at a local farmers market. Today: handmade chocolates, croissants, cupcakes, cookies, brownies, espresso drinks and lemonades.

Friendly staff. Easy ordering. Unusual requests are welcome.
