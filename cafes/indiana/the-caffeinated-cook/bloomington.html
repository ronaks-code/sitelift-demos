Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I get to The Caffeinated Cook from Bloomington?

We're at 206 N Sale St, Ellettsville, IN 47429, in downtown Ellettsville next to the municipal parking lot. Tap Directions for turn-by-turn from Bloomington.

Can I order ahead from Bloomington?

Yes. Call (812) 345-3623 or use our curbside order page, then park in a reserved spot out front and we'll bring it to your car.

When is The Caffeinated Cook open?

Thursday through Saturday, 7 am to 2 pm. We post each day's hours and any closures on our Facebook page, so check there or give us a call around holidays.

When can I get biscuits and gravy?

Fridays and Saturdays only, and they often sell out by about 9 am. Come early or call ahead.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes: gluten-free scones and muffins, and often gluten-free cookies and brownies. They're made with gluten-free ingredients, but in the same kitchen and ovens as everything else, so we can't guarantee they're 100% gluten-free.
