The people behind The Cat's Closet.

Hello, we're

Hi, we're Sydney and Clairessa

We started in 2021 selling vintage online and at markets, with cat rescue on the side. In fall 2023 we joined both in one storefront.

Slow fashion for all sizes, genders and budgets. The lounge gives the shelter's most vulnerable cats a homelike place to settle before adoption.

Outside the shop we're big women's basketball fans, movie and board game nerds, and we hope to visit every WNBA stadium.

  • Adopt, don't shopEvery lounge cat is fully vetted and ready for a home.
  • Slow fashionSecondhand pieces for all sizes, genders and budgets.
  • Sweet and welcomingWe love what we do, and it shows.
