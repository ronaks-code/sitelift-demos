Our story

Downtown on College Avenue

Exposed brick, pendant lights and a kraft-paper menu board, with outdoor seating out front, across from the Monroe County Courthouse.

A busy room of students and locals, warm staff and fast service. A second location, Inkwell Campus, is on South Woodlawn Avenue.
