Before you book

Good to know

When are you open?

Monday to Friday 7am to 5pm, and Saturday and Sunday 8am to 3pm.

Can I rent the cafe for an event?

Yes. Birthday parties, baby showers, small weddings and meetings. Send a request through the form.

Can I order online?

Yes, use the order online button on this site.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes. There is a patio with a fireplace.

Do you do catering?

Yes, you can start a catering order through the contact form.

Is it busy?

At peak hours it can be, and inside seating gets limited. The patio helps on nice days.
