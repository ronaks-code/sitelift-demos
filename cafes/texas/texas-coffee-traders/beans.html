Bean questions

Where can I buy fresh coffee beans in Austin?
At our cafe (3223 E 7th St) or the roastery warehouse (5815 Wilcab Rd), which carries the full catalog. You can also order online for shipping or pickup.
How often do you roast?
Every weekday, Monday through Friday, at our Wilcab Rd roastery in East Austin.
Can you grind my coffee?
Yes. We'll grind it any way you need: espresso, drip, pour-over, French press or cold brew.
How much is shipping?
For 1–2 lbs, average shipping is $7.50 USPS Retail Ground, $10.72 UPS Ground, $17.24 UPS 3 Day Select, $23.86 UPS 2nd Day Air, $33.90 UPS Next Day Air Saver and $36.68 UPS Next Day Air.
Is there a bulk discount?
Most coffees are $5 off when you buy a 5 lb bag.
What does “natural” process mean?
The coffee is sun-dried with the cherry still intact, which gives a flavorful, fruity sweetness.
Which coffee do you use for espresso and cold brew?
Espresso is Café Monteverde Especial (we also keep the lighter Gato Rubio in the second hopper). Cold brew is Organic Lonestar.
