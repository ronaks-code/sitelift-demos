It started with a terrible cup of coffee

A Montana cabin, a Costa Rica co-op and thirty years on the Eastside.

After the worst cup of coffee of his life in an all-night Montana diner, RC Beall found an old roaster and five bags of green beans in a wilderness cabin. In 1990 he traced coffee back to Monteverde, Costa Rica, paid the Santa Elena co-op far above market and gave a dollar back per pound sold. It was one of the first fair-trade relationships in the United States.

RC came home to Texas, married his childhood friend Beth, and together they opened Texas Coffee Traders in Austin in 1994. We still buy Monteverde coffee direct from the farmers, always at a premium.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where is Texas Coffee Traders now?
Since 2024 we have three East Austin homes: the cafe and retail shop at 3223 E 7th St (78702), the roastery and warehouse at 5815 Wilcab Rd (78721) and Coffee Techs equipment service at 1138 Airport Blvd. We left our longtime East 4th Street warehouse after nearly 30 years.
What are the cafe hours?
The East 7th Street cafe is open Monday–Friday 7am–4pm and Saturday 9am–4pm. It is closed on Sundays. The roastery on Wilcab Rd is open Monday–Thursday 8am–5pm and Friday 8am–4pm.
Can I buy fresh-roasted coffee beans in Austin from you?
Yes. We roast Monday through Friday, and you can buy whole-bean or ground coffee at the cafe, at the roastery warehouse (our full catalog), or online for shipping or pickup. Bags come in ½ lb, 1 lb and 5 lb sizes.
Do you sell coffee wholesale to cafes, restaurants and offices?
We were a wholesale roaster before we ever opened a cafe. We offer custom blends, private labeling, barista training, next-day delivery in Central Austin and equipment service through Coffee Techs. See wholesale.
