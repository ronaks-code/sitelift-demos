Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7:30am to 4:30pm, and Saturday 8am to noon. Closed Sunday.

Where do you work?

Kyle, plus Buda, San Marcos, Bastrop, Lockhart, Dripping Springs, Wimberley, Manchaca and parts of south Austin. Call if you are not sure we cover you.

Do you work on heat pumps and furnaces?

Yes. We repair, maintain and install furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps.

Do you install Lennox systems?

Yes. AIM is a Lennox Network Dealer and installs air conditioners, furnaces, heat pumps and mini-split systems.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. Routine maintenance keeps systems in shape. Ask about maintenance plans on your visit.

How much will it cost?

Every home is different, so we do not post prices here. Call or send a request. Ask about current offers and financing too.
