About Del's

A family owned shop on FM 967, open for service since 2007.

The guy behind the van

Started by Del in 2007

Delfino Torres started Del's in 2007. He had over 20 years at large AC companies. At first he was the only employee.

Word of mouth grew the shop. Del hired people he trusted and treated them like family. The company is still family owned and operated.

The techs are NATE-certified. Calls come from Buda, Kyle, Austin, Lakeway, Driftwood and more.
