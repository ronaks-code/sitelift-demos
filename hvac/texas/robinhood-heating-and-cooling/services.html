Good to know

Questions people ask

How do I reach you?

Call or text, or send the request form on the contact page. The emergency line is open 24/7.

Where do you work?

Austin, Kyle, Buda and surrounding areas. Not sure you are covered? Call and ask.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes, free in-home estimates for full system replacements, with honest advice and no pressure.

What if it breaks at night?

Call the 24/7 emergency line. The team calls back quickly after hours.

Do you offer a maintenance plan?

Yes. The annual membership plan covers two visits a year, spring and fall, with priority scheduling and waived diagnostic fees. One-time tune-ups are available too.

Do you clean ducts?

No. We do duct energy audits, checking duct sizing, air gaps and airflow to problem rooms.

Are there payment options or discounts?

Flexible payment options are available with approved credit. Ask about discounts for military, seniors, healthcare, education and first responders.

How will I know the cost?

We tell you the price before we start. No hidden fees.

System acting up?

Call the 24/7 emergency line, or book at your convenience. Free in-home estimates for full system replacements.
