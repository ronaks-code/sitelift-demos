Services

HVAC design, indoor air quality work, testing and equipment analysis for residential and commercial clients.

What we do

Design, testing and problem projects

Residential and commercial HVAC support, from load calculations to the problem project nobody else could solve.

  1. 1

    Design and load calculations

    Load calculations, equipment selection, duct design and ventilation planning. Sealed documents come from appropriately licensed professionals.

    • Load calculations
    • Equipment selection
    • Duct design
    • Ventilation planning
  2. 2

    Air quality and testing

    Indoor air quality assessments and investigations of temperature, humidity, airflow and noise concerns, to find the root cause instead of guessing.

    • IAQ assessments
    • Humidity control
    • Airflow and noise
    • Performance investigations
  3. 3

    Equipment and problem projects

    Equipment evaluation and controls review, plus problem project resolution: finding the gaps and coordinating next steps.

    • VRF systems
    • Heat pumps
    • Energy recovery ventilation
    • Problem project resolution

How a job runs

From first call to a clear answer

  1. 1

    Consult

    A no-charge 20-minute conversation about what the building is doing.

  2. 2

    Assess

    Existing conditions, load calculations and testing where needed.

  3. 3

    Advise

    Options and tradeoffs in plain language, with the root cause named.

  4. 4

    Support

    Coordination with the parties responsible for design, installation and operation, with deliverables set for each engagement.

Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, listed as open 24 hours. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

What does the first consultation cost?

The 20-minute consultation is no charge. Call or send the form to set it up.

Do you work on homes and commercial buildings?

Both. hvacMENSCH supports residential and commercial clients, including builders, contractors, architects and real estate professionals.

Do you only serve Austin?

The office is in Austin, and the company supports residential and commercial clients nationwide.

Can you provide stamped drawings?

Where permit submittals or stamped documents are required, they are developed with appropriately licensed professionals under project-specific arrangements.

Is hvacMENSCH licensed?

hvacMENSCH was founded by John K. Chavez, a Texas-licensed HVAC contractor with over 40 years in the industry.

How much will it cost?

Scopes and deliverables are set for each engagement, so prices are not posted. Call or send a message to talk through yours.

Stuck on an air problem?

Call or send a message for a no-charge 20-minute consultation, Monday to Friday.
