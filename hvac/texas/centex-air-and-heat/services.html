Good to know

Questions people ask

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7:30am to 7pm, Saturday 8am to 5pm and Sunday 1pm to 5pm. After hours service is available.

Do you offer same day or emergency service?

Yes. Call for same day service and emergencies, and after hours service is available.

Where do you work?

Bastrop, Cedar Creek, Elgin, Smithville, La Grange, Moulton and the rest of Bastrop County.

What do you service?

Residential air conditioning and heating, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, indoor air quality, commercial HVAC and commercial duct repair.

Can I finance a new system?

Financing options are available. Ask when you call.

How do I pay?

Cash, check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, and financing options.

Are your technicians certified?

Technicians are nationally certified, two team members are state master licence holders, and all staff are screened with background checks.
