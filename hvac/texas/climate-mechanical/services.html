Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you do emergency calls?

Yes. Emergency service is available, including weekends and holidays. Call the office and they will get someone out.

What do you work on?

Air conditioning and heating for homes and businesses, including new installs, replacements, indoor air quality, duct sealing and commercial refrigeration.

How much does a new system cost?

It depends on the home, the unit size and the ductwork, so they do not post prices. Call for a free estimate.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. Residential Maintenance Agreements include two visits per year, and commercial service agreements can be set up to fit your needs.

Do you check ductwork?

Yes. If you change the thermostat and notice no difference, ask about a duct inspection and air duct sealing.

Are you licensed?

Climate Mechanical lists Texas license TACLA28642E.

How long have you been around?

Since 1988, serving Buda and the Austin area.

Where do you work?

Buda, Kyle, Dripping Springs, Austin, South Austin, Westlake, Cedar Park, San Marcos and more. Call to check your address.

AC or heat acting up?

Call or send a request. Emergency service is available, including weekends and holidays.
