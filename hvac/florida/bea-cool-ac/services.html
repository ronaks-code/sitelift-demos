Questions people ask

Do you do repairs and installs?

Yes. Repairs, full AC installs and tune-ups of whole HVAC systems.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Bea Cool AC serves homes, condos and commercial clients, including larger commercial installs and refrigeration systems.

Where do you work?

Across Tampa Bay and the I-4 corridor, including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, New Port Richey and Lakeland.

Do you handle emergencies?

Urgent system failures get priority. Call and say it is an emergency.

Weekend visits may be by appointment, so call ahead to check.

Yes. A full consultation explains your options, and a thorough inspection comes before any replacement advice.
